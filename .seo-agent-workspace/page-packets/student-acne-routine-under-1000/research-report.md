# Research Report: Student Budget Acne Routine (Under ₹1000)

## 1. SERP Deep Dive & Content Gaps
- **Current Landscape:** Search queries like "cheap acne skincare routine india" and "best skincare under 1000" yield results that frequently miss the mark. Many existing guides recommend products that collectively exceed ₹2000, failing true budget constraints for students.
- **Content Opportunity:** There is a significant gap for a strict, no-nonsense routine where the total cart value remains under ₹1000. The content needs to strictly adhere to this limit to win trust and fulfill the actual user intent for 2026.

## 3. Clinical Context & Cost Efficiency
- **Minimalism Over Complexity:** Skipping toners and serums is not just a budget constraint—it actually heals the skin faster by reducing the risk of irritation and barrier damage. This clinically sound approach also keeps the total cost significantly lower.
- **Spot Treatment:** Instead of expensive spot patches, Benzoyl Peroxide gel (e.g., Benzac AC 2.5%, available at pharmacies for ~₹120) is highly cost-effective and clinically proven for targeted acne treatment.
