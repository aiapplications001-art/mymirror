## Section 1: Hero
If you have rough, bumpy skin on your arms, chest, or back, a regular moisturizing lotion won't be enough. Salicylic Acid (BHA) body lotions are specifically formulated to hydrate the skin while exfoliatng deep inside the pores. This dual action dissolves hard sebum plugs, smooths skin texture, and prevents body breakouts from forming.

## Section 2: Biological Pathway
Salicylic Acid is oil-soluble, meaning it can easily penetrate sebum to clean inside the follicle walls. While a facial salicylic serum can be too drying when applied in large amounts to the body, a body lotion formulation combines the acid with hydrating emollients. This ensures that the thick skin of your body receives active exfoliation without damaging your natural moisture barrier.
