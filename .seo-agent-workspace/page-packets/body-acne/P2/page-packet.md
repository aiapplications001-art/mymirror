## Section 1: Hero
Experiencing rough bumps or pimples on your buttocks can be highly uncomfortable. Unlike facial breakouts, butt bumps are typically folliculitis rather than true acne. Long sitting hours, tight clothing, and sweat friction irritate the hair follicles, leading to red, itchy, or painful bumps. Learn the clinical steps to treat buttocks folliculitis and restore smooth skin.

## Section 2: Biological Pathway
Buttock breakouts are mostly caused by folliculitis—a condition where hair follicles become infected by yeast or bacteria. Sitting for extended periods (especially on non-breathable chairs) raises skin temperature and traps sweat. This friction damages the follicle walls, allowing common bacteria like Staphylococcus to enter and cause infection. True acne is rare on the buttocks; understanding this distinction is key to choosing the correct treatment.
