## Section 1: Hero
Frequent gym sessions are great for your body, but the combination of sweat, friction, and gym equipment bacteria is a recipe for back and chest breakouts. Sweat creates a warm, moist environment where bacteria multiply rapidly. Establishing a strict post-workout shower protocol is essential to protect your skin barrier and stop gym acne in its tracks.

## Section 2: Biological Pathway
When you exercise, your sweat glands produce moisture while your sebaceous glands produce oil. If left on the skin, this mixture feeds C. acnes and Malassezia (fungal acne yeast). If you sit in sweaty clothes, the fabric continually rubs against your skin, driving this mixture deep into your pores. Showering within 15 minutes of finishing your session removes these triggers before they can cause inflammation.
