## Section 1: Hero
Have you noticed red, painful bumps spreading across your chest after a workout or on hot summer days? Chest acne (often called "chestne") is a common yet frustrating skin issue that affects both men and women in India. The chest has a high concentration of oil glands, making it highly susceptible to sweat-induced breakouts, friction, and clogged pores. Fortunately, targeted clinical body washes and simple daily habits can clear your chest skin for good.

## Section 2: Biological Pathway
Chest acne is primarily driven by three factors: excess sebum (oil) production, bacterial overgrowth (C. acnes), and sweat occlusion. In humid climates like India, sweat acts as a sealing agent, trapping dead skin cells inside the chest's hair follicles. This creates an anaerobic environment where bacteria thrive, leading to painful red bumps. Additionally, friction from tight synthetic clothing or heavy backpack straps pushes the mixture of sweat and oil deeper into the pores, aggravating the condition.
