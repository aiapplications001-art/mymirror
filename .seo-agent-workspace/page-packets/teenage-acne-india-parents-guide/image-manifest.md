# Image Manifest: Teenage Acne in India (Parent & Student Manual)

## Target Aesthetics
- **Vibe:** Authentic, hopeful, clinical but accessible, Gen Z/Alpha energy.
- **Setting:** Modern Indian context (Urban schools, student study desks, typical Indian gym/sports settings).
- **Diversity:** Include a mix of genders and skin tones typical of the Indian subcontinent. Avoid "airbrushed" perfection—show realistic skin textures with minor imperfections to build trust.
