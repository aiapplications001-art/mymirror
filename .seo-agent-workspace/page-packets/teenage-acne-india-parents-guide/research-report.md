# Research Report: Teenage Acne in India (2026)

## 3. Clinical & Product Deep Dive
### Dairy & IGF-1 Link (2024-2026 Evidence)
- **The IGF-1 Axis:** Research confirms that dairy (especially **skim milk**) spikes Insulin-like Growth Factor-1 (IGF-1), which hyper-activates sebaceous glands.
- **mTORC1 Signaling:** High-glycemic Indian student diets (Maggi, Samosas) combined with dairy activate the mTORC1 pathway, driving sebum overproduction.
- **Whey Protein Warning:** Whey (common in student fitness supplements) has a high "insulinotropic" effect, often triggering cystic acne.

### OTC Product Safety (2026 Guidelines)
- **Benzoyl Peroxide (BPO) vs. Salicylic Acid (SA):** 
  - BPO (2.5%) remains the "Strong Recommendation" for inflammatory acne.
  - SA is preferred for non-inflammatory blackheads/whiteheads.
- **Storage & Stability:** New 2026 guidelines emphasize storing BPO in cool, dry places (not steamy bathrooms) to prevent degradation into benzene. Replace BPO products every 3 months.
- **The "Sandwich" Method:** Recommended to apply moisturizer *before* and *after* actives to protect the developing teen skin barrier.
