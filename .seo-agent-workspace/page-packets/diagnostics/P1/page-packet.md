## Section 1: Hero
Have you noticed your breakouts lingering for weeks, leaving dark scars that refuse to fade? The hidden culprit might be low iron. Iron is essential for cell division, collagen synthesis, and tissue oxygenation. When iron levels are depleted (indicated by low Ferritin), your skin's healing mechanism stalls, making normal pimples turn into long-lasting blemishes.

## Section 2: Biological Pathway
Iron acts as a critical cofactor for enzymes that produce collagen and rebuild skin tissues. When your body is low on iron, it prioritizes oxygen for vital organs, leaving your skin cell turnover sluggish. Consequently, dead skin builds up, clogging pores and causing acne to heal at a fraction of its normal rate.
