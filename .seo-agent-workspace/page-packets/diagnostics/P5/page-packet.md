## Section 1: Hero
If your acne consists of severe, swollen, and red nodulocystic breakouts that refuse to resolve with standard spot creams, your body might be in a state of chronic systemic inflammation. Blood tests like hs-CRP and ESR measure general inflammatory activity, helping identify systemic triggers.

## Section 2: Biological Pathway
Systemic inflammation hypersensitizes skin hair follicles, causing them to overreact to normal bacteria and sebum. High hs-CRP indicates that minor clogs quickly turn into massive, painful cysts. Managing diet, sleep, and internal inflammatory root causes is essential.
