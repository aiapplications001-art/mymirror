## Section 1: Hero
Struggling with red, inflamed pimples that keep returning? Zinc is a critical trace mineral that regulates sebum production, suppresses acne-causing bacteria, and inhibits DHT hormones locally in the skin. When zinc levels drop, your skin loses its natural anti-inflammatory defense, resulting in oilier skin and angry, inflamed bumps.

## Section 2: Biological Pathway
Zinc regulates the activity of sebaceous glands by controlling the expression of inflammatory cytokines. Without sufficient zinc, skin cells stick together inside the pore (hyperkeratinization), trapping excess oil and bacteria. Restoring zinc levels helps control breakouts and supports skin barrier recovery.
