## Section 1: Hero
Have you recently started taking a multivitamin or B12 supplement and suddenly broken out in small, red, itchy bumps? While B12 is essential for nerve health, taking high-dose supplements can paradoxically alter the metabolic pathway of your skin's natural bacteria, triggering inflammation.

## Section 2: Biological Pathway
Excess Vitamin B12 downregulates the synthesis of B12 within Propionibacterium acnes. This forces the bacteria to overproduce inflammatory molecules called porphyrins, which irritate the hair follicles and cause sudden, uniform, inflammatory acne breakouts.
