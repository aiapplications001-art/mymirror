## Section 1: Hero
Living in a polluted Indian metro or using groundwater contaminated with heavy metals? Toxic metals like Lead, Mercury, and Arsenic accumulate in your tissues over time, generating severe oxidative stress (ROS) and breaking down your skin's protective lipid barrier, leading to chronic breakouts.

## Section 2: Biological Pathway
Heavy metals disrupt cellular mitochondria, leading to the overproduction of Reactive Oxygen Species (ROS). This oxidizes squalene in your sebum, converting it into a highly comedogenic substance that clogs pores. Testing heavy metals helps pinpoint why your skin remains reactive despite gentle care.
