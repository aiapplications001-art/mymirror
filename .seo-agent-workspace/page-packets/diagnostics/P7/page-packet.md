## Section 1: Hero
Did you know that the lipid composition of your blood directly influences the composition of your skin's sebum? Elevated triglycerides and imbalanced cholesterol profiles can make your sebum thick, sticky, and highly comedogenic, causing rapid pore blockages.

## Section 2: Biological Pathway
Healthy skin sebum relies on a precise balance of squalene, wax esters, cholesterol, and ceramides. When serum lipids are abnormal, your skin sebum lacks essential protective fats (like ceramides), leading to barrier damage, dryness, and severe cystic breakouts.
