## Section 1: Hero
Do you get sudden cystic breakouts, especially after consuming high-carb or sugary foods? The metabolic link between blood sugar and skin is mediated by insulin. When your cells resist insulin, your body produces more of it, which stimulates your liver to release IGF-1 (Insulin-like Growth Factor 1), triggering sebum surges.

## Section 2: Biological Pathway
IGF-1 stimulates the proliferation of sebocytes (sebum-producing cells) and increases local androgen activity, leading to thick, pore-clogging oil. Checking both your average blood sugar (HbA1c) and fasting insulin reveals metabolic imbalances driving your breakouts.
