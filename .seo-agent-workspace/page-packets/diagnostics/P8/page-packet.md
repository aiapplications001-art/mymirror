## Section 1: Hero
Do you breakout consistently during the week leading up to your period? While testosterone is the most famous acne driver, the balance between Estrogen and Progesterone is equally crucial. An imbalance—especially relative estrogen dominance or low progesterone—triggers cyclical breakouts.

## Section 2: Biological Pathway
Progesterone acts as a natural inhibitor of 5-alpha reductase, helping suppress sebum production. When progesterone levels drop too low in the luteal phase relative to estrogen, your skin loses this protective control, leading to sebum spikes and jawline breakouts.
