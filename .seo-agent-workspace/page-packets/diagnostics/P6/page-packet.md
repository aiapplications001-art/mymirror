## Section 1: Hero
Your liver acts as the master filter, detoxifying metabolic waste and processing excess sex hormones. When liver enzymes (SGOT/SGPT) are elevated or detoxification pathways are sluggish, hormones build up in the bloodstream, driving sebum spikes and chronic breakouts.

## Section 2: Biological Pathway
Sluggish liver function reduces the clearance of androgens and estrogens, contributing to hormone dominance. Furthermore, when the liver is overwhelmed, the skin acts as an alternative elimination pathway, driving toxic buildup and local follicular irritation.
