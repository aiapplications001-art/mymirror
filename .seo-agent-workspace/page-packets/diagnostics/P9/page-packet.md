## Section 1: Hero
Struggling with acne alongside bloating, acid reflux, or irregular digestion? The gut-skin axis is a vital pathway. An overgrowth of harmful bacteria in your gut (dysbiosis) leaks inflammatory toxins into your blood, triggering widespread systemic skin flares.

## Section 2: Biological Pathway
Dysbiosis compromises your intestinal lining (leaky gut), allowing bacterial lipopolysaccharides (LPS) to enter circulation. These toxins trigger systemic immune responses that cause your skin's follicles to flare. A Stool PCR test analyzes gut bacterial diversity to locate these triggers.
