## 1. The Biology: How Stress Causes Pimples

When your brain perceives stress, it activates the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis, triggering your adrenal glands (located on top of your kidneys) to release hormones.

### Cortisol & Sebum Overproduction
* **The Biology:** Cortisol is your primary stress hormone. 
* **The Acne Connection:** Your sebaceous (oil-producing) glands have direct receptors for cortisol. When cortisol levels spike due to chronic stress, it binds to these receptors, sending your oil production into overdrive. Furthermore, cortisol reduces the production of natural lipids and ceramides, weakening your skin barrier and leaving it highly vulnerable to bacterial invasion and chronic redness.

### DHEA-S (Adrenal Androgens) & Jawline Breakouts
* **The Biology:** DHEA-S (Dehydroepiandrosterone Sulfate) is an androgenic hormone produced almost exclusively by the adrenal glands.
* **The Acne Connection:** When stress is high, your adrenal glands produce excess DHEA-S. Local enzymes in your skin convert DHEA-S into stronger androgens like Testosterone and DHT. These hormones enlarge your pores and make your sebum thick, driving painful cystic nodules along your jawline, chin, and lower face.

## 2. The Core Stress Panel: What to Test

If you suspect stress is driving your chronic breakouts, you can order these targeted blood tests:

### Fasting Morning Cortisol (Serum)
* **What it is:** Measures the peak levels of cortisol in your blood.
* **The Acne Connection:** Cortisol follows a circadian rhythm (high in the morning, low at night). A high morning cortisol reading (exceeding 20 mcg/dL) indicates chronic HPA-axis activation and systemic stress.
* **Optimal Level:** For clear skin, morning cortisol should ideally remain **between 10 and 15 mcg/dL**.

### DHEA-S (Dehydroepiandrosterone Sulfate)
* **What it is:** Measures adrenal androgen output.
* **The Acne Connection:** High DHEA-S directly indicates stress-induced androgen dominance, causing cystic acne.
* **Optimal Level:** For women, keeping DHEA-S **under 200 mcg/dL** is key for managing jawline breakouts.
