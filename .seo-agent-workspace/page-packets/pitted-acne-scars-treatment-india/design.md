# Design Specification: Acne Scar Pillar Guide (Pitted Scars & Texture)

## 1. Objective
Draft a 1500+ word "Pillar" guide for Indian users (20-35) dealing with pitted acne scars. The guide must transition the user from emotional frustration to practical, clinical action while maintaining high EEAT and trust.

## 2. Target Audience
- **Primary:** "Post-Acne Survivors" in India.
- **Tone:** Professional, Clinical, Empathetic.

## 3. Page Structure (Narrative Flow)
1.  **H1: Acne Scars in India: 2026 Guide to Treating Pitted Scars & Texture**
2.  **Introduction:** Empathy-led hook for survivors who have cleared acne but kept the texture.
3.  **Marks vs. Scars:** The critical distinction between PIH/PIE (pigment) and Atrophic Scars (structural damage).
4.  **The Scar Diagnostic (Bento Grid - Diagnostic First):**
    - **Ice-Pick:** Checklist (depth, V-shape, "pin-prick").
    - **Boxcar:** Checklist (U-shape, sharp borders, "punched out").
    - **Rolling:** Checklist (M-shape, wavy texture, tethered to muscle).
5.  **The 6-Month Rule:** Managing expectations for collagen induction and remodeling.
6.  **Clinical Gold Standards for Indian Skin:**
    - **MNRF:** The safety hero for brown skin.
    - **Fractional CO2:** The texture resurfacer.
    - **Subcision:** The rolling scar liberator.
    - **TCA CROSS:** The ice-pick closer.
7.  **Procedure Costs (2026 Table):** City-wise pricing for Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, and Pune.
8.  **At-Home Support:** The role of Retinoids and Peptides (and their limitations).
9.  **FAQ:** Safety for brown skin, long-term scars, and downtime.
10. **MyMirror CTA:** AI Texture/Depth mapping scan.

## 4. Key Assets
- **Bento Grid:** For the Scar Diagnostic section.
- **Cost Table:** 2026 pricing estimates per session/package.
- **Comparison Table:** Topical vs. Clinical effectiveness for different scar types.
