# Research Synthesis: Pitted Acne Scar Treatment Landscape in India (2025-2026)

## 1. Executive Summary
The Indian acne scar market has shifted from "at-home DIY" to "clinical combination therapy." Patients are increasingly educated on scar types (Ice-pick, Boxcar, Rolling) and are seeking session-by-session transparency over vague "before-and-after" claims. The 2026 trend emphasizes safety for Fitzpatrick IV-VI skin types, with a preference for **MNRF** and **Subcision** over aggressive ablative lasers.

## 4. Clinical Context & Safety for Indian Skin (Fitzpatrick IV-VI)

### The PIH Challenge
Indian skin is highly prone to **Post-Inflammatory Hyperpigmentation (PIH)**. 2025 clinical guidelines mandate:
- **Pre-Conditioning:** 2-4 weeks of Tyrosinase inhibitors (Kojic Acid, Tranexamic Acid) before any laser/MNRF.
- **Conservative Energy:** Lower fluence and longer pulse widths for CO2 lasers.
- **The "Subcision Revolution":** Because subcision is non-thermal, it is the safest "gold standard" for rolling scars in dark skin, showing **40-80% improvement** with zero laser-related PIH risk.

### 2024-2026 Treatment Standards
- **Combination Therapy:** The "Standard of Care" is now **Subcision + MNRF + PRP** in a single session.
- **Early Intervention:** Treating scars while still on low-dose Isotretinoin is now clinically supported, debunking the old "wait 6 months" rule.
- **PRF vs. PRP:** Platelet-Rich Fibrin (PRF) is replacing PRP as it provides a slower release of growth factors and better tissue regeneration.
