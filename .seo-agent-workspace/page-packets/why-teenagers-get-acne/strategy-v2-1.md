# Strategy V2.1: Why Teenagers Get Acne (Indian Adolescent Master Manual)

## 1. Persona Mapping
- **Primary: The Indian Adolescent / Student (Ages 13–21)**
  - **Context:** Navigating puberty, CBSE/ICSE board exams, coaching commutes, college hostels, high monsoon humidity, and tight pocket money.
  - **Pain Points:** Sudden facial breakouts, post-acne dark marks (PIH), peer anxiety, confusion over expensive influencer marketing vs affordable pharmacy solutions.
  - **Tone:** Direct, empowering, scientifically grounded, respectful of budget realities.
- **Secondary: The Concerned Indian Parent**
  - **Context:** Anxious about permanent pitting/scarring and emotional withdrawal, prone to suggesting traditional home remedies (besan, haldi, multani mitti) before understanding hormonal etiology.
  - **Needs:** Clear clinical explanation that acne is endocrine (not bad hygiene), plus distinct red flags indicating when to see a dermatologist.
  - **Tone:** Authoritative, reassuring, empathetic.
