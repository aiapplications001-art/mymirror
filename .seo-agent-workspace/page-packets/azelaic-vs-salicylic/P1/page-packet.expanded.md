## Quick Verdict: Which Active Ingredient Should You Pick?

Cheek acne in India is primarily caused by physical friction from dirty phone screens, helmet straps, and heavy hair oil (Champi) residue, combined with monsoon humidity. Quick Verdict Browse Target Routines and Solutions.

- **Choose Azelaic Acid (10%-20%):** If you suffer from post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH) dark marks, post-acne red spots (PIE), rosacea, sensitive redness, or inflammatory papules. It is an FDA-approved tyrosinase inhibitor safe for daily use.
- **Choose Salicylic Acid (1%-2%):** If you have deep blackheads, whiteheads, open pores, or high sebum production in humid Indian weather. As an oil-soluble BHA, it penetrates deep into pores to dissolve keratin plugs.

## Faq

### Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions FAQ.

**Which is faster for pimple mark removal?**
Azelaic Acid is significantly faster for fading dark acne spots (PIH) because it directly blocks tyrosinase enzyme activity in melanocytes.

**Does Azelaic Acid cause purging?**
Minor purging can occur during the first 2-3 weeks as cell turnover increases, but it is typically milder than retinoid purging.
