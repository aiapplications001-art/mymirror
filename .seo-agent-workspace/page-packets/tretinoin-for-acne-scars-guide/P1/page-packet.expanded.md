# Tretinoin for Acne Scars: Clinical Expectations & 12-Week Guide

Tretinoin is a powerful prescription retinoid that helps smooth acne scars. Unlike standard skincare actives, it requires careful sandwich method application to prevent chemical irritation. In this guide, we outline the clinical expectations and a 12-week ease-in calendar.

<!-- SECTION_ID: S2_quick_answer -->
## Quick Answer

## Quick Summary: Tretinoin for Acne Scars

Tretinoin accelerates cellular turnover to smooth out texture and fade dark marks. However, users must build tolerance slowly to avoid a severe purging phase. Always apply a pea-sized amount and use sun protection daily.

<!-- SECTION_ID: S3_problem_diagnosis -->
## Problem Diagnosis

## Understanding Tretinoin and Acne Scar Pathophysiology

Tretinoin binds to retinoic acid receptors to stimulate collagen synthesis. While it works wonders for post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH) and superficial rolling scars, deep boxcar or icepick scars may require clinical procedures.

<!-- SECTION_ID: S4_selection_criteria -->
## Selection Criteria

## The 12-Week Tretinoin Ease-In Protocol

To manage the purging phase, follow our structured schedule:

1. **Weeks 1-2:** Apply a pea-sized amount twice a week using the sandwich method (moisturizer, tretinoin, moisturizer).
2. **Weeks 3-6:** Increase to once every 3 nights. Expect the initial purging phase as microcomedones rise.
3. **Weeks 7-12:** Transition to alternate nights. Watch for texture smoothing and fading marks.
