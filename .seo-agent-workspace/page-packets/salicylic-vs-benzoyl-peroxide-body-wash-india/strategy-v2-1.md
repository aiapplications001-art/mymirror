# SEO Strategy V2.1: Salicylic Acid vs. Benzoyl Peroxide Body Wash (India)

## 1. Persona: The Active Decision-Maker
*   **Profile:** Individuals aged 18-40 dealing with recurring body acne (bacne, chest acne, shoulder acne).
*   **Pain Point:** Confused by the influx of "active" body washes on platforms like Nykaa, Amazon, and Tira. They want to know which ingredient actually works for their specific type of acne without wasting money or damaging their skin barrier.
*   **Voice:** Category-manager-with-editorial-empathy (Professional, Clinical, yet approachable).

*   `salicylic acid vs benzoyl peroxide body wash` (Targeted Comparison)
*   `best medicated body wash for bacne india` (High Intent)
*   `benzoyl peroxide body wash side effects` (Informational/Safety)
*   `2 percent salicylic acid body wash vs bpo` (Specific concentration comparison)
