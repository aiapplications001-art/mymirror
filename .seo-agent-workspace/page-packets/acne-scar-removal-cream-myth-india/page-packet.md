            <p>The skincare marketplace in India is saturated with over-the-counter creams claiming to "erase acne scars permanently." From a biological standpoint, these claims are highly misleading. Acne marks (Post-Inflammatory Hyperpigmentation/Erythema) are pigment deposits left in the skin after inflammation resolves; because they are flat, they can be successfully faded using topical tyrosinase inhibitors and chemical exfoliants. However, pitted craters represent a structural loss of collagen in the dermis. Since topical creams cannot rebuild deep collagen tissue from the surface, they are clinically incapable of "filling" pitted scars.</p>
        </section>

        <section id="when-to-use">
            <h2>2. What Topical Ingredients Can Actually Do</h2>
            <p>While creams cannot cure pitted scars, specific topical ingredients serve important clinical roles in a post-acne routine:</p>
            <ul>
                <li><strong>Melanin Inhibitors (Kojic Acid, Alpha Arbutin, Azelaic Acid):</strong> Essential for fading dark brown or black post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH) spots on Indian skin.</li>
                <li><strong>Exfoliating Acids (Glycolic Acid, Salicylic Acid):</strong> Sweep away surface dead cells, smoothing out micro-roughness and improving skin light-reflectivity (giving a glass-like appearance).</li>
                <li><strong>Silicone Gel:</strong> Highly effective for raised hypertrophic scars and keloids by trapping hydration and regulating collagen synthesis.</li>
            </ul>
        </section>
