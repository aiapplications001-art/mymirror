## Core Narrative & Unique Value Angles
1. **The Fundamental Mistake (Vascular vs Melanin)**:
   - Red/pink marks are NOT pigment. They are damaged, dilated microscopic blood vessels (capillaries) engorged with hemoglobin right beneath the skin's surface.
   - Treating a vascular wound with exfoliating acids (Glycolic, AHA peels) is like pouring acid onto a bruised capillary. It sparks secondary melanogenesis, locking in stubborn dark brown PIH.
2. **The "Glass Tumbler Test" (Diascopy)**:
   - A 5-second home test using a clean clear glass tumbler. Pressing the glass against the spot squeezes blood out of capillaries (blanches white = PIE). If it stays brown, it is melanin (PIH).
3. **The 4 Non-Irritating Vascular Repair Actives**:
   - *Centella Asiatica (Cica / Madecassoside)*: Rebuilds micro-capillary walls and calms endothelial inflammation.
   - *Tranexamic Acid (3%–5%)*: Calms micro-vessel hyper-dilation and prevents UV-induced redness.
   - *Azelaic Acid (10%–15%)*: Dual anti-inflammatory that shrinks swollen vessels and halts pigment transition.
   - *Niacinamide (2%–5%)*: Reinforces the lipid barrier and reduces microvascular permeability.
4. **The Sun Protection Non-Negotiable**:
   - Why 100% Zinc Oxide mineral sunscreen is mandatory: chemical sunscreen filters create thermal heat in the skin that dilates capillaries, whereas mineral Zinc Oxide physically reflects heat and UV radiation.
5. **In-Clinic Laser Interventions**:
   - Pulsed Dye Laser (PDL) and Long-Pulsed Nd:YAG 1064nm for chronic, stubborn microvascular pools on Indian skin types.
