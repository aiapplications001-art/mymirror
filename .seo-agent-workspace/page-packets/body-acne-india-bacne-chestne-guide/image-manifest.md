# Image Manifest: Body Acne in India Guide

This manifest outlines the visual assets required for the "Body Acne in India: The Ultimate Guide to Clearing Bacne & Chestne (2026)" guide. All images should maintain a high-end, clinical-yet-aesthetic vibe consistent with the MyMirror brand.

---

## 1. Hero Image: "The Backless Confidence"

**Placement:** Top of the page, below the main H1.

## 2. Infographic: The "Anti-Residue" Hair Wash Sequence
**Purpose:** Teach the "Reverse Wash" protocol to prevent acne caused by hair products.
**Placement:** Under the "The 'Champi' Drip Effect" or "Hair Conditioner Residue" sections.

### Visual Concept
A 3-step horizontal or vertical diagram:
1.  **Step 1: Treat & Wash Hair.** (Icon of shampoo/oil on hair).
2.  **Step 2: Rinse Conditioner.** (Arrow showing water rinsing hair *away* from the back).
3.  **Step 3: Medicated Body Wash.** (Icon of a cleanser being applied to the back *last*).

### Prompt Strategy (Midjourney for Base Assets)
> **Prompt:** 3D isometric minimalist icons for a skincare routine: 1. A bottle of hair oil and shampoo. 2. A shower head rinsing long hair. 3. A medical-grade body wash bottle with a lathering sponge. Clean white background, soft blue and teal accents, professional medical illustration style. --v 6.0

## 3. Comparison Infographic: Body vs. Face Pores
**Purpose:** Explain the clinical difference in skin thickness (9 layers vs 13 layers) and pore size.
**Placement:** Under the "Anatomy of Body Acne" section.

### Visual Concept
Side-by-side macro cross-section illustration:
- **Left (Face):** Thin epidermis, small sebaceous gland, fine hair follicle. Label: "Face: 9 Layers (Delicate)".
- **Right (Back):** Thick stratum corneum (visually denser), large sebaceous gland, deep hair follicle. Label: "Back: 13 Layers (Stubborn)".

### Prompt Strategy (Midjourney for Base Assets)
> **Prompt:** Medical illustration cross-section of human skin, side-by-side comparison. Left side shows thin delicate skin layers, right side shows thick dense skin layers with a large oil gland. Scientific, clean, labeled aesthetic, soft studio lighting, white background. --ar 3:2 --v 6.0

## 4. Behavioral Guide: "The Fabric Choice"
**Purpose:** Visualizing "Acne Mechanica" causes and solutions.
**Placement:** Under the "Sari Blouses & Synthetic Fabrics" section.

### Visual Concept
Side-by-side comparison:
- **"The Trap" (Left):** Close-up of synthetic, shiny polyester or georgette fabric with heat waves trapped underneath. Label: "Synthetic/Polyester (Traps Heat & Bacteria)".
- **"The Breathable" (Right):** Close-up of porous, soft cotton weave with air-flow arrows. Label: "100% Breathable Cotton (Wicks Moisture)".

### Prompt Strategy (Midjourney)
> **Prompt:** Split screen macro photography. Left side: shiny synthetic polyester fabric, sweat droplets visible, red "trapped heat" glow. Right side: high-quality breathable white cotton fabric weave, airy blue arrows indicating airflow. Professional product photography, clean clinical vibe. --ar 16:9 --v 6.0
