## Core Narrative & Unique Value Angles
1. **The Fundamental Misdiagnosis ("Weeds in a Cleared Garden")**:
   - Fungal acne is not acne at all. It is *Malassezia folliculitis*—an opportunistic overgrowth of lipophilic yeast (*Malassezia globosa*, *Malassezia restricta*) naturally resident in human hair follicles.
   - When individuals take oral antibiotics (like doxycycline) or use aggressive antibacterial washes, they eradicate protective surface bacteria. With bacterial competition wiped out, yeast flourishes unchecked—exactly like weeds overtaking an over-cleared garden bed.
2. **The "Lipid Feast" (C11–C24 Carbon Chain Feeding)**:
   - Malassezia yeast lacks the enzymes to synthesize its own fatty acids. It relies exclusively on external lipids with carbon chain lengths between C11 and C24.
   - Common skincare oils, fatty acids (oleic, palmitic, stearic), polysorbates, and esters commonly found in Indian hair oils (coconut oil, almond oil) and rich creams act as high-octane fuel for yeast proliferation.
3. **The 3-Point Diagnostic Triad (Uniformity, Location, Itch)**:
   - *Uniformity*: Monomorphic, tiny 1–2 mm dome-shaped bumps with zero blackheads or varied cyst sizes.
   - *Location*: High sebaceous and sweat-prone zones—forehead hairline, temples, chest, upper back.
   - *Sensation*: Characteristic pricking itch that intensifies with heat, gym sweat, and tropical humidity.
4. **The Antifungal Clearance Protocol**:
   - *Phase 1: Short-Contact Antifungal Wash*: 2% Ketoconazole solution applied as a 5-minute mask 2–3 times weekly.
   - *Phase 2: Total Lipid Starvation*: Replacing heavy creams and oils with certified fungal-acne-safe gel formulations (pure hyaluronic acid, aloe vera, squalane).
   - *Phase 3: Environmental Barrier Defense*: Sweat-wicking hygiene, post-workout cleansing, and lightweight 100% mineral sun protection.
