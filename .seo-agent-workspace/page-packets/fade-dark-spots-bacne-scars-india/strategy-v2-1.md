# SEO Strategy V2.1: How to Fade Dark Spots (PIH) and Bacne Scars (India)

*   **Profile:** Individuals aged 20-35 preparing for major life events (weddings, beach vacations, festivals) where they want to wear open-back or sleeveless outfits (e.g., sarees, lehengas, backless dresses).
*   **Pain Point:** Frustrated by stubborn, dark brown or grayish spots left behind after body acne has healed. They feel "stuck" because while the acne is gone, the marks won't budge. They are looking for reliable, fast-acting (yet safe) Indian pharmacy and D2C solutions.
*   **Voice:** Category-manager-with-editorial-empathy (Professional, Clinical, yet deeply understanding of the social anxiety caused by body hyperpigmentation).
