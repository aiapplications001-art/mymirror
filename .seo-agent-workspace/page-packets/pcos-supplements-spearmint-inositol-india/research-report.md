## 1. Clinical Evidence Deep Dive (2024-2026)

### **Spearmint Tea (Mentha Spicata)**
*   **Anti-Androgenic Breakthrough (2024):** A major RCT (Najafi et al., 2024) confirmed that 2 cups/day for 12 weeks reduced free testosterone by **15%** and significantly lowered DHEA and androstenedione. 
*   **Mechanism:** Inhibits **5-alpha reductase**, stopping the conversion of testosterone to DHT (the primary driver of sebum/cystic acne).
*   **Acne Link:** Reduction in oiliness is typically observed within **30 days**, while inflammatory acne clearance takes **12 weeks** (matching the skin cycle).

### **Myo-Inositol (40:1 Ratio)**
*   **Insulin-Skin Axis (2024):** Systematic reviews in 2024 solidified Inositol as a primary non-pharmacological intervention for PCOS-related acne.
*   **The 40:1 Standard:** 40:1 Myo-to-D-Chiro ratio remains the physiological gold standard. It restores the follicular environment and reduces the "Ovarian Paradox" where ovaries have too much DCI and too little MI.
*   **Efficacy:** Found comparable to Metformin for insulin sensitivity but with a significantly **better safety profile** (fewer GI issues). Acne reduction becomes statistically significant at **3–6 months**.

## 3. Social & Video Listening (Anxieties & Qs)

### **Common User Anxieties (India)**
*   **"Does spearmint tea cause stomach pain?"** (Menthol can relax the esophageal sphincter, causing acid reflux in some; need to advise drinking *after* meals if sensitive).
*   **"Can I take Inositol if I'm not trying to conceive (TTC)?"** (Major anxiety for young Indian women; we must clarify it’s for *metabolic/skin* health, not just fertility).
*   **"Best time to drink?"** (Consistency over timing, but evening is best for spearmint due to sedative effects; Inositol is best *before* largest meals).

### **User Feedback (Reddit/YouTube)**
*   **Maintenance Rebound:** Users report oiliness returning within 3-5 days of stopping spearmint.
*   **The "Purge":** Some Inositol users report a temporary breakout in Month 1 before clearing.
