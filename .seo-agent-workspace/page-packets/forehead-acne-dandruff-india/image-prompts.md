# Image Prompts: The Dandruff-Acne Connection

**Project:** MyMirror.fit Forehead Acne Expansion
**Target Platform:** SEO Page / Social Preview

---

### Prompt 1: Hero Banner (Split Screen)
- **Concept:** Visualizing the migration of dandruff yeast to the forehead.
- **Visuals:** High-contrast split screen. 
    - **Left Side:** A stylized, clinical close-up of a human scalp showing small, white dandruff flakes. 
    - **Right Side:** A clear, healthy forehead of an Indian individual being protected by a glowing 'shield' or 'barrier' as flakes fall towards it.
- **Vibe:** Clinical, informative, dermatology-first.
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (Desktop Hero)
- **URL Placeholder:** `https://mymirror.fit/assets/images/dandruff-acne-hero.jpg`

### Prompt 2: Infographic (The Double Scalp Wash)
- **Concept:** A 3-step procedural guide for using medicated shampoo.
- **Step 1:** Icon of regular shampoo bottle. Text: "Cleanse (Remove Dirt/Oil)".
- **Step 2:** Icon of a pharma-bottle (like Nizoral). Text: "Treat (Massaging Medicated Shampoo)".
- **Step 3:** Icon of a clock at 5 minutes. Text: "Wait (Let it Work!)".
- **Vibe:** Minimalist, clean, easy to read on mobile.
- **Aspect Ratio:** 1:1 (Social/Infographic)

### Prompt 3: Product Flat-lay (Blinkit-Friendly Pharma)
- **Concept:** Showing the recommended routine with recognizable Indian pharmacy packaging.
- **Visuals:** A clean, aesthetic flat-lay on a white marble or wood surface. Includes:
    - A bottle of Nizoral or Scalpe Plus.
    - A tube of Sebamed Clear Face Gel.
    - A bottle of gentle face wash (like SaliAc).
    - A single, clean white towel.
- **Vibe:** Relatable, trustworthy, "I can buy this today".
- **Aspect Ratio:** 4:5 (Portrait)
