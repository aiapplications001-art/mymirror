# Image Manifest: Microneedling vs. Chemical Peels for Acne Scars

This manifest outlines the visual assets required for the "Microneedling vs. Chemical Peels: Which is Best for Indian Skin?" guide. All images must adhere to the MyMirror brand aesthetic: clinical, high-tech, and empathetic to Indian skin concerns.

---

## 1. Hero Image: "The Precision Choice: Needle vs. Peel"
**Purpose:** A high-impact visual that immediately communicates the two distinct mechanisms of action (Internal vs. External).
**Placement:** Top of the page, immediately following the H1.

### Prompt Strategy (Midjourney)
> **Prompt:** A high-end clinical split-screen photography shot. Left side: cool blue and silver tones, a macro close-up of a medical microneedling device with fine gold-plated needles entering the dermis, glowing collagen fibers below. Right side: warm amber and golden tones, a macro close-up of a clear chemical peel solution droplet spreading over smooth skin, light reflecting off a glass pipette. Editorial medical aesthetic, 8k resolution, professional studio lighting, ultra-modern dermatology vibe. --ar 16:9 --v 6.0

**Alt Text:** A split-screen visual comparing a microneedling device (left) with a chemical peel applicator (right).

## 2. Infographic: Depth of Action (Epidermis vs. Dermis)
**Purpose:** Educate users on why Microneedling is better for deep atrophic scars while Peels excel at surface texture.
**Placement:** Under the "The Scar Revision Battle: Surface vs. Deep Dermis" section.

### Visual Concept
A cross-section illustration of the skin layers:
- **Chemical Peels:** Illustrated as a top-down effect, with arrows penetrating only the Epidermis (Top layer).
- **Microneedling:** Illustrated as a vertical effect, with needles or energy (MNRF) reaching deep into the Dermis (Middle layer) to trigger collagen.

### Prompt Strategy (Midjourney for Base Assets)
> **Prompt:** A professional medical illustration cross-section of human skin. Clearly defined layers: Epidermis and Dermis. Stylized icons: 'Chemical Peels' with arrows indicating surface penetration; 'Microneedling' with vertical lines indicating deep dermal stimulation. Clean white background, teal and coral accents, minimalist medical design, high-quality vector style. --ar 3:2 --v 6.0

## 3. Comparison Chart: 7-Day Recovery Timeline
**Purpose:** Manage expectations regarding downtime, specifically the "social downtime" vs. "active healing" phase.
**Placement:** Under the "Side-by-Side Comparison: Safety, Downtime, and Results" section.

### Visual Concept
A horizontal 7-day calendar view comparing the two treatments:
- **Microneedling:** Days 1-2 (Redness/Sunburn look), Days 3-7 (Subtle healing, glowing skin).
- **Chemical Peels:** Days 1-2 (Tightness), Days 3-5 (The "Peel" phase - visible flaking), Days 6-7 (Fresh new skin).

### Prompt Strategy (Midjourney for Base Assets)
> **Prompt:** A minimalist 7-day recovery timeline infographic for dermatology treatments. 1-7 numbered circles. Top row: Microneedling icons showing initial redness fading by day 2. Bottom row: Chemical Peel icons showing skin flaking and peeling between days 3 and 5. Clean professional layout, soft blue and gray color palette, white background. --v 6.0
