## 3. Clinical Context: 2024-2026 Data & Safety

### MNRF vs. Traditional Microneedling (The 2026 Verdict)
- **Superiority for Boxcar Scars:** 2025 studies show MNRF (Radiofrequency) leads to a **35-40% greater improvement** in boxcar scar depth compared to traditional microneedling due to thermal "tightening" of the scar floor.
- **Fitzpatrick Safety:** MNRF is now confirmed as the "Gold Standard" for Indian Skin (IV-VI) because the energy is delivered *below* the pigment-producing layer, unlike lasers which hit the surface.

### Chemical Peel Risks (Glycolic vs. TCA)
- **The "Melanin Alert":** 
  - **Glycolic (20-70%):** Generally safe but requires 2-4 weeks of "priming" with Kojic acid to prevent PIH.
  - **TCA (Trichloroacetic Acid):** High risk for Fitzpatrick V-VI. Concentrations above 15% for full-face peels are being phased out in Indian clinics in favor of **TCA CROSS** (focal application only to the pit of the scar).
- **Post-Peel "Crackening":** A term used in 2026 clinical circles to describe the dark, brittle stage of Indian skin post-TCA. MyMirror should educate users on "Cica-balms" and "Snail Mucin" to manage this.
