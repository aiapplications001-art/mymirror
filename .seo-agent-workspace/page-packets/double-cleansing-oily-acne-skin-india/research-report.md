# Research Report: Double Cleansing for Oily Acne-Prone Skin (India 2026)

## 1. SERP Gaps & Competitor Analysis
* **Competitor Content:** Standard blogs (e.g. Nykaa, Purplle, generic e-commerce sites) recommend heavy cleansing oils containing comedogenic bases (like isopropyl myristate or coconut oil) that trigger acne flares on Indian skin. They also fail to emphasize the critical step of *emulsification* (massaging with water to wash away the oil).
* **Gaps Identified:**
  1. No step-by-step emulsification guide.
  2. Failure to explain the difference between micellar water (surfactant micelle-based) and cleansing oils (lipid-based).
  3. No specific guidelines for humid commuter weather in India.

## 2. Social & Video Listening (Reddit India / YouTube)
* **Reddit Concerns:** 
  * "I tried double cleansing with cleansing oil and got cystic breakouts." (Usually due to poor emulsification or comedogenic oil bases).
  * "Is oil cleansing necessary if I only wear silicone gel sunscreens?" (Yes, because silicones are lipophilic and resist single water-based cleansers).
* **Key Product Mentions:** Bioderma Sensibio H2O (micellar water favorite), DHC Deep Cleansing Oil, Skin1004 Centella Oil, Garnier Pink Micellar.

## 3. Brand & Internal Proof
* **MyMirror Scan Context:** Users who commute daily in cities like Bangalore or Delhi show significantly higher rates of sub-surface sebum plugs on their forehead and cheeks. Single cleansing fails to remove the bound dust-sunscreen residue, which shows up as yellow/white fluorescence under UV-spectrum analysis.
