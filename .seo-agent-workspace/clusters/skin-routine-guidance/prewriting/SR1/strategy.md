# SR1 — What skincare routine should I follow for my skin type?

## Intent and promise

**Primary query:** “What skincare routine should I follow for my skin type?”

The reader wants a routine that is simple enough to start today but safe enough not to over-promise. MyMirror will help them distinguish stable **skin type** (dry, oily, combination, sensitive-feeling) from a changing **skin state** (dehydrated, irritated, breaking out, humid-weather shine), then build around cleanser, moisturiser, sunscreen and one carefully introduced treatment.

## Page position

This page differs from the existing concern-clarity guide: it is a practical routine builder, not an investigation of a new symptom. It must avoid diagnosis and rigid product prescriptions. The value is sequence, selection criteria and a calm pace.

## Evidence ledger

1. AAD, [Skin care on a budget](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-basics/care/skin-care-budget): skin-type definitions; gentle cleansing; moisturiser; sun protection; non-comedogenic labels.
2. AAD, [Product order](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-basics/care/apply-skin-care-certain-order): cleanse, treatment, moisturiser/sunscreen, makeup; too many products can irritate.
3. AAD, [Dry skin guidance](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-basics/dry/dermatologists-tips-relieve-dry-skin): gentle fragrance-free care and creams/ointments for dry-feeling skin.
4. AAD, [Maximise results](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-secrets/anti-aging/maximize-anti-aging-products): start one product; patch test; stop a product that stings, burns or tingles.
5. AAD, [Choosing sunscreen](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/sun-protection/shade-clothing-sunscreen/choosing-right-sunscreen): broad-spectrum SPF 30+, with non-comedogenic choices for acne-prone skin.
