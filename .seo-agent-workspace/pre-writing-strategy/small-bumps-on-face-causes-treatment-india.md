## 2. Target Persona & Clinical Context
- **Persona**: Indian women and men who struggle with persistent, textured, flesh-colored or white small bumps across their forehead, cheeks, and chin that give the skin a rough, sandpaper feel. They are confused because conventional acne spot treatments fail or make it itchier.
- **Clinical Nuance**:
  - Bumps are often misidentified. There are 3 completely distinct pathologies:
    1. **Closed Comedones**: Retention hyperkeratosis + sebum plug trapped beneath an intact stratum corneum. Requires lipid-soluble BHA (Salicylic acid) and retinoids.
    2. **Fungal Acne (Malassezia Folliculitis)**: Yeast overgrowth in hair follicles thriving on Indian humidity and heavy hair oils (champi). Requires 2% Ketoconazole wash and strict removal of fatty acid/ester heavy moisturizers.
    3. **Milia**: Tiny, rock-hard keratin cysts under the eyes and upper cheeks. Cannot be squeezed; requires professional unroofing or gentle retinoids.
  - Using apricot walnut physical scrubs or pore strips causes micro-tears and spreads follicular inflammation.
