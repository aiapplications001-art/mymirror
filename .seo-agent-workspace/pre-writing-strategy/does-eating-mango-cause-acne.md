## 1. Intent Pattern & Audience Profile
- **Intent Pattern:** `informational` + `category_solution`
- **Structure Variant:** `educational_guide` (Approach A: Direct-Answer & Protocol-First Flow)
- **Target Audience:** Indian young adults, teens, and working professionals (ages 16–42) who love mangoes in summer but experience sudden inflammatory breakouts, pustules, or perioral lip bumps.
- **Pain Points:** 
  - Conflicting advice: Traditional family elders saying *"mango has excessive heat (garmi)"* vs modern internet saying *"fruit is healthy"*.
  - Frustration over breakouts right during the Indian mango season (April - July).
  - Confusion about whether mango shakes, smoothies, or raw unpeeled mangoes are the culprit.
  - Need a concrete rule on how to eat mangoes without breaking out.

## 3. Editorial Tone & Anti-AI Slop Guardrails
- **Selected Tone:** Conversational, empathetic, myth-busting, dermatologist-grounded.
- **Sloptotal Guardrails:**
  - Ban generic introductory filler: *"In today's fast-paced world"*, *"It is important to remember"*, *"Delving into the realm of"*, *"A tapestry of factors"*, *"In conclusion"*.
  - Every medical claim must cite a concrete physiological mechanism (e.g., Insulin → FoxO1 dephosphorylation → mTORC1 activation → hypersecretion of sebum).
  - Use plain, friendly everyday English that an average Indian reader immediately grasps.
