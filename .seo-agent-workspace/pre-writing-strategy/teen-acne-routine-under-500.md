# Pre-Writing Strategy: Teen Acne Routine Under 500 INR (Indian Pharmacy Focus)

**Topic:** A complete, dermatologist-approved morning and night skincare routine for teenagers and students under a total cost of ₹500, using high-efficiency Indian pharmacy products.
**Primary Keyword:** `teen acne routine under 500 inr`
**Target Audience:** Indian school and college students looking for cheap, effective acne-clearing routines without spending money on expensive marketing-heavy skincare brands.

## 1. Editorial Empathy & Voice
- **Voice:** Encouraging, peer-like, yet scientifically grounded. Focused on cost-to-benefit transparency.
- **Hook:** "You are a student dealing with hormonal breakouts, and every influencer on social media is telling you to buy a ₹1,200 serum, a ₹800 moisturizer, and a ₹900 sunscreen. Your pocket money simply doesn't cover that. Skincare doesn't need to be expensive to clear your skin. In fact, some of the most effective, dermatologist-recommended acne products in India are sitting on the shelves of your local pharmacy for under ₹150. Let's build a complete, clinical-grade morning and night routine that clears active pimples, fades dark marks, and protects your barrier—all for less than ₹500 total."
- **Empathy Point:** Relate directly to the feeling of budget exclusion in the beauty space and the stress of dealing with teenage hormonal breakouts.

## 2. Key Pillars & Content Points
- **The Pocket-Money Skincare Philosophy:**
    - Explain that clinical actives (Salicylic Acid, Benzoyl Peroxide, Clindamycin) are highly regulated commodities. Indian pharmacy brands sell them at manufacturing cost, unlike cosmetic brands which markup heavily.
- **The Morning (AM) Budget Routine:**
    - **AM Step 1: Cleanse:** Sebamed Clear Face Cleansing Bar (approx. ₹190, lasts 3 months) OR Joy pH 5.5 Cleanser (approx. ₹140).
    - **AM Step 2: Treat & Moisturize:** Excela Gel (sample/pharmacy price) or simple Aloe Gel + spot treating with Benzoyl Peroxide 2.5% (approx. ₹110).
    - **AM Step 3: Protect:** La Shield Lite SPF 50 or Episoft AC (budget size/combo wash).
- **The Night (PM) Budget Routine:**
    - **PM Step 1: Cleanse:** Same AM gentle wash.
    - **PM Step 2: Clear:** Adapalene Gel 0.1% (e.g., Adaferin/Deriva - approx. ₹150) for blackheads/microcomedones, or Clindamycin Phosphate Gel (approx. ₹100) for active pus pimples.
    - **PM Step 3: Rebuild:** Lightweight budget lotion (e.g., Biosilk or Joy Ceramide).
- **The Smart Shopping Matrix (Sub-500 Combinations):**
    - Provide a breakdown of combinations totaling under ₹500.
