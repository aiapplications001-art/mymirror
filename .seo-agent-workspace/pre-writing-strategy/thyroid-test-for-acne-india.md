## 2. Key Pillars & Content Points
- **The Thyroid-Skin Connection:**
    - **Hypothyroidism (Underactive):** Slows down cellular metabolism. The skin doesn't shed dead cells quickly enough, leading to follicular hyperkeratosis (clogged pores) and dry, scaly skin that triggers reactive breakouts.
- **The Core Thyroid Panel (What to test):**
    - **TSH (Thyroid Stimulating Hormone):** Standard screening marker.
    - **Free T3 & Free T4:** Active thyroid hormones. Standard panels often only test total hormones, but Free T3/T4 measures what is actually available for your tissues to use.
- **Optimal Skin Ranges vs. Lab Normals:**
    - General labs list TSH up to 4.5 or 5.0 uIU/mL as normal. However, for active skin barrier regeneration, many endocrinologists and functional dermatologists recommend keeping TSH **between 1.0 and 2.5 uIU/mL**.
- **Cost & Lab Options in India:**
    - A standard Thyroid Profile (TSH, Total T3, Total T4) costs **₹300 - ₹500** at labs like Tata 1mg, Lal PathLabs, and Apollo Diagnostics. An advanced panel including Free T3 and Free T4 costs **₹800 - ₹1,200**.
