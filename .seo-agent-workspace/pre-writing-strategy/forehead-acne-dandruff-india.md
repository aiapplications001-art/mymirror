# Pre-Writing Strategy: The Dandruff-Acne Connection (India)

**Topic:** How dandruff (Malassezia) triggers forehead acne and how to treat it using Indian pharmacy brands.
**Primary Keyword:** `forehead acne due to dandruff india`
**Target Audience:** Indian youth (15-25) struggling with persistent, itchy forehead bumps.

## 1. Editorial Empathy & Voice
- **Voice:** Category Manager with Editorial Empathy.
- **Hook:** "You've spent thousands on Salicylic acid and Pimple patches, but those small forehead bumps won't budge. That's because it's not 'normal' acne—it's your scalp talking to your skin."
- **Empathy Point:** Validate the frustration of misdiagnosis. It’s not a "dirty face" problem; it's a fungal hygiene problem.

## 2. Key Pillars & Content Points
- **The Science:** Explain Malassezia yeast and how it migrates from scalp to forehead.
- **The "Indian Context" Triggers:** 
    - Heavy Oiling (Champi) feeding the fungus.
    - Humidity/Sweat during Monsoon/Summer.
    - Helmet hygiene for scooty/bike commuters.
- **The Solution (The "Indian Pharma" Routine):**
    - **Step 1: Scalp Treatment:** Nizoral (2% Ketoconazole) or Scalpe Plus.
    - **Step 2: The "Shampoo Mask" Hack:** Leaving medicated shampoo on the forehead for 5 mins.
    - **Step 3: Oil-Free Hydration:** Why traditional creams fail (esters feed fungus) and why Sebamed Care Gel or Excela is the answer.
- **The "No-Oiling" Rule:** Explaining why Coconut/Amla oil is a "no-go" for this specific condition.

## 3. SEO & Connectivity
- **Secondary Keywords:** `fungal acne india`, `Scalpe Plus for acne`, `shampoo as face wash for pimples`, `Nizoral india face`.
- **Internal Links:** 
    - Link to: `index.html` (The Ultimate Indian Diet Guide)
    - Link to: `checklist.html` (Blinkit-friendly grocery list)
- **External/EEAT:** Mentioning Indian dermatologists' common prescriptions (Ketoconazole, ZPTO).

## 4. Visual Strategy
- **Hero Image:** A high-contrast split between a microscopic view of a scalp and a clear forehead being "protected" from flakes.
- **Infographic:** The "Double Scalp Wash" technique (Regular Shampoo -> Medicated Shampoo -> 5 Min Wait).
