## 2. Target Persona & 'Average Human' Tone Mandate
- **Reader Persona**: An everyday Indian teenager, college student, or adult troubled by a dark velvety neck, dark elbows/knees, underarms, or post-acne face marks. They have tried loofahs, besan packs, and scrubbing in the shower, only to find the skin turning even darker and rougher.
- **Tone Guidelines**:
  - Warm, empathetic, conversational, completely free from medical jargon overload.
  - Everyday Metaphor: 'Delicate Silk (Face) vs Tough Denim (Body)':
    - **Face Skin is Delicate Silk**: Only 10 to 15 cell layers thin. If you put 12% acid directly on it, it stings, turns red, and the skin reacts by producing more melanin (rebound darkening / PIH). 6% (Glyco-6) is the safe speed limit.
    - **Body & Neck Skin is Tough Denim**: The stratum corneum is 3 to 5 times thicker, especially where friction or insulin resistance builds up compacted layers of dead keratin. Here, 6% is too weak to dissolve the cellular glue; 12% (Glyco-12) has the penetration power needed to unglue dead cells.
  - **The Zero-Friction Mandate**:
    - Scrubbing a dark neck with loofahs or rough towels is the #1 mistake. High-melanin Indian skin treats rubbing as injury and darkens to protect itself (Frictional Melanosis).
  - **The Dry Skin Only Rule**:
    - Never apply glycolic acid cream on wet or damp skin. Water speeds up acid absorption dramatically, causing burning. Skin must be 100% dry for 10 minutes before application.
  - **Short-Contact Therapy (SCT)**:
    - For beginners or sensitive body folds, start by applying for 20 to 30 minutes, then rinse off. Only move to leave-on overnight once your skin proves it is happy.
