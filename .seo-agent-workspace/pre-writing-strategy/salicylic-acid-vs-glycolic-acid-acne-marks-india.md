## 2. Target Persona & "Average Human" Tone Mandate
- **Reader Persona**: An everyday Indian teenager, college student, or working professional standing in front of a bathroom mirror or scrolling an online pharmacy. They have breakouts or leftover dark marks and feel overwhelmed by skincare chemistry jargon.
- **Tone Guidelines**:
  - Warm, conversational, reassuring, and completely jargon-free.
  - Explain complex science through simple, memorable everyday metaphors:
    - **Salicylic Acid (BHA)**: "The Deep Chimney Sweeper" — loves oil, dives down inside the greasy pore to clean out trapped gunk, blackheads, and prevent red pimples from exploding.
    - **Glycolic Acid (AHA)**: "The Surface Polisher" — loves water, sweeps across the top layer of skin, gently dissolving the glue holding old, stained, dark dead skin cells so that stubborn brown acne marks fade fast.
  - **The Golden Question**: "Do I have an active bump right now, or am I staring at the brown shadow left behind after a bump went away?"
  - **Indian Skin Rule**: High melanin (Fitzpatrick III–V) means aggressive peeling triggers rebound dark marks. Gentle pharmacy percentages (1–2% BHA, 6% AHA) plus mandatory daily SPF 50 are essential.
