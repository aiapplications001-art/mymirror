## 2. Key Pillars & Content Points
- **The Cortisol-Sebum Pathway:**
    - High stress triggers the adrenal glands to release **cortisol**. Cortisol binds to receptors on sebaceous glands, stimulating sebum production and local skin inflammation.
- **DHEA-S (Adrenal Androgens):**
    - High stress spikes DHEA-S, which is converted in the skin to more potent androgens (testosterone and DHT), causing cystic jawline breakouts.
- **Optimal Skin Ranges vs. Lab Normals:**
    - Morning cortisol ranges vary. For clear skin, keeping TSH, glucose, and cortisol stable is key. High morning cortisol (>20 mcg/dL) indicates systemic chronic stress.
- **Cost & Lab Options in India:**
    - Morning cortisol costs ~₹450 - ₹650. DHEA-S costs ~₹600 - ₹850. Major providers include Tata 1mg, Lal PathLabs, and Apollo Diagnostics.
