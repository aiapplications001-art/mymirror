# Pre-Writing Strategy: Salicylic Acid (BHA) Purging Timeline (Indian Skin Specific)

**Topic:** Scientific explanation, week-by-week timeline, and barrier mitigation strategies for the initial purging phase of Salicylic Acid (BHA) on Indian skin.
**Primary Keyword:** `salicylic acid purging timeline`
**Target Audience:** Skincare users in India who have recently started or plan to start Salicylic Acid serums or washes (like Sebogel or Minimalist BHA) and are experiencing panic over whiteheads rising to the surface.

## 1. Editorial Empathy & Voice
- **Voice:** Reassuring, expert, highly scientific, yet easy to follow. 
- **Hook:** "You bought a salicylic acid serum or gel (like Sebogel) to clear your blackheads and tiny forehead bumps. But within two weeks, your face is covered in a fresh crop of small whiteheads. You panic, thinking the product is causing breakouts and making your skin worse. Stop. This is the Salicylic Acid purge. Because BHA is oil-soluble, it penetrates deep into the pores to dissolve sebum and push hidden clogs to the surface. Let's break down the exact week-by-week purging timeline and how to tell a purge from a true breakout."
- **Empathy Point:** Reassure the user that the temporary bumps are normal and a sign of active clearing.

## 2. Key Pillars & Content Points
- **The Biology of BHA Purging:**
    - Explain that Salicylic Acid is a beta hydroxy acid (BHA). It is lipophilic (oil-soluble), allowing it to penetrate deep inside the pore lining to dissolve dead skin cells and sebum.
    - By cleaning out the follicle, it accelerates the timeline of microcomedones rising to the surface as whiteheads.
- **The Week-by-Week BHA Timeline:**
    - **Weeks 1–2: The Rise:** Small microcomedones start rising. You might see a sudden increase in tiny whiteheads in congested areas.
    - **Weeks 3–4: Peak Purging:** Bumps are fully visible. This is the peak of the cleaning cycle.
    - **Weeks 5–6: The Clearance:** Bumps flatten out, redness decreases, and skin begins to look smoother.
    - **Week 8+: Clear Skin:** Pores look tighter, blackheads are reduced, and new breakouts are minimized.
- **How to Tell the Difference: Purge vs. Allergic Breakout:**
    - Purging occurs *only* in areas where you normally get acne (e.g. T-zone, forehead).
    - Allergic reactions occur in new, clear zones, accompanied by hot, itchy hives or swelling.
- **The BHA Purge Survival Protocol:**
    - **Do not over-use:** Start with 2–3 times a week (for serums) or every other day (for washes).
    - **Hydrate:** Pair BHA with a basic, non-comedogenic ceramide moisturizer.
    - **Sunscreen:** Mandatory next morning.
