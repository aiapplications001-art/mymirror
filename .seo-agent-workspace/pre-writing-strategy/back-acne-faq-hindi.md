## 2. Key Pillars & Core Lessons (In Spoken Hinglish)
1. **Peeth Par Pimples Kyu Nikalte Hain? (4 Mukhya Wajah):**
   - *Gym Ka Paseena Aur Tight Clothes:* Paseena aur dead skin pores ko block kar dete hain (Acne Mechanica).
   - *Hair Conditioner Ki Residue:* Baal dhote waqt conditioner peeth par bahta hai aur pores band kar deta hai.
   - *Hair Champi Ka Tel:* Sar par lagaya gaya tel sote waqt takiye aur peeth par lagta hai.
   - *Whey Protein Aur Meetha:* Gym supplements aur sugar spikes se body mein oil production tezi se badh jata hai.
2. **Bacterial Bacne vs Fungal Bacne (Kaise Pehchanein?):**
   - *Bacterial Pimples:* Bade, laal, dard wale aur pus bhare dane. Inke liye Benzoyl Peroxide (Perobar 2.5%) ya Salicylic Acid (Saslic DS) chahiye.
   - *Fungal Folliculitis:* Chote, ek barabar size ke dane jinme bohot khujli hoti hai, khas kar paseene mein. Iske liye Ketoconazole shampoo/wash (Nizral 2%) chahiye.
3. **The 3-Minute Shower Rule (Peeth Dhone Ka Sahi Tareeka):**
   - Step 1: Pehle baal dhoye aur conditioner ko pura dho kar baal upar baandh lein.
   - Step 2: Medicated body wash ya soap ko peeth par lagayein.
   - Step 3: Turant paani na dalein! Kam se kam 2 se 3 minute foam ko peeth par rehne dein taaki active ingredients bacteria ko maar sakein.
4. **Loofah Aur Scrubbing Ka Khatra (Kyu Band Karein?):**
   - Plastic loofah germs aur bacteria ka ghar ban jata hai.
   - Peeth ko zor se ghisne se skin chhil jaati hai aur kaale daag (PIH) pad jaate hain.
5. **Peeth Ke Kaale Daag Kaise Hatayein? (Dark Marks Solution):**
   - Tyrosinase inhibitors: Kojic Acid, Azelaic Acid 10%, Lactic Acid 10% body lotion.
   - Kabhi bhi daane ko phodein ya kharchein nahi.
