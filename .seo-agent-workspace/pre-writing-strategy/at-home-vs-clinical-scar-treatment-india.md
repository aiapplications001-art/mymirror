## 3. GEO & AI Overview Fast Takeaway
- **Post-Hero Fast Takeaway (`⚡ 10-Second Dermatologist Takeaway`):** Placed in a dedicated full-width container directly after the hero banner:
  - **Flat Mark vs Pitted Hole:** Red/brown flat spots (PIH/PIE) are surface pigment issues that respond to at-home actives (Azelaic acid, Retinoids). Depressed holes and pits are structural collagen loss that creams cannot fill.
  - **Why Depressed Scars Need Clinic Procedures:** Tethered rolling scars require Subcision to cut internal fibrous cords. Deep boxcars require MNRF to heat and rebuild dermal collagen. Narrow ice picks require TCA CROSS chemical focal tightening.
  - **The At-Home Dermaroller Trap:** Cheap home rollers (0.5mm) only scratch the surface epidermis, introduce bacterial infections, and cause micro-tears on Indian skin without reaching the 1.5–2.5mm dermal scar depth.
  - **Honest India Cost Guide:** Realistic multi-session protocols (MNRF ₹5,000–₹12,000/session, Subcision ₹4,000–₹8,000/session) typically deliver 50% to 70% visible smoothing over 6 to 9 months; 100% complete eradication is a marketing myth.
