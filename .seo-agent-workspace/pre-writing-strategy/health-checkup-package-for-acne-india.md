## 2. Key Pillars & Content Points
- **The Insulin-IGF-1 Loop (Metabolic Markers):**
    - High blood sugar (HbA1c) triggers insulin spikes.
    - Fasting insulin + glucose gives **HOMA-IR**. Elevated HOMA-IR stimulates **IGF-1**, causing sebocyte hyperplasia and follicular clogging.
- **The Liver Clearance Pathway (Liver Function Tests - LFT):**
    - Liver enzymes SGOT/SGPT indicate metabolic clearance capacity.
    - The liver must break down circulating excess androgens; if it fails, they recirculate and stimulate sebum glands.
- **Cost & Lab Package Selection in India:**
