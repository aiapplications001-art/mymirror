Because the fungal acne yeast doesn't just feed on oil; it feeds on polysorbates, esters, and fatty acids that standard acne brands use as emulsifiers. ## 2. Key Pillars & Content Points
- **The Science: What Makes a Moisturizer "Fungal Acne Safe"?**
    - Recall that *Malassezia* feeds on carbon chains C11–C24. A moisturizer is only safe if it contains **zero plant oils, zero fatty acids (palmitic, stearic, oleic), and zero esters (glyceryl stearate, polysorbates, isopropyl palmitate)**.
    - Safe hydration sources: Glycerin, Hyaluronic Acid, Aloe Vera, Panthenol, Allantoin, and Squalane (C30).
- **The Top Fungal-Safe Moisturizers in India ( Vetted List):**
    - **1. Sebamed Clear Face Gel:** The gold standard. 100% lipid-free, pH 5.5, contains aloe and panthenol.
    - **2. Cipla Excela Max Hydrating Gel:** Pharmacy favorite, rich in niacinamide and ceramides in a safe gel base.
    - **3. Minimalist Vitamin B5 10% Gel Moisturizer:** Oil-free, lightweight gel with high-concentration panthenol.
    - **4. Laneige Water Bank Blue Hyaluronic Gel:** Premium option, deeply hydrating with zero yeast-feeding lipids.
- **The Ingredient Checker Cheat-Sheet:**
    - How to scan labels for hidden triggers (like *Polysorbate-80*, *PEG-100 Stearate*, *Isopropyl Myristate*).
    - Pairing moisturizers with active treatments (like Ketoconazole or Salicylic washes).
