# Pre-Writing Strategy: Gut-Skin Axis Diagnostics (SIBO, Food Intolerance & H. Pylori)

**Topic:** Scientific breakdown of the gut-acne connection, IgG vs. IgE testing, SIBO breath tests, H. Pylori diagnostics, and cost comparisons in India.
**Primary Keyword:** `food intolerance test for acne india`
**Target Audience:** Indian adults experiencing digestive distress (bloating, acidity, IBS) alongside stubborn inflammatory acne.

## 1. Editorial Empathy & Voice
- **Voice:** Category Manager with Editorial Empathy.
- **Hook:** "Have you cut out dairy, sugar, and gluten, but your acne is still flaring? Many people turn to expensive 'food sensitivity' blood tests to find the culprit, only to get a list of 50 foods they can supposedly never eat again."
- **Empathy Point:** Validating the anxiety and stress of food restriction, and redirecting focus toward actual medical diagnostics.

## 2. Key Pillars & Content Points
- **The Food Intolerance Hype (IgG vs. IgE):**
    - Explain that IgG panels measure exposure/tolerance, not allergies. Cutting out foods based on IgG tests reduces gut diversity, which actually worsens acne. True allergies are IgE-mediated.
- **True Gut Diagnostics for Acne:**
    - **SIBO Breath Test:** Small Intestinal Bacterial Overgrowth leaks lipopolysaccharides (LPS) into the blood, triggering systemic skin inflammation.
    - **H. Pylori Stool Antigen:** Stomach infection that lowers stomach acid, leading to poor protein digestion and nutritional/acne issues.
- **Cost & Lab Options in India:**
    - IgG food sensitivity tests cost ₹3,000 - ₹10,000. Recommend spending this budget on SIBO breath tests (~₹2,500) or H. Pylori stool tests (~₹800).
