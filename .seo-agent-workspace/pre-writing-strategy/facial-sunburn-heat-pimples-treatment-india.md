## 2. Target Persona & Clinical Context
- **Persona**: Indian women and men who experience intense skin burning, tight peeling cheeks, or sudden clusters of prickly red bumps across their forehead and cheeks during hot, humid summer months or after intense outdoor sun exposure (commuting, beach vacations, two-wheeler helmets, sports).
- **Clinical Nuance**: 
  - Heat pimples are Miliaria rubra (occluded eccrine sweat glands releasing sweat into deeper epidermis), NOT Cutibacterium acnes bacterial infection.
  - Applying harsh acne spot treatments (benzoyl peroxide, salicylic acid, glycolic acid) to heat pimples or sunburnt skin strips damaged barrier lipids, inducing severe contact dermatitis and persistent post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH).
- **Tone**: Medically rigorous, reassuring, practical, structured for fast decision-making during an acute skin emergency.
