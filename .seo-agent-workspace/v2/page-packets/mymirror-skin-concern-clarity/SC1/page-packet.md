# What Is Wrong With My Skin? — SC1 Page Packet

- **Canonical:** `https://mymirror.fit/skin-analysis/what-is-wrong-with-my-skin/`
- **Audience:** people seeking an understandable first step for a new, persistent, or confusing visible facial-skin change.
- **Primary CTA:** `/scan` — “Start your free skin analysis now”.
- **Conversion promise:** organise visible patterns and context before deciding whether to observe, simplify a routine, scan, or seek professional care.
- **Scope boundary:** education and visible-pattern organisation only; no diagnosis, prescriptions, or medical triage replacement.

## On-page structure

Hero; quick answer; four-clue framework; seven visible-pattern descriptions; timeline; face-zone context; product-routine audit; skin-tone-aware observations; scan explanation; next-step table; escalation boundary; photo guidance; eight FAQs; sources; final CTA.
