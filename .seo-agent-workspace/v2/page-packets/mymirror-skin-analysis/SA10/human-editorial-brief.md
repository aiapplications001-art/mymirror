# Human Editorial Brief

Tone: calm, cautious, practical.

Depth focus: Visible pores are normal anatomy, not a defect. The reason people search for pores on face is usually that pores look larger than usual in certain light, around the nose, across the cheeks, or when skin feels oily, congested, irritated, or less firm. A useful guide separates pore visibility from acne, blackheads, texture, scarring, and camera distortion. MyMirror can help you track where pores look most noticeable and whether oil, clogged-looking areas, redness, or rough texture are present. It cannot shrink pores, diagnose acne, or prove that a product works. The practical goal is to make pores less noticeable while protecting the skin barrier and keeping normal skin texture in perspective.

Do not convert visible-signal guidance into diagnosis or treatment claims.
