1. Overview

Welcome to MyMirror (accessible via mymirror.fit). At MyMirror, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data and sensitive facial information. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle camera permissions, real-time facial pattern analysis, diagnostic logs, and website data when you use our interactive skin analysis tools.

Our Core Privacy Promise We process visual facial patterns directly inside your web browser or transient scanning memory. We do not store, sell, or monetize raw facial photographs or biometric identity maps.

2. On-Device & Real-Time AI Scanning

MyMirror uses advanced real-time computer vision models to identify visible skin texture, redness, oily zones, and forehead patterns.

  • Camera Stream: When you initiate a live skin scan at https://mymirror.fit/scan, camera access is requested explicitly by your web browser.
  • Transient Memory Processing: Video frames are analyzed locally in your client device memory to overlay visual Care Maps.
  • No Raw Image Saving: Once your scan is rendered, raw camera video feeds are discarded immediately and never transmitted to external storage buckets.

3. Information We Collect

We collect limited categories of technical and self-reported information to deliver your personalized skin routine recommendations:

  • Diagnostic Scan Metrics: Aggregated, non-identifiable numerical scores (e.g., pore density index, barrier hydration score, redness level).
  • Device & Browser Data: IP address, browser type, operating system version, screen resolution, and referring website URLs.
  • Self-Reported Quiz Inputs: Skin type (oily, combination, dry, sensitive), current skincare product usage, and primary skincare goals.
  • Support Inquiries: Name, email address, and message contents when you contact our support team.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected information for the following specific purposes:

  • To render your interactive 3-Layer Face Care Map and personalized skincare routine steps.
  • To improve our scanning algorithm accuracy and reduce pattern recognition false positives across diverse skin types.
  • To deliver non-medical educational content, ingredient guides, and product comparisons tailored to your concerns.
  • To monitor platform health, prevent abuse, and diagnose web performance bottlenecks.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or skin analysis metrics to third-party marketers or advertisers.

We may share minimal data with trusted service infrastructure providers strictly for operating the website:

  • Hosting & CDN: Vercel Inc. for secure content deployment and web request routing.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics (GA4) with IP anonymization enabled to measure page visit performance.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by valid court order, search warrant, or applicable regulatory law.

6. Security & Data Retention

We enforce industry-standard technical safeguards to protect data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration:

  • End-to-end SSL/TLS (HTTPS) encryption for all web communications.
  • Strict access controls and automated log expiration policies.
  • Transient analytics logs are retained only as long as necessary to fulfill service operations and security audits.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location (including rights under regional privacy laws), you have the right to:

  • Request access to any personal data associated with your account or email address.
  • Request the immediate deletion of your support correspondence and quiz submissions.
  • Revoke camera permissions at any time via your browser settings menu.

To exercise any privacy rights, please email us at support@mymirror.fit.

8. Cookies & Local Browser Storage

MyMirror uses essential session storage and functional cookies to remember your active scan settings and user preferences across page navigation. You can disable non-essential cookies through your browser configuration without breaking basic website viewing.
