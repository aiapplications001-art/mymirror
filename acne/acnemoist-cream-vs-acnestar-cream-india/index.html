The Confusion: Moisturiser vs Treatment

Because both products are widely sold at Indian pharmacies under similar names, users often confuse them. **Acnemoist** is a highly-prescribed non-comedogenic moisturiser designed to soothe dry, peeling skin. **Acnestar** is an active antibacterial gel/soap containing clindamycin and nicotinamide, designed to kill acne-causing bacteria on active spots. Confusing the two can lead to either dry, irritated skin (using Acnestar all over as a moisturiser) or blocked pores.

Deep Dive: Acnemoist Cream

Acnemoist is formulated specifically to combat the extreme dryness and peeling caused by oral isotretinoin (Accutane) or topical retinoids. It utilizes **jojoba esters** which mimic the skin's natural sebum structure to restore moisture without trigger comedones.

Key Benefit 💧

Non-Comedogenic Hydration

Unlike standard heavy moisturisers, Acnemoist is clinically tested to be non-comedogenic on Indian skin. It provides deep, long-lasting hydration without blocking pores or creating a greasy layer.

Key Benefit 🛡️

Retinoid Companion

If you are experiencing peeling, flaking, or redness from starting tretinoin or adapalene, Acnemoist is one of the safest pharmacy moisturisers to buffer the active and restore skin comfort.

4
Deep Dive: Acnestar Gel / Soap

Acnestar Gel is a dual-action active treatment. It contains **Clindamycin 1%** (a topical lincosamide antibiotic that stops bacterial protein synthesis) and **Nicotinamide 4%** (vitamin B3, which reduces inflammation and fades dark marks).

Key Active ⚔️

Antibacterial Power

Clindamycin directly kills the *Cutibacterium acnes* bacteria trapped inside inflamed pustules, resolving surface redness and swelling within 48–72 hours.

Key Active 🩹

Nicotinamide Anti-Inflammatory

At 4% concentration, Nicotinamide matches the anti-inflammatory power of mild steroids, calming the redness of active acne and helping to prevent the formation of dark spots (PIH).

⚠️ Antibiotic Resistance Warning: Using clindamycin (Acnestar) as a lone active for more than 4–6 weeks can lead to bacterial resistance. Always use it alongside a benzoyl peroxide wash (like Benzac AC) to prevent bacteria from adapting.
5
How to Combine Acnemoist & Acnestar Safely

If your dermatologist has prescribed both, here is the correct way to layer them in your daily routine:

☀️ AM Routine

1. Wash face with a gentle cleanser.
2. Apply a thin layer of **Acnestar Gel** directly to active red pimples only. Let dry for 3 minutes.
3. Apply **Acnemoist Cream** generously across the entire face.
4. Layer non-comedogenic SPF 50 sunscreen.

🌙 PM Routine

1. Double cleanse to remove sunscreen/sebum.
2. Apply your retinoid (tretinoin or adapalene) using the sandwich method.
3. Finish with **Acnemoist Cream** all over the face to lock in hydration and prevent overnight peeling.

Expert FAQ: Acnemoist & Acnestar
Is Acnemoist a pimple removal cream?+
No. Acnemoist is a non-comedogenic moisturiser. It does not contain active pimple-clearing ingredients like benzoyl peroxide or clindamycin. It is specifically formulated to hydrate skin and repair barriers damaged by drying acne treatments like isotretinoin or tretinoin.
Can I apply Acnestar cream all over my face?+
No. Acnestar Gel contains clindamycin (an antibiotic) and sometimes nicotinamide. It is an active spot treatment designed to be applied in a thin layer directly onto active, inflamed pimples only. Applying it all over can cause unnecessary irritation and increase the risk of topical antibiotic resistance.
Does Acnestar cause skin peeling?+
Acnestar Gel containing clindamycin and nicotinamide is relatively gentle, but some formulations with benzoyl peroxide can cause mild dryness or peeling on spot areas. Pairing it with Acnemoist moisturiser prevents this dryness.
