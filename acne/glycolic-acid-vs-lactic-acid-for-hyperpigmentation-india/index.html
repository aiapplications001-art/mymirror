1. Quick Verdict

Quick Verdict

Quick Verdict: Which Active Ingredient Should You Pick?

Choose Glycolic Acid (5%-10%) if your main skin concern is deep epidermal hyperpigmentation, stubborn sun spots, or coarse skin texture, provided your skin barrier is resilient. Choose Lactic Acid (5%-10%) if you have sensitive, dry, or melanin-dense skin prone to post-acne marks, as it offers gentle exfoliation with natural humectant hydration.

2. Dermatological Action Mechanisms

Comparison Methodology

Dermatological Comparison & Action Mechanisms

Glycolic Acid has the smallest molecular size (76.05 g/mol) among Alpha Hydroxy Acids, enabling deep epidermal penetration to break desmosome bonds. Lactic Acid has a larger molecular structure (90.08 g/mol) and inherent NMF humectant properties, exfoliating surface dead cells with minimal burning.

3. Decision Criteria for Indian Skin Types

Decision Criteria

Decision Criteria for Indian Skin Types

Indian skin Fitzpatrick IV-VI has higher epidermal melanin density. High-concentration Glycolic Acid can trigger post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH) if overused. Lactic Acid is preferred for sensitive or barrier-compromised skin.
