Dermatological Comparison & Action Mechanisms

Humid Indian weather elevates sebum viscosity. High UV exposure triggers sebum oxidation (squalene hydroperoxides), aggravating comedonal acne. Modern fluid SPFs utilize micro-encapsulated organic filters that form a light breathable film over pores.
