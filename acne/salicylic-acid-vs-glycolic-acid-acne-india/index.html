The Solubility Difference: Oil vs Water

The core clinical difference between Salicylic Acid and Glycolic Acid comes down to chemical solubility. **Salicylic Acid is a Beta Hydroxy Acid (BHA)** that is **oil-soluble**, allowing it to pass through sebum to deep inside pore linings. **Glycolic Acid is an Alpha Hydroxy Acid (AHA)** that is **water-soluble**, working exclusively on the skin surface to break desmosome bonds between dead skin cells.

When to Choose Salicylic Acid (BHA)
Pore Unclogger 🫧

Active Breakouts & Blackheads

If your primary skin issue is active pus pimples, oily congestion, or stubborn nose blackheads, Salicylic acid 2% is the essential active for clearing the pore lining.

Calming Effect 🌿

Anti-Inflammatory Relief

Salicylic acid is structurally related to aspirin (salicylate), giving it natural anti-inflammatory properties that reduce swelling and redness around painful inflamed lesions.

4
When to Choose Glycolic Acid (AHA)
Cellular Turnover ✨

Fading Post-Acne Dark Marks (PIH)

Glycolic acid has the smallest molecular size among AHAs, allowing it to rapidly break down stratum corneum bonds to shed hyperpigmented skin cells and reveal fresh, even skin.

Pharma Staples 💊

Glyco-6 & Glyco-12 in India

Pharmacy favorites like Glyco-6 Cream (~₹220) are widely prescribed in India for targeted night-time application on stubborn dark spots and rough texture.

How to Combine AHA & BHA Safely

Combining both acids gives comprehensive pore + surface clearance, but doing so recklessly will disrupt your moisture barrier:

💡 Safe Exfoliation Routine:
• Option A (Time-Split): Use a Salicylic acid wash (BHA) in the AM, and Glycolic acid toner/cream (AHA) 2 nights a week.
• Option B (Formulated Blends): Use a pre-formulated AHA 10% + BHA 2% chemical peel solution (e.g. Minimalist / The Ordinary peel) once every 10–14 days.

Expert FAQ: Salicylic vs Glycolic
Which is better for active acne: Salicylic or Glycolic acid?+
Salicylic acid (BHA) is superior for active acne, blackheads, and oily skin because it is oil-soluble and penetrates deep inside sebum-filled pores to dissolve blockages. Glycolic acid (AHA) works on the skin surface to fade post-acne dark marks.
Can I use Salicylic acid and Glycolic acid together?+
Yes, but alternate them (e.g. Salicylic acid AM/BHA cleanser and Glycolic acid PM toner 2x/week). Applying high-strength AHA and BHA in the exact same step can strip the skin barrier on Indian skin.
What concentration of Salicylic acid is safe for daily use in India?+
1% to 2% Salicylic acid is the dermatologist-recommended concentration for daily cleansers or leave-on serums for oily, acne-prone Indian skin.
