Quick Verdict: Which Active Ingredient Should You Pick?

Red marks (PIE) stem from damaged capillaries and require Niacinamide and Azelaic Acid. Dark brown spots (PIH) stem from melanin overproduction and require Alpha Arbutin 2% and Tranexamic Acid 3-5%.

Dermatological Comparison & Action Mechanisms

Melanin synthesis relies on the tyrosinase enzyme. Dermatological actives like Alpha Arbutin and Tranexamic Acid block tyrosinase activity safely without destroying healthy melanocyte cells.
