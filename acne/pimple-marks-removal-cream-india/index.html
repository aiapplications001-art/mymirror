1. Know Your Mark: PIE vs PIH

PIE (Red / Purple Marks)

Post-Inflammatory Erythema caused by damaged capillaries near skin surface. Treated best with Niacinamide, Azelaic Acid, and Cica.

PIH (Brown / Dark Spots)

Post-Inflammatory Hyperpigmentation caused by excess melanin deposition. Treated best with Alpha Arbutin, Tranexamic Acid, and Kojic Acid.

2. Dermatologist Recommended Fading Actives

Active Ingredient Concentration Action Mechanism
Alpha Arbutin 2% Safe hydroquinone derivative that inhibits tyrosinase without cytotoxicity.
Tranexamic Acid 3% - 5% Blocks plasminogen pathway to prevent UV-induced melanogenesis.
Kojic Acid 1% - 2% Cheles copper ions in tyrosinase enzyme to lighten stubborn brown spots.

3. Why Avoid OTC Triple Combination Creams (Hydroquinone)

⚠️ Avoid OTC Steroid Creams (Melacare / No Scars)

Steroid-hydroquinone combination creams can cause exogenous ochronosis (permanent blue-black skin darkening), skin thinning, and rebound hyperpigmentation when stopped. Stick to non-steroid tyrosinase inhibitors.

4. Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take for pimple marks to fade?

Epidermal PIH spots usually take 4 to 8 weeks of daily sunscreen + active usage to fade significantly. Deep dermal marks may take 3 to 6 months.
