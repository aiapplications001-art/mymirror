The Vitamin A Conversion Pathway

All retinoids must ultimately become **Retinoic Acid** to bind to retinoic acid receptors (RARs) in human skin cells to accelerate cellular turnover. The fundamental difference between Retinol and Tretinoin lies in how many conversion steps are required:

🧬 The Retinoid Conversion Chain:
• Tretinoin: Pure Retinoic Acid ➔ Binds to cell receptors immediately (100% active).
• Retinaldehyde: 1-Step Conversion ➔ Retinoic Acid.
• Retinol: 2-Step Conversion ➔ Retinaldehyde ➔ Retinoic Acid (Enzymatic loss reduces potency).
• Retinyl Esters (Palmitate): 3-Step Conversion ➔ Retinol ➔ Retinaldehyde ➔ Retinoic Acid.

The Sandwich Method for Indian Skin

To prevent severe barrier damage, dryness, and post-retinoid hyperpigmentation on brown skin, use the **Sandwich Technique**:

  1. Layer 1 (Base): Apply a thin layer of ceramide-rich moisturizer to clean, dry skin. Wait 10 minutes.
  2. Layer 2 (Active): Apply a pea-sized amount of Tretinoin or Retinol evenly across the face (avoiding eyes and lip corners).
  3. Layer 3 (Top): Seal with a second layer of cream moisturizer.

Expert FAQ: Retinol vs Tretinoin
Is Tretinoin stronger than Retinol?+
Yes. Tretinoin is pure retinoic acid and binds directly to skin cell receptors without requiring enzymatic conversion. OTC Retinol must undergo a 2-step conversion process (Retinol -> Retinaldehyde -> Retinoic Acid) inside skin cells, making Tretinoin roughly 20 times more potent.
Do I need a prescription for Tretinoin in India?+
Yes. Tretinoin (Retino-A, A-Ret, Stieva-A) is classified as a Schedule H prescription drug in India due to risks of retinoid dermatitis, severe purging, and teratogenicity.
What is the Sandwich Method for applying retinoids?+
The Sandwich Method involves applying a layer of moisturizer first, waiting 10 minutes, applying a pea-sized amount of retinoid, and sealing with another layer of moisturizer. This buffers the active to prevent flaking and barrier damage on Indian skin.
