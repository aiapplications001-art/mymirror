1. The Active Compounds of Centella Asiatica: Gotu Kola Biochemistry

Centella Asiatica, commonly known as Gotu Kola or Tiger Grass, has been a staple in traditional medicine and is now a superstar in modern dermatology. The efficacy of Cica in treating acne and sensitive skin stems from its potent triterpenoids, specifically Madecassoside, Asiaticoside, Madecassic Acid, and Asiatic Acid.

When applied to the skin, these compounds actively downregulate pro-inflammatory cytokines such as IL-1b and TNF-a, significantly reducing the erythema (redness) and swelling associated with inflammatory acne lesions.

Collagen Synthesis & Scar Prevention

Beyond calming active breakouts, Cica acts as a powerful wound healer. It stimulates type I collagen synthesis, encouraging the skin to repair itself quickly and effectively. This accelerated healing process is critical in preventing deep atrophic scars and post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH) often left behind by severe acne.

4. Cica for Barrier Repair Post-Acne Over-Exfoliation

Aggressive acne treatments—like daily use of high-percentage AHAs, BHAs, or prescription retinoids—often lead to a compromised skin barrier. Symptoms include chronic redness, stinging upon applying moisturizer, and increased breakouts.

🚨 The Over-Exfoliation Trap

When your barrier is damaged, your skin cannot retain moisture or defend against bacteria. Continuing to use active acids will only worsen the condition, leading to a cycle of irritation and acne.

Cica is the perfect antidote. A recovery protocol using a Cica-rich cream (like Formularx Barrier Relief) provides the necessary lipids and soothing triterpenoids to rebuild the stratum corneum, stop transepidermal water loss, and bring down inflammation.

5. Cica + Salicylic Acid: The Acne-Fighting & Soothing Combo

While Salicylic Acid is exceptional at dissolving sebum plugs and treating acne, it can be inherently drying and irritating. Pairing it with Centella Asiatica creates a synergistic "dual pathway" approach:

1. The Exfoliation Pathway: Salicylic Acid deeply cleanses the pores and prevents comedone formation.
2. The Soothing Pathway: Cica immediately neutralizes the irritation caused by the acid, preventing redness and supporting the skin barrier.

Products like Dot & Key's Cica + Salicylic Gel utilize this synergy, offering effective acne treatment with a significantly reduced risk of irritation, making it ideal for sensitive Indian skin.

6. AM/PM Routine for Redness-Prone Sensitive Indian Skin

Flare-Up Protocol
