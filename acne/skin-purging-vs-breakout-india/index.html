1 The Cellular Science: Turnover Acceleration & Microcomedone Extrusion

Outpatient clinical protocols across Indian tertiary dermatology centers require careful differential diagnosis between active follicular purging and cosmetic product-induced breakouts. When introducing prescription keratolytics such as topical adapalene (Glenmark Adaferin 0.1% gel, INR 320 to 395 per 15g tube) or salicylic acid washes (Cipla Saslic DS 2% foaming wash, INR 420 to 495 per 100ml bottle), active cell turnover accelerates follicular desquamation. Under physiological baseline conditions, epidermal corneocyte maturation requires 28 days. Topical retinoids and lipophilic beta-hydroxy acids compress this latency to 10 through 14 days, forcing subclinical microcomedones to the stratum corneum surface simultaneously as erythematous papules or superficial pustules. Conversely, cosmetic contact dermatitis or product occlusion from comedogenic lipids manifests as irregular inflammatory papulopustular eruptions in previously clear anatomical zones. Dermatologists assess lesion latency: follicular purging resolves rapidly within 4 to 6 weeks as microcomedonal reserves exhaust, whereas true breakouts persist indefinitely while triggering secondary post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation. To buffer stratum corneum permeability, clinicians recommend co-administering multi-lamellar ceramide emulsions (Cipla Excela Max, INR 480 to 540 per 50g tube) to stabilize barrier lipids without follicular clogging across Fitzpatrick phototypes IV through VI. Anatomical distribution: habitual breakout zones vs novel unaffected zones. Lesion resolution speed: rapid pustular clearance in 3 to 5 days vs prolonged cystic erythema over 14 days. Epidermal barrier symptoms: mild focal desquamation vs intense itching and dermatitis. 2 Clinical Intervention Guidelines & The 6-Week Cap

Outpatient dermatologists prescribe structured intervention protocols across Indian retail pharmacies and clinical dispensaries to manage keratolytic adjustment periods. For patients experiencing acute retinoid dermatitis or localized microcomedone extrusion, clinicians recommend temporary frequency reduction of topical adapalene (Glenmark Adaferin 0.1% gel, INR 320 to 395 per 15g tube) or topical benzoyl peroxide lotions (Galderma Benzac AC 2.5% gel, INR 145 to 195 per 30g tube) to alternate evenings. To maintain stratum corneum hydration without inducing comedogenesis, patients apply a physiological multi-lamellar ceramide emulsion (Cipla Excela Max, INR 480 to 540 per 50g) twenty minutes prior to active application. Outpatient dermatology consensus guidelines mandate an upper boundary of six weeks for purging resolution: if inflammatory papules persist beyond week six without lesion reduction, clinicians discontinue the offending vehicle, screen for allergic contact dermatitis, and initiate non-comedogenic azelaic acid formulations (Aziderm 10% to 15% gel, Micro Labs, INR 280 to 360 per 15g tube) to suppress follicular inflammation across Fitzpatrick phototypes IV through VI.
