Step 1: Cleanser — Keep It Boring

The biggest mistake Indian acne patients make is choosing a "drying" or "acne-fighting" face wash with multiple actives. A cleanser is a rinse-off product — it's on your face for 30 seconds. The active ingredients don't have time to work. All a cleanser needs to do is remove sunscreen, sebum, pollution, and sweat without stripping the skin barrier.

Expert FAQ: Budget Acne Routine India
Can I clear acne with products under ₹500 in India?+
Yes. The core acne-clearing actives — salicylic acid and benzoyl peroxide for active pimples, adapalene for prevention — are all available in India for well under ₹500 total. Expensive luxury brands don't outperform budget options on ingredient efficacy. A basic 4-step routine with a gentle cleanser, SA or BPO treatment, simple moisturiser, and mineral SPF is clinically proven to clear mild-moderate acne.
What is the cheapest effective acne treatment in India?+
Adaferin Gel 0.1% (Adapalene) by Galderma is the most cost-effective prescription-level acne treatment available OTC in India at around ₹180 for a 15g tube. Used 4 nights per week with the Sandwich Method, it both clears and prevents acne while fading dark marks. Paired with a ₹80–150 salicylic acid face wash, this two-product combination covers most acne needs under ₹350.
Do I need a moisturiser if I have oily acne-prone skin?+
Yes — skipping moisturiser with oily skin makes acne worse. When the skin is dehydrated, it overproduces sebum to compensate, clogging pores. A lightweight, non-comedogenic gel moisturiser hydrates without adding oil, keeping the barrier healthy and preventing the dryness that actives like BPO and adapalene cause.
Can I skip sunscreen to save money?+
No — skipping SPF negates most of your treatment results. UV exposure on Indian skin causes and worsens PIH (post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation), the dark marks that remain after acne heals. Adapalene also increases UV sensitivity. Lotus Herbals SPF 50 at ₹80 is the cheapest reliable option and should be considered non-negotiable even on a tight budget.
Is Adaferin Gel available without a prescription in India?+
Yes — Adaferin Gel 0.1% is technically a prescription product but is widely available OTC at most pharmacies across India without requiring a prescription. It's one of the most accessible retinoid options in the country. The combination product Deriva-CMS (Adapalene + Clindamycin) does require a prescription as it contains an antibiotic.
