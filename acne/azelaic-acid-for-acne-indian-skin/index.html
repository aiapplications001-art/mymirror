Why 10% Azelaic Acid is Superior for Melanin-Rich Skin

Topical 10% Azelaic Acid is a dicarboxylic acid naturally derived from grains. For Indian skin (Fitzpatrick phototypes IV-VI), it offers a crucial advantage over aggressive peeling acids: it selectively targets hyperactive, abnormal melanocytes while leaving normal baseline skin tone completely unaffected.

Antibacterial Action

Reduces follicular C. acnes bacteria without creating antibiotic resistance or skin dryness.

Selective PIH Fading

Inhibits the tyrosinase enzyme to fade stubborn brown post-acne marks (PIH) safely.

Gentle Exfoliation

Normalizes keratinization inside pores to clear comedones and smooth rough skin texture.

The 4-Step Sandwich Protocol for Barrier Protection

Applying active dicarboxylic acids directly to damp skin in humid Indian weather can cause temporary tingling or stinging. Follow our dermatologist-tested Sandwich Protocol to buffer the active ingredient safely:

The 4-Step Application Checklist

Buffers active acid between hydrating layers to eliminate paresthesia and protect skin barrier function.

1

Cleanse Gently

Wash face with a sulfate-free, pH-balanced cleanser and pat dry thoroughly.

2

Apply Light Buffer

Apply a thin layer of oil-free moisturizer on damp skin and wait 5 minutes until fully dry.

3

Apply Azelaic 10%

Spread a pea-sized amount of 10% Azelaic Acid gel evenly across active breakout zones.

4

Seal Barrier

Seal with barrier repair cream at night or follow with broad-spectrum SPF 50 daytime.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does 10% Azelaic Acid cause skin purging? +

Mild temporary purging can occur during weeks 2 to 3 as microcomedones come to the surface. Continued consistent use resolves active breakouts within 4 to 6 weeks.

Can I combine Azelaic Acid with Niacinamide? +

Yes. Pairing 10% Azelaic Acid with 5% Niacinamide is highly effective for Indian skin because Niacinamide repairs barrier lipids and inhibits melanosome transfer, enhancing overall spot-fading results.

How long does it take to fade post-acne marks (PIH)? +

Clinical studies show noticeable reduction in dark mark intensity by weeks 6 to 8 of daily night application, with maximum tone evenness achieved by week 12 when paired with daytime sunscreen.
