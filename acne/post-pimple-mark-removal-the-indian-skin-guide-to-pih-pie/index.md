## Quick Answer

To remove post-pimple marks, you must first identify the type: **PIH (Post-Inflammatory Hyperpigmentation)** appears as brown or black spots and responds best to Tyrosinase inhibitors like **Kojic Acid (Kojivit)**. **PIE (Post-Inflammatory Erythema)** appears as red or pink marks and requires barrier-repairing agents like **Niacinamide** and **Azelaic Acid (Aziderm)**. Regardless of the type, clinical fading is impossible without daily broad-spectrum sunscreen to prevent the Indian sun from re-darkening the marks.

<!-- SECTION_ID: S3_context -->
## Context: Why Indian Skin Marks Differently

If you have Fitzpatrick Skin Type III to VI (typical for most Indians), your skin is built to produce melanin. When a pimple causes inflammation, your skin's defense mechanism overproduces melanin in that specific spot. This is why a simple breakout often leads to a dark brown mark that lasts for months.

 PIGMENTATION PATIENCE:
- **Scrubbing doesn't work:** You cannot "wash away" internal melanin.
- **Inflammation is the trigger:** If you pick or squeeze, you increase the trauma and the resulting darkness.

<!-- SECTION_ID: S4_main_content -->
## Main Content: The Pharma "Golden Duo"

For fading stubborn marks without the high cost of D2C luxury brands, Indian dermatologists rely on stabilized pharmacy staples.

### 1. Kojivit / Kojivit Ultra (The Melanin Inhibitor)
Kojivit contains **Kojic Acid Dipalmitate**, a potent Tyrosinase inhibitor that stops the enzyme responsible for melanin production. It's often paired with Arbutin and Mulberry extract.
- **Best For:** Old, stubborn brown spots (PIH).
- **Application:** Use as a spot treatment at night on dry skin.

### 2. Aziderm (The Multi-Tasker)
**Azelaic Acid** (available as Aziderm 10% or 15%) is a unique active that treats active acne while simultaneously preventing new marks from forming.
- **Best For:** Red marks (PIE) and preventing marks while acne is still active.
- **Pro Tip:** It's a large-molecule acid, making it gentler than Glycolic Acid for sensitive Indian skin.

### The UV Rule
If you use Kojivit or Aziderm at night but skip sunscreen during your morning commute, you are effectively canceling out your progress. UV rays "wake up" the melanocytes you're trying to put to sleep.

## FAQ

**How long does it take for Kojivit to work?**
Expect to see the first visible signs of fading after 4-6 weeks of consistent nightly use. Full results often take 12 weeks.

**Can I use Kojic Acid and Salicylic Acid together?**
Yes, but layer them carefully. Use Salicylic Acid (Sebogel) for active acne and Kojivit as a spot treatment on the marks.

**Does 'Fairness Cream' work for acne marks?**
Avoid generic fairness creams; they often contain steroids (like Clobetasol) which can thin your skin and cause 'steroid-induced acne'. Stick to clinical inhibitors like Kojic and Azelaic Acid.
