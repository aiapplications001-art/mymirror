1. Quick Verdict

Quick Verdict

Quick Verdict: Which Active Ingredient Should You Pick?

Niacinamide does not increase skin cell turnover, so it does NOT cause true cell purging. Small red bumps after applying 10% Niacinamide serum stem from concentration overload or vehicle sensitivity (zinc/preservatives). Switch to a 2%-5% Niacinamide formulation to heal your skin barrier.

2. Dermatological Action Mechanisms

Comparison Methodology

Dermatological Comparison & Action Mechanisms

Vitamin B3 (Niacinamide) functions as a coenzyme precursor (NAD+/NADP+) that reduces sebum production and strengthens ceramide synthesis. High 10% concentrations over-saturate epidermal receptors, triggering flushing, histamine release, and follicular irritation.

3. Decision Criteria for Indian Skin Types

Decision Criteria

Decision Criteria for Indian Skin Types

Differentiate irritation from purging: if small bumps appear in new areas accompanied by burning, stinging, or redness, it is barrier contact dermatitis from a 10% serum. True purging only occurs with cell-turnover actives like Retinoids and AHAs/BHAs.

How To Layer Both Actives Safely
