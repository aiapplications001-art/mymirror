1. The 3:1:1 Molar Lipid Ratio: Science of Dermal Barrier Repair

The stratum corneum barrier requires a specific 3:1:1 molar ratio of Ceramides, Cholesterol, and Free Fatty Acids to repair damaged skin lamellae. Single-lipid formulas fail to provide full structural barrier recovery.

Why 3:1:1 Ratio Matters for Acne-Prone Skin

When using potent acne actives like Differin, Tretinoin, or Salicylic Acid, your lipid barrier breaks down. Replenishing all 3 lipids in physiological proportion restores barrier function without causing breakouts.
