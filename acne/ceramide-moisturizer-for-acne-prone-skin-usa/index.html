1. The 3:1:1 Molar Lipid Ratio: Science of Dermal Barrier Repair

Physiological ratio of Ceramides, Cholesterol, and Free Fatty Acids needed to rebuild stratum corneum lipid lamellae. Why single-ceramide creams fail without cholesterol.

Why 3:1:1?

Ceramides alone cannot repair the skin barrier effectively. The synergistic presence of cholesterol and free fatty acids is crucial to mimic the skin's natural lipid matrix and accelerate barrier restoration.

4. How Ceramide Creams Stop Retinoid Peeling & BHA Dermatitis

Using potent actives like tretinoin, adapalene, or BHA can strip your barrier. Incorporating a proper ceramide moisturizer uses a barrier buffering protocol.

Barrier Buffering Protocol

Apply a thin layer of your chosen ceramide moisturizer before your active treatment. This acts as a buffer, reducing the penetration rate and minimizing irritation and peeling without compromising efficacy.
