How It Works

How Adapalene Acts: Inside the Pore vs On the Barrier

Understanding the simple cellular mechanism between follicular clearing and stratum corneum adaptation.

Inside the Pore

Accelerated Microcomedone Expulsion

  • Binds to Nuclear Receptors: Adapalene selectively targets RAR-beta and RAR-gamma receptors in basal keratinocytes, telling pores to shed cells faster.
  • Compacts 90 Days into 14 Days: Pre-existing microcomedone plugs that would take 3 months to surface are propelled upward all at once.
  • Prevents Future Micro-Plugs: By normalizing cell desquamation, adapalene keeps pore canals open so new acne cannot form underground.
On the Barrier

Retinization vs Barrier Breakdown

  • Lipid Desynchronization: During weeks 1 to 2, skin cells divide faster than natural ceramides can glue them, causing temporary surface flaking.
  • Microscopic Cracks Cause Stinging: If active gel is applied to damp skin, it penetrates too deep, splitting surface lipids and provoking pain with water.
  • Restoration via Buffering: Layering a lightweight ceramide moisturizer first cushions the epidermis without weakening follicular clearance.

Indian Skin Guidelines

The 4 Golden Rules for Indian Skin on Adapalene

Indian skin complexions (Fitzpatrick IV–VI) possess hyper-reactive melanocytes. Following these rules prevents post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH).

1. The 10-Minute Bone-Dry Rule

Never apply adapalene on damp skin. Water acts like a penetration sponge that pulls the active ingredient straight into deep tissue, triggering severe burning, redness, and rapid barrier collapse.

2. No Acid Stacking for the First 6 Weeks

Discontinue salicylic acid face washes, glycolic toners, and vitamin C serums while navigating the purge. Adapalene manages cell turnover alone; adding acids guarantees lipid depletion and dark marks.

3. Zero Fingernail Squeezing or Picking

Squeezing a purging pustule ruptures follicular walls beneath the surface. In melanin-rich Indian skin, this trauma deposits pigment deep into the dermis, creating dark spots that persist for 6 to 9 months.

4. Mandatory Morning Broad-Spectrum SPF 50

Accelerated cell division brings fresh, unpigmented epidermal cells to the surface. Without broad-spectrum UV protection, outdoor daylight triggers rapid pigment overproduction, leaving dark blotches.

Buffering Protocol

The 4-Step Sandwich Buffering Protocol

Step-by-step application rules to maintain active pore clearance without provoking painful flaking.

Step 01 • Cleanse

Hydrating Wash & Wait

Cleanse with a mild non-foaming wash. Pat completely dry with a fresh towel and wait 5 full minutes until skin is bone dry before touching any products.

Step 02 • Buffer

Ceramide Buffer Coat

Smooth a dime-sized layer of fragrance-free ceramide lotion across your face. Wait 10 minutes to allow the protective moisture buffer to absorb fully.

Step 03 • Active

Pea-Sized Dotting

Squeeze one small pea of Adaferin or Deriva-MS. Dot evenly over forehead, cheeks, and chin. Keep clear of eyelid folds, nostril wings, and lip edges.

Step 04 • Seal

Light Moisture Seal

Gently pat a second thin coat of moisturizer over top. Avoid petroleum jelly slugging, which locks in excess heat and provokes chemical dermatitis.
