## 3. Human-First Conceptual Analogies
- **The "Sliced Apple" Analogy:** Blackheads are not dirt from pollution; they are natural sebum that oxidized and turned black upon contact with air, exactly like a cut apple left on a counter.
- **The "Oil Sponge" Action:** Water-soluble AHAs bead up and slide off facial oil (like water in a greasy pan). Oil-soluble BHA acts like an oil sponge, dissolving straight into the sebum river to break desmosome bonds.
- **The "Leather Shoe" Principle:** Pores have no muscles to open or shut. Hardened plugs stretch pore walls like bulky socks stuffed in a shoe. Once BHA clears the plug, skin elasticity allows pore walls to contract back to normal size.
- **Sebaceous Filaments vs. Blackheads:** Squeezing soft natural filaments causes broken capillaries and permanent pore stretching. BHA keeps them clean and barely visible.
