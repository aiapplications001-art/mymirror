The Retinoid Ladder: Two Levels of Vitamin A Power

Retinoids are Vitamin A derivatives widely considered the gold standard in acne treatment. They work by binding to Retinoic Acid Receptors (RARs) in skin cells, normalising follicular desquamation (how dead cells shed inside pores) and reducing the formation of microcomedones — the root of all acne.

However, not all retinoids are created equal. Think of them as a ladder — adapalene sits on the lower, safer rung, and tretinoin climbs higher in potency:

Gentler · OTC Available

Adapalene (0.1%)

A 3rd-generation naphthoic acid derivative that selectively binds RARβ and RARγ receptors, reducing inflammation while normalising pore cell shedding. Photostable (safe to apply morning or night), less irritating, and available OTC in India as Adaferin Gel.

More Potent · Prescription

Tretinoin (0.025–0.05%)

The original retinoic acid that binds all RAR subtypes (α, β, γ) non-selectively, delivering aggressive cell turnover. More effective for severe acne and scarring, but higher irritation and PIH risk on melanin-rich Indian skin. Available as Retino-A 0.025%/0.05%.

💡 The Indian Skin Rule: Fitzpatrick IV-VI skin produces melanin rapidly in response to inflammation. A strong retinoid purge can leave dark marks (PIH) worse than the original acne. Starting with adapalene significantly reduces this risk while still delivering excellent long-term clearing.

When to Choose Adapalene

Adapalene is the ideal first retinoid for the majority of Indian skin types. Choose adapalene if you have:

  • Forehead or chin comedonal acne (blackheads, whiteheads, tiny bumps)
  • Teenage or early-adult acne with mild-to-moderate severity
  • Sensitive or reactive skin that flares with strong actives
  • High PIH risk — darker skin tones prone to dark marks after irritation
  • No prior retinoid experience — starting retinoid journey from scratch
🛒 Recommended Adapalene Formulations in India: Adaferin Gel 0.1% (Galderma, OTC), Deriva-CMS Gel (Clindamycin + Adapalene 0.1%, prescription), Differin Gel 0.1% (imported, prescription).
4
When to Choose Tretinoin

Tretinoin delivers faster, deeper results and is reserved for skin that can tolerate a more aggressive retinoid. Choose tretinoin if you have:

  • Moderate to severe inflammatory acne — red papules, pustules, nodules
  • Post-acne rolling or shallow boxcar scars requiring collagen stimulation
  • Experienced retinoid users who have tolerated adapalene for 3+ months
  • Sun damage, fine lines, or pigmentation alongside acne (photo-rejuvenation benefit)
  • Dermatologist supervision available for monitoring and dose titration
🛒 Recommended Tretinoin Formulations in India: Retino-A 0.025% (Janssen Cilag — start here), Retino-A 0.05% (step-up after 3 months), RETTEN 0.025% Gel (Menarini).

The Sandwich Method: Reducing Retinoid Irritation on Indian Skin

The Sandwich Method buffers retinoid potency by applying moisturizer before and after the retinoid, reducing irritation by up to 50% while maintaining efficacy:

Step 1

Apply Moisturizer First

Cleanse and pat face dry. Apply a thin layer of a ceramide moisturizer. Wait 5–10 minutes for it to absorb. This "buffers" the acid microenvironment so the retinoid doesn't penetrate too aggressively.

Step 2

Apply Retinoid (Pea-Sized Amount)

Apply a pea-sized amount of adapalene or tretinoin across the entire face (not just pimples). Avoid eye area and lips. Dot onto forehead, cheeks, chin, and nose — then spread gently.

Step 3

Seal with Moisturizer Again

After 15–20 minutes, apply another layer of ceramide moisturizer to form a protective barrier over the retinoid and prevent transepidermal water loss.

Frequency Titration

Build Tolerance Gradually

Week 1–2: Use retinoid 1x/week.
Week 3–4: Increase to 2x/week.
Week 5–8: 3x/week.
Month 3+: Nightly if tolerated well.

Expert FAQ: Tretinoin vs. Adapalene
Does adapalene cause purging? +
Yes, adapalene causes a purging phase of 4–8 weeks as it accelerates cell turnover and pushes existing microcomedones to the surface. This is not a breakout — it is the retinoid clearing congestion already trapped inside pores.
Can I use tretinoin without a prescription in India? +
Tretinoin (Retino-A) is technically a prescription medication in India, though many pharmacies dispense it OTC. Adapalene 0.1% gel (Adaferin) is available OTC and is the safer starting retinoid without requiring a prescription.
How long until retinoids clear acne? +
Adapalene begins showing visible improvement in 8–12 weeks of consistent nightly use. Tretinoin typically shows faster results in 6–8 weeks but with more initial irritation. Full clearance takes 3–6 months for both.
Can I use retinoids with niacinamide? +
Yes. Niacinamide and adapalene/tretinoin are safe to layer and actually work synergistically. Apply the retinoid first, wait 20 minutes, then apply niacinamide serum. Niacinamide helps soothe retinoid-induced redness and prevents PIH during the purge phase.

💡 Quick Guide

  • Beginner: Adapalene 0.1% (Adaferin)
  • Severe Acne: Tretinoin 0.025%
  • OTC in India: Adapalene only
  • Sandwich Method: Moisturizer first
