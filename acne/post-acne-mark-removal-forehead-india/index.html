Quick Answer

To remove post-pimple marks on your forehead, you must first identify the type: PIH (Post-Inflammatory Hyperpigmentation) appears as brown or black spots and responds best to melanin inhibitors like Kojic Acid (Kojivit). PIE (Post-Inflammatory Erythema) appears as red or pink marks and requires barrier-repairing agents like Niacinamide or Azelaic Acid (Aziderm). Regardless of the type, fading is impossible without daily broad-spectrum sunscreen, as UV rays instantly re-darken the pigment.

Context: Why Indian Skin Marks Differently

Indian skin is built to produce melanin. When a pimple causes inflammation on the forehead, your skin's defense mechanism overproduces pigment in that specific spot.

  • The Depth: Forehead skin is relatively thin, meaning marks can appear more prominent.
  • The Heat Factor: Urban Indian heat increases blood flow to the face, which can worsen red marks (PIE) and make them more persistent.

    1. Aziderm (Azelaic Acid)

    Available in 10% and 15% gel, Aziderm is the ultimate multi-tasker. It kills acne bacteria while simultaneously blocking the enzymes that cause dark spots.

  • Best For: Red marks and active acne.
  • Why it works: It is an anti-inflammatory that calms the "fire" in the skin.

    • 2. Kojivit / Kojivit Ultra

    Kojivit contains Kojic Acid Dipalmitate, a potent Tyrosinase inhibitor. It stops melanin production at the source.
  • Best For: Old, stubborn brown spots.
  • Application: Use as a targeted spot treatment at night.

    • 3. Glyco-6 (Glycolic Acid)

    A 6% AHA that speeds up cell turnover, "lifting" the pigmented cells to the surface so they can be washed away.

  • Caution: Use only 2x a week to avoid damaging your skin barrier.

    FAQ

    Can I use Vitamin C and Azelaic Acid together?

    Yes. Use Vitamin C in the morning (under sunscreen) to protect the skin, and Azelaic Acid at night to treat the marks.

    How long does it take to see results?

    Pigment fading is a marathon. Expect to see the first visible signs after 4-6 weeks, with full results taking 12 weeks of consistent use.

    Does 'Fairness Cream' work for acne marks?

    Avoid generic fairness creams; they often contain steroids which can thin your skin and cause 'steroid-induced acne'. Stick to clinical inhibitors like Kojic and Azelaic Acid.
