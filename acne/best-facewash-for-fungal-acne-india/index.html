Quick Verdict: Malassezia-Safe Cleansers

Fungal acne (Malassezia folliculitis) is caused by yeast overgrowth fed by lipids (carbon chain lengths C12-C24). Use Ketoconazole 2% wash or Zinc Pyrithione (ZPT) cleansers to clear forehead bumps.

How Anti-Fungal Cleansers Work

  • Ketoconazole 2%: Destroys fungal cell membranes and stops ergosterol synthesis in Malassezia yeast.
  • Zinc Pyrithione (1%): Suppresses yeast proliferation and relieves scalp and forehead itchiness.

Identifying Fungal Acne vs Bacterial Acne

Fungal acne presents as monomorphic (identical size) small itchy bumps along the forehead and hairline, worsened by monsoon humidity and sweat.

Fungal Safety Verification & Precautions

Reviewed by Board-Certified Dermatologists. Avoid plant oils (coconut, argan, olive oil) and fatty acids in leave-on products as they feed Malassezia yeast.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can oil cleansing worsen fungal acne?

Yes! Natural plant oils feed Malassezia yeast. Use Micellar Water or Caprylic Triglyceride cleansers instead.

Is Salicylic Acid fungal safe?

Yes, pure Salicylic Acid is fungal-safe as long as the formulation contains no fatty esters or lipids.
