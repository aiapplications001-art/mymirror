## Quick Answer

To stop forehead acne from hair products, follow the **60-Minute Oiling Rule**: Apply your hair oil (like coconut or amla), let it work for one hour max, and wash it out. Never leave hair oil in overnight if you are acne-prone. To clear existing bumps, use a lipophilic active like **Sebogel (Salicylic Acid)** which can cut through the migrating hair oil and dissolve the clogs in your pores. Always wash your face *after* you wash your hair to ensure no product residue remains on your skin.

<!-- SECTION_ID: S3_context: The Migration Cycle -->
## Context: Why "Natural" Oil Clogs Your Skin

Your forehead has some of the highest densities of oil glands on your body. When you apply heavy traditional oils like **Coconut** or **Mustard oil**, or styling waxes, they don't stay on your hair. They migrate. 

- **The Transfer:** Sweat, gravity, and even your pillowcase help hair products travel from your scalp to your forehead.
- **The Blockage:** These products are often "comedogenic," meaning they physically block the pore opening. This traps sebum and bacteria underneath, leading to painful, under-the-skin bumps.

<!-- SECTION_ID: S4_main_content: The "Safe Champi" Protocol -->
## Main Content: The Hairline Safety Rules

You don't have to choose between hair health and clear skin. It's about clinical timing and product choice.

### 1. Identify the Offenders
Not all hair products are equal. In the Indian market, be wary of:
- **Heavy Oils:** Unrefined Coconut oil, Amla oil, and Castor oil.
- **Styling Waxes:** Any oil-based pomade or wax that feels "sticky" on the skin.
- **Switch To:** Lightweight alternatives like **Argan oil** or **Jojoba oil** for your hair ends.

### 2. The 1-Inch Hairline Gap
When applying any product to your hair, leave a one-inch gap from your actual hairline. Focus the product on the mid-lengths and ends where it is needed most.

### 3. The "Post-Wash" Face Cleanse
This is the most common mistake. Many Indians wash their face *before* their hair wash. Always wash your face **last**. Hair conditioners and oils leave a "film" on your forehead during the rinse. Using a foaming wash like **Saslic DS** after your hair rinse ensures that film is completely dissolved.

### 4. The Overnight Barrier
If you must oil your hair overnight, wear a silk bonnet or a cotton headwrap. This prevents the oil from transferring to your pillowcase, which would otherwise act as a "delivery system" for oil back onto your face as you sleep.

## FAQ

**Does onion hair oil cause acne?**
Yes, if it has a coconut oil base. The base oil is usually what causes the clogging, not the onion extract itself.

**Can I use a hair serum instead of oil?**
Yes. Modern hair serums are often silicone-based and less likely to migrate to your skin and cause clogs compared to heavy traditional oils.

**How do I clear the 'itchy' bumps on my hairline?**
If they are itchy, they might be fungal. Try using an anti-dandruff shampoo as a 5-minute forehead mask once a week.
