# Pre-Writing Strategy & Page Packet
## URL / Slug: /acne/cerave-acne-foaming-cream-cleanser-4-percent-bpo-usa/
## Market: USA

### 1. Persona
Targeting individuals in the USA dealing with mild to moderate acne, who are looking for a scientifically backed, gentle yet effective over-the-counter cleanser. They often suffer from a compromised skin barrier due to harsh acne treatments and seek a formulation that respects the skin barrier (ceramides, niacinamide, hyaluronic acid).

### 2. Search Intent Gaps
- Competitor content often misses the specific pharmacokinetic benefits of a 4% BPO cream wash versus gel washes.
- Lack of deep dives into how ceramides mitigate the transepidermal water loss (TEWL) typically associated with BPO.
- Minimal emphasis on exactly how to integrate this cleanser into a layered active routine without causing irritation.

### 3. Competitor Analysis
- Most articles are superficial reviews focusing merely on texture and price.
- Lack of clinical data citation regarding 4% vs 10% BPO efficacy (where 4% is just as effective for acne but significantly less irritating).
- Missing clear FAQs tailored to patient queries (e.g., bleaching towels, combining with retinoids).

### 4. Clinical Section Blueprint
1. **Introduction & Scientific Overview:** The exact mechanism of 4% Benzoyl Peroxide (BPO) against *C. acnes*.
2. **Barrier Preservation Formulation:** Deep dive into the "3 Essential Ceramides", Hyaluronic Acid, and Niacinamide.
3. **Efficacy vs. Tolerability:** Why 4% is the sweet spot compared to 2.5% or 10%.
4. **Integration Protocol:** How to use this wash alongside other topicals (tretinoin, clindamycin, AHA/BHAs).
5. **Managing Side Effects:** Contact therapy (short contact therapy) vs. standard washing.
6. **Detailed FAQ:** Addressing patient concerns (pregnancy safety, fabrics bleaching, fungal acne).
