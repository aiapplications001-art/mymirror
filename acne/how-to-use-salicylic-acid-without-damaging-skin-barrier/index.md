## Quick Answer

To use Salicylic Acid without irritation, follow the **Barrier Sandwich Method**: Apply a lightweight moisturizer first, followed by a pea-sized amount of your active (like Sebogel), and seal it with another layer of moisturizer. Avoid applying on damp skin, and never start with daily use. For Indian urban skin, choosing pharmacy staples with added Niacinamide can significantly reduce the risk of "purging" or redness.

<!-- SECTION_ID: S3_context -->
## Context: The 'Over-Exfoliation' Trap

In India's humid cities, many of us struggle with oily skin and active breakouts. The common reaction is to reach for the strongest Salicylic Acid available. But here’s the clinical truth: Salicylic Acid is lipophilic, meaning it loves oil and dives deep into your pores. If your skin barrier—the 'invisible shield' of lipids—is already compromised by pollution or harsh cleansers, that deep dive becomes an irritation disaster.

 MASTERING THE BALANCE:
- **Purging:** A temporary increase in breakouts as pores clear.
- **Irritation:** Redness, peeling, and stinging that signals a broken barrier.

## Main Content: The Indian Pharmacy Legends

When it comes to high-efficacy, budget-friendly acne treatment, two names stand out in the Indian market: **Sebogel** and **Saslic DS**.

### 1. Sebogel (The Leave-on Specialist)
Sebogel is a dermatologist-favorite gel containing 2% Salicylic Acid and 6% Nicotinamide (Niacinamide). The inclusion of Niacinamide is critical—it helps soothe the skin while the acid works on the blackheads.
- **Best For:** Targeted application on whiteheads and blackheads.
- **Why it works:** It’s a stable, oil-free formula that doesn't feel heavy in humidity.

### 2. Saslic DS (The Deep-Cleanse Wash)
If your skin is too sensitive for leave-on gels, Saslic DS (Salicylic Acid Foaming Face Wash) is your entry point. It provides 'short-contact therapy', giving the acid enough time to work without staying on the skin all night.

### The 2026 Protocol: The Barrier Sandwich
Mastering these actives requires a specific layering strategy:
1. **Cleanse:** Use a gentle, soap-free cleanser.
2. **Buffer:** Apply a thin layer of a ceramide-based moisturizer (like Re'equil Oil-Free).
3. **Active:** Apply a pea-sized amount of Sebogel only on affected areas.
4. **Seal:** Wait 2 minutes, then apply another thin layer of moisturizer.

## FAQ

**Can I use Salicylic Acid every day?**
No, start 2-3 times a week. Even 'tough' Indian skin needs time to build tolerance.

**Should I apply it on wet skin?**
Never. Wet skin increases penetration, which drastically increases the risk of chemical burns and irritation.

**What if my skin starts peeling?**
Stop all actives immediately. Focus on 'Barrier Repair' (Ceramides and Hyaluronic Acid) for 48 hours until the stinging stops.
