Elevated intracellular DHT binds nuclear androgen receptors within basal sebocytes, triggering sebum hypersecretion and follicular hyperkeratosis. Outpatient clinical dermatologists differentiate inflammatory acne vulgaris from fungal Malassezia folliculitis (Pityrosporum folliculitis) using polarized contact dermoscopy and Wood's lamp examination. Acne vulgaris presents with polymorphic lesions including open blackheads, closed microcomedones, and tender inflammatory nodules distributed irregularly across the mandibular jawline. Conversely, Malassezia folliculitis manifests as uniform monomorphic erythematous 1 to 2 mm follicular papulopustules accompanied by intense pruritus, typically triggered by tropical heat and antibiotic-induced microbial dysbiosis.
