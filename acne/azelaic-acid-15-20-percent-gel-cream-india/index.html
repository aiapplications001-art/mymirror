1. Azelaic Acid 15% vs 20%: Mechanism & Formulation

Azelaic Acid is a naturally occurring dicarboxylic acid produced by Malassezia furfur yeast. At clinical concentrations of 15% and 20%, it selectively targets hyperactive, abnormal melanocytes by inhibiting the enzyme tyrosinase, suppressing melanin overproduction without bleaching normal surrounding skin.

Unlike retinoids or glycolic acid, Azelaic Acid does not increase photosensitivity. It acts as an anti-inflammatory antioxidant that neutralizes reactive oxygen species (ROS) and reduces follicular hyperkeratinization.

Clinical Overview

Understanding how active pharmacological compounds interact with the follicular unit is critical for clearing stubborn acne. Treatment success depends on using targeted active strengths, preventing skin barrier collapse, and supporting cellular repair.

Clinical Considerations for Indian Skin (Fitzpatrick IV–VI)

Melanin-rich skin requires deliberate care when treating active acne. Inflammatory lesions rapidly trigger Post-Inflammatory Hyperpigmentation (PIH) if over-exfoliated or picked. Indian climate factors—such as high humidity during monsoons—increase sebum oxidation and Malassezia yeast activity.

Always pair active spot correctors or retinoids with morning non-comedogenic SPF 50 sunscreen and follow evening treatments with a ceramide barrier moisturizer.

Dermatologist Treatment Protocol

Follow this structured active application schedule to achieve maximum clinical efficacy while minimizing stinging, flaking, or reactive hyperpigmentation:

  1. Cleanse Gently: Wash with a non-stripping cleanser before applying active treatments.
  2. Apply Targeted Active: Use a pea-sized amount or 2-3 drops directly on dry skin.
  3. Buffer & Hydrate: Always follow with a non-comedogenic ceramide moisturizer.
  4. Daily Sun Protection: Broad-spectrum SPF 50+ is non-negotiable every morning.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does 15% and 20% Azelaic Acid treat in India? +

Prescription-strength 15% and 20% Azelaic Acid (like Aziderm 15 or 20) treats mild-to-moderate inflammatory acne, Post-Inflammatory Hyperpigmentation (PIH), rosacea redness, and epidermal melasma by inhibiting tyrosinase and C. acnes bacteria.

Should I choose Azelaic Acid Gel or Cream formulation? +

Gel formulations (Aziderm 15% Gel) penetrate deeper and are ideal for oily, acne-prone skin in humid Indian weather. Cream formulations (Exazel N or Aziderm Cream) are richer and better suited for dry or sensitive skin.

Why does Azelaic Acid cause a tingling or itching sensation at first? +

Azelaic acid lowers skin pH temporarily as it penetrates the stratum corneum. Mild itching or tingling for 10-15 minutes after application is completely normal during the first 2 weeks and subsides as your skin adapts.

Can I use 15% Azelaic Acid with Retinol or Adapalene? +

Yes! Combining Azelaic Acid in the morning with Adapalene at night is one of the most powerful dermatologist protocols for acne and dark marks. It targets both cell turnover and melanin synthesis.

Does Azelaic Acid 15% cause purging? +

Azelaic acid is a mild keratolytic exfoliant, so minor 1-2 week purging can occur if you have deep microcomedones. However, purging is far milder than with Retinoids or 2% BHA.

How long does it take for Aziderm 15% to fade dark marks? +

Initial fading of red post-acne marks (PIE) occurs in 3-4 weeks. Significant fading of deep brown melanin spots (PIH) takes 8 to 12 weeks of daily morning application.

Is Azelaic Acid safe during pregnancy in India? +

Yes! Azelaic Acid is rated Category B by dermatologists and is considered one of the safest active topical treatments for pregnancy acne and melasma.

Do I need a prescription for Aziderm 15% or 20% in India? +

Concentrations of 15% and 20% are Schedule H prescription dermatological medications in India. Always consult a dermatologist before starting high-strength Azelaic Acid.
