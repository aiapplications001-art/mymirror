Medical Dual-Action

What Makes Klenzit CMS Gel So Effective?

Manufactured by Glenmark Pharmaceuticals, Klenzit CMS pairs two distinct gold-standard acne medications into one water-based gel base.

🔬

Adapalene 0.1% (3rd-Gen Retinoid)

A synthetic retinoid that normalizes how skin cells shed inside your hair follicles. It prevents dead skin cells from sticking together and forming micro-comedones (the root of all pimples and blackheads).

Cell turnover & unclogging
🛡️

Clindamycin Phosphate 1% (Antibiotic)

A broad-spectrum lincosamide antibiotic that targets Cutibacterium acnes bacteria multiplying inside blocked pores. It halts bacterial protein synthesis, shrinking red, inflamed pimples quickly.

Anti-inflammatory & antibacterial
💧

Aqueous Gel Formulation

Unlike heavy cream bases that can feel sticky in hot Indian weather, Klenzit CMS is built in a lightweight, non-comedogenic water gel that absorbs rapidly into oily and acne-prone skin.

Zero pore clogging

How Adapalene Clears Clogged Pores from Within

Normal skin cells shed every 28 days. In acne-prone individuals, cells stick together to form a plug. Adapalene loosens the cellular glue, expelling micro-comedones before they can inflame into cystic acne.

Symptom Checker

Is Your Skin Purging, Peeling, or Burning?

Most patients panic when their skin turns red or starts flaking. Here is how to distinguish between healthy retinoid adjustment and dangerous skin barrier damage.

Sign Normal Purging (Expected) Retinoid Peeling (Needs Buffer) Barrier Damage (Stop Gel)
What you see Small pimples in places where you usually break out. Fine white flaking around mouth, chin, and nose creases. Bright red patches, tight plastic shine, raw skin.
How it feels Normal mild tenderness on active pimples. Dryness, slight tightness, powdery texture under makeup. Severe burning when applying plain water or gentle lotion.
Why it happens Dormant clogged pores surfacing weeks faster. Moisture evaporating faster than skin can replenish lipids. Acid mantle completely stripped by applying too much gel.
Action plan Stay patient. Continue using the gel on schedule. Switch to the sandwich method. Add hydrating gel. Stop Klenzit CMS for 4 days. Apply pure barrier cream only.

The Science of Barrier Damage from Direct Retinoid Application

Applying strong retinoid gels directly onto unmoisturised skin strips natural ceramides and cholesterol. Water escapes (transepidermal water loss), leaving nerve endings exposed to burning and itching.

Expectation Timeline

The 12-Week Klenzit CMS Journey: What to Expect

Understanding the timeline prevents you from quitting right before your skin clears up.

1
Weeks 1 to 2: The Acclimatization Phase
🌱 Building Tolerance

Your skin is introduced to cell-turnover stimulation. Clindamycin immediately starts shrinking large red pimples, but you may notice mild tightness or slight peeling around your mouth. Apply only 2 nights per week.

Expected feeling: Mild dryness, slight flaking, reduced swelling on pus bumps.
Routine rule: Always use the sandwich method. Do not apply on consecutive nights.
2
Weeks 3 to 4: The Purge Window
⚡ Pores Expelling Debris

Micro-comedones hidden deep beneath your skin are accelerated to the surface. You may see small whiteheads or papules appear in your normal breakout zones. These pimples resolve much faster than traditional cysts.

Expected feeling: Temporary spike in small pimples. Do not panic or squeeze them.
Routine rule: Increase frequency to every other night if there is zero burning.
3
Weeks 5 to 8: The Turning Point
✨ Calming & Smoothing

New breakouts slow down significantly. Existing inflammation flattens out, and skin texture feels noticeable smoother under your fingers. Flaking and redness reduce as your skin barrier adapts.

Expected feeling: Fewer active pimples, reduced oiliness throughout the day.
Routine rule: Begin fading flat red post-acne marks (PIE) with gentle niacinamide.
4
Weeks 9 to 12: Clearance & Antibiotic Transition
🎯 Long-Term Maintenance

Your skin reaches consistent clarity. Because Clindamycin is an antibiotic, consult your dermatologist about tapering off Klenzit CMS to prevent bacterial resistance, transitioning to plain Adapalene for maintenance.

Expected feeling: Clear pores, reduced blackheads, resilient skin barrier.
Routine rule: Never stop sunscreen. Maintain daily barrier protection.
Derm Technique

The Sandwich Method: How to Apply Klenzit CMS Safely

The sandwich technique buffers active retinoid penetration without reducing its clinical acne-clearing power.

Moisturiser Buffer Layering

By placing Klenzit CMS between two light layers of moisturiser, you prevent high-concentration hot spots on sensitive facial curves while allowing the medication to gently diffuse into hair follicles.

1
Cleanse & Wait 10 Minutes
Dry Skin Rule

Wash face with a gentle, non-foaming cleanser like Cetaphil. Pat dry and wait 10 full minutes. Applying retinoid gels on damp skin opens pores too wide and causes burning.

2
First Layer of Light Moisturiser
Bottom Slice

Smooth a dime-sized amount of an oil-free, water-based gel moisturiser across your face. This creates a protective lipid film that softens the initial penetration of Adapalene.

3
One Pea-Sized Dot of Klenzit CMS
The Active Layer

Squeeze exactly one pea-sized drop onto your fingertip. Dab dots onto your forehead, chin, and cheeks. Gently blend outwards. Stay 1 centimeter away from your eyes, nose creases, and lips.

4
Second Layer of Moisturiser
Top Slice

Wait 2 to 3 minutes for the gel to settle. Apply a second light layer of moisturiser over the top to seal in hydration and prevent overnight trans-epidermal water loss.

Daily Schedule

The Complete AM & PM Skincare Routine Matrix

Klenzit CMS works best when surrounded by a supportive, non-irritating routine. Here is your daily morning and night schedule.

Morning Defense & Night Recovery

Morning focuses on hydration and broad-spectrum sun defense to protect delicate, renewing skin. Night focuses on gentle cleansing and targeted treatment.

🌅 Morning Protocol (Protection)

  1. Step 1: Rinse with lukewarm water or a gentle lotion cleanser.
  2. Step 2: Apply a hydrating, oil-free gel moisturiser.
  3. Step 3: Apply generous broad-spectrum SPF 50 sunscreen. Reapply if outdoors.

🌙 Night Protocol (Treatment)

  1. Step 1: Wash face thoroughly to remove sunscreen and dust. Pat completely dry.
  2. Step 2: Apply thin layer of light moisturiser (Wait 5 minutes).
  3. Step 3: Apply one pea of Klenzit CMS on acne-prone areas.
  4. Step 4: Top with second layer of barrier moisturiser. Sleep on clean cotton.
Active Clash Prevention

Ingredients You Must NOT Mix with Klenzit CMS

Layering conflicting active ingredients is the fastest way to get severe contact dermatitis and peeling.

🛑 Never Use in the Same Routine:

1. Salicylic or Glycolic Acid (BHA/AHA): Dual exfoliation causes intense burning, raw redness, and chemical flaking.
2. Pure Vitamin C (L-Ascorbic Acid): Low pH acid causes stinging and destabilizes the skin barrier when paired with retinoids.
3. Physical Walnut or Apricot Scrubs: Scrubbing already retinized skin creates micro-tears and triggers post-acne dark marks.
4. Heavy Mineral Oil / Cold Creams: Heavy petroleum creams trap the medication too aggressively, leading to hot spots.

Clinical Responsibility

The 12-Week Antibiotic Rule: Why You Must Taper Off

Understanding why Klenzit CMS is a treatment course, not a lifelong face cream.

⚠️ Preventing Antibiotic Resistance

The Clindamycin in Klenzit CMS is a topical antibiotic. When used continuously for longer than 12 weeks, acne-causing bacteria can mutate and develop resistance, making the medication stop working completely. Dermatologists recommend using Klenzit CMS for 8 to 12 weeks to clear active lesions, then switching to standalone Adapalene (like Adaferin) or Azelaic Acid for long-term acne prevention.

First Aid

Already Have Redness & Burning? Here Is Your 3-Day Reset

If your skin is currently stinging even when washing with water, your barrier is compromised. Follow this 3-day emergency reset.

🛑

Day 1: Complete Stop

Stop applying Klenzit CMS immediately. Wash face with cool tap water only. Do not use warm water, face wipes, or foaming cleansers.

🧴

Day 2: Intensive Soothing

Apply pure aloe vera gel or a ceramide barrier balm (Bioderma Atoderm or Cica gel) 3 times a day. Keep the skin constantly hydrated.

🛡️

Day 3: Re-Evaluation

Once burning and tightness disappear completely, wait 2 additional days before re-introducing Klenzit CMS using the sandwich method twice weekly.

Frequently Asked

Common Questions About Klenzit CMS Gel

How long does Klenzit CMS take to show visible results?+
The Clindamycin component begins reducing bacterial inflammation and swelling within 7 to 10 days. However, the Adapalene component takes 8 to 12 weeks of consistent use to fully regulate skin cell turnover and prevent new blackheads and cysts from forming.
Can I use Klenzit CMS gel every night right away?+
No. Jumping straight into daily night use is the most common reason patients experience severe peeling and redness. Start by applying it just 2 nights a week for the first 2 weeks. Gradually increase to every other night once your skin builds tolerance.
Can I apply Klenzit CMS during the daytime?+
No. Klenzit CMS should only be applied at night. While Adapalene is photostable compared to older retinoids, sunlight degrades open follicles and drastically increases the risk of sunburn and post-inflammatory dark marks on sensitive, renewing skin.
Why is my skin burning when I apply plain moisturiser?+
Stinging when applying basic moisturiser is the classic sign of a damaged moisture barrier (over-retinization). If this happens, stop using Klenzit CMS for 3 to 5 days, focus purely on gentle hydration, and restart using the sandwich method once stinging stops.
How long should I continue using Klenzit CMS?+
Topical antibiotics like Clindamycin should generally not be used continuously for more than 12 weeks to prevent bacterial resistance. Dermatologists usually taper patients off Clindamycin after 8 to 12 weeks and transition them to a standalone retinoid (such as plain Adapalene 0.1%) for long-term maintenance.
Can Klenzit CMS clear dark spots and acne marks?+
Adapalene accelerates epidermal turnover, which helps fade flat brown marks (post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation) over 3 to 6 months. However, Klenzit CMS cannot fill pitted or indented textural scars (ice-pick or boxcar scars), which require in-clinic dermatological procedures.
