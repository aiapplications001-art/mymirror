How Chemical Peels Remodel Acne Scars

Chemical peeling is a controlled clinical procedure where dermatologists apply chemical exfoliants to resurface damaged skin layers. By removing epidermal or dermal layers in a uniform, controlled manner, chemical peels stimulate cell turnover, shed stubborn melanin deposits, and trigger **neocollagenesis** (new collagen synthesis) to smooth out acne scars.

3
Pre-Peel Priming for Indian Fitzpatrick IV–VI Skin

Because melanin-rich Indian skin contains larger, hyper-reactive melanosomes, undergoing a medium-depth chemical peel without preparation can trigger **post-peel paradoxical hyperpigmentation**.

🛑 Mandatory 2–4 Week Priming Protocol: Indian patients undergoing TCA or combination peels must use a daily tyrosinase inhibitor (Azelaic acid 10–15%, Kojic acid, or Tretinoin 0.025%) alongside broad-spectrum SPF 50 for at least 2 to 4 weeks prior to the peel procedure to "quiet" melanocytes.

Post-Peel Recovery Timeline
Day 1 ❄️

Frosting & Tightness

Immediate white "frosting" (protein denaturation) settles into mild erythema and a sensation of skin tightness.

Days 2–5 🍂

Darkening & Crust Formation

Treated areas darken and form a thin leathery crust. For TCA CROSS, tiny micro-scabs form over ice-pick points.

Days 5–10 🐍

Sheet Peeling (Do NOT Pick)

Old skin peels off in sheets. It is critical to let scabs fall off naturally — picking causes permanent pitted scarring.

Month 1–3 ✨

Neocollagenesis Results

New collagen remodels the dermal matrix under the fresh pink skin, filling shallow scar depressions over 90 days.

5
Sessions Required & Cost in India

Acne scar remodeling is a progressive process. A single peel session will clarify skin tone, but **3 to 6 sessions spaced 3 to 4 weeks apart** are required for structural pitted scar elevation.

  • Superficial Peels (Glycolic / Salicylic): ₹1,500 – ₹3,500 per session.
  • Medium-Depth / TCA CROSS: ₹3,500 – ₹8,000 per session.
  • Clinical Combination Peels (Metro Clinics): ₹8,000 – ₹12,000 per session.
6
Expert FAQ: Chemical Peels
How many chemical peel sessions are needed for acne scars?+
Most dermatologists recommend 3 to 6 sessions spaced 3 to 4 weeks apart to achieve visible remodeling of superficial pitted scars and flat hyperpigmentation.
Is chemical peel safe for dark Indian skin (Fitzpatrick IV-VI)?+
Yes, superficial to medium peels (Glycolic, Salicylic, Yellow Peel, TCA CROSS) are safe when preceded by 2-4 weeks of skin priming with tyrosinase inhibitors (Azelaic acid/Kojic acid) and strict SPF 50. Deep Phenol peels are strictly contraindicated on Indian skin.
What is TCA CROSS and how does it treat ice-pick scars?+
TCA CROSS (Chemical Reconstruction of Skin Scars) uses high-concentration 50-100% Trichloroacetic Acid applied precisely inside deep ice-pick scar channels with a toothpick or fine probe. It induces local protein denaturation (frosting) which triggers intense localized collagen synthesis to fill the scar crater.
How much does a chemical peel cost in India?+
Superficial Glycolic or Salicylic peels cost ₹1,500 – ₹3,500 per session. Medium-depth peels or TCA CROSS cost ₹3,500 – ₹8,000 per session, while premium multi-layer clinical peels cost up to ₹12,000 per session.

📋 Quick Summary

  • Ice-Pick Scars → TCA CROSS (50–100%)
  • Flat Dark Spots → Glycolic / Salicylic
  • Priming → Mandatory 2–4 Weeks
  • Phenol Peels → Strictly Avoid
