Spearmint Tea For Hormonal Acne in India

Struggling with painful jawline pimples before your period or PCOS breakouts? Learn how drinking 2 cups of spearmint tea daily can naturally inhibit free testosterone and clear hormonal acne.

1. The Science: How Spearmint Tea Reduces Androgens

Hormonal acne around the chin, jawline, and neck is driven by elevated levels of circulating free testosterone and dihydrotestosterone (DHT). High androgens trigger overproduction of thick sebum in facial oil glands, leading to deep cystic nodules.

Clinical trials published in Phytotherapy Research demonstrated that drinking organic Spearmint Tea (Mentha spicata) twice daily significantly reduces serum free testosterone levels while increasing luteinizing hormone (LH) and follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH).

2. Spearmint Tea & PCOS Breakouts in Indian Women

Key Benefits For PCOS Acne:

  • Natural alternative or complement to Spironolactone (under medical supervision).
  • Helps decrease hirsutism (excess facial hair growth on chin and upper lip).
  • Reduces pre-menstrual acne flare-ups in the luteal phase (7-10 days before periods).

3. Recommended Dosage & How To Brew

  • Dosage: 2 cups per day (approx. 5 grams of loose spearmint leaves or 2 tea bags).
  • Brewing Method: Steep loose organic spearmint leaves in 200ml hot water (85°C-90°C) for 7-10 minutes covered.
  • Peppermint Warning: Do not substitute Spearmint with Peppermint (Mentha piperita). Peppermint contains high menthol and does not have proven anti-androgen effects.

4. Expected Results Timeline

Weeks 1 - 2: Mild reduction in sebum shininess across T-zone.

Weeks 4 - 6: Fewer new deep cystic pimples forming along the jawline before periods.

Weeks 8 - 12: Significant reduction in hormonal acne frequency and improved overall skin clarity.

5. Precautions & Medical Considerations

While spearmint tea is natural, consult a doctor before use if you:

  • Are pregnant or actively trying to conceive (androgen suppression should be avoided).
  • Suffer from severe Acid Reflux (GERD) or kidney/liver disorders.
  • Are currently taking prescription hormonal contraceptives or anti-androgen medications.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Can men drink spearmint tea for acne?

Men should exercise caution as spearmint tea reduces free testosterone levels, which may impact muscle recovery and libido in males.

Can I take spearmint capsules instead of tea?

Spearmint capsules exist, but organic brewed tea ensures standardized bioavailability and avoids concentrated extract liver toxicity risks.
