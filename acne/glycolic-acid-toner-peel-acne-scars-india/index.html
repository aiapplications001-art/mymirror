1. How Glycolic Acid Resurfaces Textured Acne Scars

Glycolic Acid is an Alpha Hydroxy Acid (AHA) with the smallest molecular weight (76.05 Da) among all hydroxy acids. This tiny molecular size allows it to penetrate deep into the upper dermal layers, cleaving desmosomal bonds that hold dead skin cells together.

Clinical Overview

Understanding how active pharmacological compounds interact with the follicular unit is critical for clearing stubborn acne. Treatment success depends on using targeted active strengths, preventing skin barrier collapse, and supporting cellular repair.

Clinical Considerations for Indian Skin (Fitzpatrick IV–VI)

Melanin-rich skin requires deliberate care when treating active acne. Inflammatory lesions rapidly trigger Post-Inflammatory Hyperpigmentation (PIH) if over-exfoliated or picked. Indian climate factors—such as high humidity during monsoons—increase sebum oxidation and Malassezia yeast activity.

Always pair active spot correctors or retinoids with morning non-comedogenic SPF 50 sunscreen and follow evening treatments with a ceramide barrier moisturizer.

Dermatologist Treatment Protocol

Follow this structured active application schedule to achieve maximum clinical efficacy while minimizing stinging, flaking, or reactive hyperpigmentation:

  1. Cleanse Gently: Wash with a non-stripping cleanser before applying active treatments.
  2. Apply Targeted Active: Use a pea-sized amount or 2-3 drops directly on dry skin.
  3. Buffer & Hydrate: Always follow with a non-comedogenic ceramide moisturizer.
  4. Daily Sun Protection: Broad-spectrum SPF 50+ is non-negotiable every morning.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Glyco 6 and Glyco 12 in India? +

Glyco 6 contains 6% Glycolic Acid (AHA) for gentle surface cell turnover, beginner exfoliation, and mild dark marks. Glyco 12 contains 12% Glycolic Acid for advanced resurfacing of stubborn texture and shallow acne scars.

Is Glycolic Acid safe for dark Indian skin (Fitzpatrick IV-VI)? +

Glycolic acid has the smallest molecular size of all AHAs, meaning it penetrates quickly. High concentrations (above 10%) can trigger reactive melanin production (PIH) if overused. Always start with 6% 2-3 nights a week and wear SPF 50.

How often should I apply Glyco 6 cream? +

Begin by applying Glyco 6 cream 2 nights a week at night. If no redness or peeling occurs after 2 weeks, increase to every other night. Always follow with a hydrating ceramide moisturizer.

Can Glycolic Acid remove deep pitted boxcar or icepick scars? +

Glycolic acid smooths rough surface texture and shallow rolling scars. However, deep icepick or boxcar scars require clinical procedures like microneedling, TCA cross, or CO2 fractional laser.

Should I use Glycolic Acid or Salicylic Acid for acne? +

Salicylic Acid (BHA) is oil-soluble and penetrates deep inside pores to clear blackheads and active pimples. Glycolic Acid (AHA) is water-soluble and works on the surface to fade dark spots and smooth rough skin texture.

Can I mix Glycolic Acid with Retinol or Tretinoin? +

Never apply Glycolic Acid and Retinol/Tretinoin on the same night. Doing so will strip your skin barrier and cause chemical burns. Alternate nights or use a 4-night skin cycling schedule.

Why does my skin look darker after using Glycolic Acid? +

If you skip sunscreen or over-exfoliate, UV rays easily penetrate freshly revealed skin cells, triggering dark melanin pigmentation. Morning sunscreen is mandatory when using AHAs.

What is the best way to use a 25% AHA + 2% BHA chemical peel in India? +

Use at-home AHA/BHA chemical peels (like Minimalist 10% AHA or Ordinary 30% AHA) once every 2 weeks maximum. Leave on clean dry skin for no more than 10 minutes, rinse with cold water, and apply Cica barrier balm.
