Surface vs. Deep Dermis

If you are looking at your reflection and seeing "pits," "craters," or "uneven shadows" left behind by old acne, you are likely deciding between two of the most popular clinical procedures in India: Microneedling and Chemical Peels.

While both treatments aim to "resurface" the skin, they operate on entirely different biological levels:

  • 🎯 Chemical Peels work from the outside-in. They use specialized acids to "unglue" the top layers of dead skin.
  • 🎯 Microneedling works from the inside-out. It creates "micro-injuries" in the deep dermis to trigger a massive surge of new collagen.
2

Microneedling (PRP/MNRF)

Microneedling, or Collagen Induction Therapy, has become the gold standard for treating deep, pitted scars in India.

The Safety Hero

MNRF Technology

Microneedling Radiofrequency delivers heat deep into the dermis. This "shrinks" the scar floor and is 40% more effective for deep Boxcar scars. Safest for brown skin tones.

Healing Booster

GFC / PRP

Using your own blood’s growth factors (GFC) accelerated healing and collagen production, essential for recovery in India's humid climate.

Best For: Rolling scars, deep Boxcar scars, and overall textural irregularities.

3

Chemical Peels (TCA/Glycolic)

Chemical peels are "controlled exfoliation." They are excellent for smoothing out shallow texture and erasing the dark marks (PIH) common in Indian skin.

The Hole Closer

TCA CROSS

High-strength acid is "dotted" directly into the pits of Ice-pick scars. This is the only topical-style treatment that can close these narrow "holes."

Surface Polisher

Glycolic Peels

Safely dissolves the "glue" of dead skin. Best for shallow boxcar scars and post-acne dark marks. Requires SPF 50+ post-care.

Best For: Ice-pick scars (via TCA CROSS), shallow boxcar scars, and PIH marks.

The Melanin Risk

Safe for Brown Skin

Why Microneedling Wins

Because the needles bypass the epidermis (where pigment lives), there is a much lower risk of causing dark spots. MNRF is the "Gold Standard" for Indian skin.

Proceed with Caution

Peel Limitations

Peels must pass through the pigment layer. If the acid is too strong, it can trigger "rebound pigmentation" in melanin-rich skin.

Expert FAQ

Can I do microneedling at home? +

No. Home derma rollers often use poor-quality needles that "tear" the skin, leading to more scarring. For Indian skin, the risk of infection and PIH from DIY needling is too high.

How soon will I see results? +

True structural lifting from microneedling takes 3 to 6 months to become fully visible as collagen builds. Peels show surface "glow" in 7 days.
