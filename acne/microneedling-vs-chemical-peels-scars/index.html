Surface vs. Deep Dermis

If you are looking at your reflection and seeing "pits," "craters," or "uneven shadows" left behind by old acne, you are likely deciding between two of the most popular clinical procedures in India: Microneedling and Chemical Peels.

While both treatments aim to "resurface" the skin, they operate on entirely different biological levels.

2

Microneedling (PRP/MNRF)

Microneedling, or Collagen Induction Therapy, has become the gold standard for treating deep, pitted scars in India.

Safety Hero

MNRF Technology

Delivers heat deep into the dermis. This "shrinks" the scar floor and is 40% more effective for deep Boxcar scars. Safest for melanin-rich skin tones.

Healing Booster

GFC / PRP

Using your own blood’s growth factors accelerated healing and collagen production, essential for recovery in India's humid climate.

3

Chemical Peels (TCA/Glycolic)

Chemical peels are "controlled exfoliation." They are excellent for smoothing out shallow texture and erasing the dark marks (PIH) common in Indian skin.

Hole Closer

TCA CROSS

High-strength acid is "dotted" directly into the pits of Ice-pick scars. This is the only topical-style treatment that can close these narrow "holes."

Surface Polish

Glycolic Peels

Safely dissolves the "glue" of dead skin. Best for shallow boxcar scars and post-acne dark marks. Requires strict SPF 50+ post-care.
