The Science: Ceramides & Acne-Prone Skin

Your skin barrier is composed of cells held together by a lipid matrix consisting of **ceramides, cholesterol, and free fatty acids**. When you over-exfoliate or use drying actives (salicylic acid, benzoyl peroxide, retinoids), this barrier becomes damaged. A compromised barrier allows moisture to escape (TEWL) and irritants to enter — triggering inflammation and sebum overproduction that leads to worse acne.

✅ The Ceramide Rule: Ceramides act as the mortar that repairs this barrier matrix. By introducing non-comedogenic ceramides, you calm active inflammation, accelerate healing of popped pimples, and enable your skin to tolerate strong acne actives.

How to Choose: Avoid Comedogenic Bases

When shopping for a ceramide moisturiser, the ingredients used to create the *base* of the product are what dictate pore clogging. Avoid moisturisers containing these heavy comedogenic elements:

  • Isopropyl Myristate / Isopropyl Palmitate: Highly comedogenic esters used to make creams glide easily.
  • Heavy Shea Butter / Coconut Oil: Excellent for dry body skin, but will clog facial pores on oily skin.
  • Heavy Mineral Oil / Petrolatum bases: Creates an occlusive seal that traps sweat and sebum, triggering breakouts in humid climates.
💡 Shopping Rule: Look for terms like **"Gel Cream"**, **"Lotion"**, or **"Fluid"** rather than "Balm" or "Thick Cream" if you have active acne.

Expert FAQ: Ceramide Moisturisers
Can ceramides clog pores on acne-prone skin?+
Ceramides themselves do not clog pores — they are lipids naturally present in your skin barrier. However, the formulation base (heavy creams containing mineral oil, isopropyl myristate, or shea butter) can be comedogenic. Look for gel-creams or fluid lotions explicitly marked non-comedogenic to ensure pore clarity.
Why is a ceramide moisturiser essential when using tretinoin?+
Tretinoin speeds up cell turnover, temporarily thinning the outer stratum corneum and breaking down the skin's lipid barrier, leading to peeling, dryness, and purging irritation. Ceramides act as the mortar between skin cells, repairing this damaged lipid matrix and significantly reducing tretinoin side effects.
Should I apply ceramide moisturiser on wet or dry skin?+
Apply ceramide moisturiser on damp skin to lock in maximum hydration. However, if you are layering a retinoid (like tretinoin or adapalene) on top, wait 10–15 minutes until the moisturiser layer is completely dry before applying the active.
