## Quick Answer

The choice between the two depends on your acne type: Use **Salicylic Acid** (like **Saslic DS** or **Sebogel**) if you have oily skin, blackheads, and tiny "bumpy" texture. Use **Benzoyl Peroxide** (like **Benzac AC**) if you have red, painful, or pus-filled inflammatory pimples. For the Indian climate, we recommend the **Sandwich Method** for both actives—layering them between moisturizer to prevent the aggressive dryness caused by urban pollution and heat.

<!-- SECTION_ID: S3_context: The Chemistry of Clear Skin -->
## Context: How They Work (The Clinical Truth)

Acne in India's humid metros is often a combination of excess sebum (oil) and bacterial growth. 

- **Salicylic Acid (The Drain Cleaner):** It is oil-soluble (lipophilic). It dives deep into the pore to dissolve the "glue" that holds oil and dead skin together. It effectively "cleans the pipes."
- **Benzoyl Peroxide (The Oxygen Bomb):** It introduces oxygen into the pore. Since *C. acnes* bacteria cannot survive in oxygen-rich environments, BP kills them on contact. It acts as the "emergency brake" for active infections.

## Main Content: The Legend Comparison

When shopping for these ingredients, Indian pharmacy brands offer the most stable and affordable formulations.

### 1. The Salicylic Acid Routine (BHA)
- **Best For:** Blackheads, whiteheads, and "Maskne."
- **Saslic DS (Foaming Wash):** A 2% SA wash. Perfect for "short-contact therapy"—massage for 60 seconds and rinse to get the benefits without the peeling.
- **Sebogel (Targeted Gel):** Contains 2% SA and 6% Niacinamide. The Niacinamide is a "hero" addition for Indian skin as it helps fade post-acne marks (PIH).

### 2. The Benzoyl Peroxide Routine (BP)
- **Best For:** Red, angry pimples and "blind" bumps.
- **Benzac AC (Gel):** Available in 2.5% and 5%. **Always start with 2.5%.** Research shows 2.5% is just as effective as 5% but cause 50% less irritation.
- **Perobar (Soap):** A convenient 2.5% BP wash, ideal for body acne (back and chest).

### THE BP WARNING:
Benzoyl Peroxide **will bleach** your clothes, towels, and pillowcases. When using Benzac AC or Perobar, stick to white linens to avoid orange stains.

## FAQ

**Can I use Salicylic Acid and Benzoyl Peroxide together?**
Not in the same step. It’s too drying for most Indian skin. Alternate them (SA in AM, BP in PM) once your skin builds tolerance.

**Why is my skin peeling after using Benzac?**
You are likely using too much or applying it on wet skin. Only use a pea-sized amount for the entire face and apply it *only* after your skin is completely dry.

**Which one is better for "Teenage Acne"?**
Generally, **Salicylic Acid** is a better starting point for teenagers as it focuses on the oil control needed during puberty.
