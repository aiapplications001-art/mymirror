- **The Popcorn Kernel (Purge Whitehead vs Subterranean Potato):**
  - A real purging bump is like a microwave popcorn kernel: it pops up fast near the surface, stays small, has a tiny white tip, and dries out naturally in 48 to 72 hours without deep throbbing pain. - **The Speedometer vs Engine Fire (Acid Frequency):**
  - Salicylic acid is an accelerator pedal for skin cell turnover. Pressing the pedal to 2–3 nights a week gets your pores clear smoothly. Stomping the pedal twice every day does not speed up the purge—it blows your skin's protective moisture gasket, causing an angry engine fire (barrier burn, peeling, and dark PIH spots).
