The Pathogen Difference: Malassezia vs C. acnes

Fungal acne is technically not true acne — it is **Malassezia Folliculitis**, an infection of the hair follicle caused by an overgrowth of *Malassezia* yeast. In humid Indian weather, feeding this yeast with heavy botanical oils or fatty acids causes explosive outbreaks of itchy bumps that are immune to antibacterial acne creams.

Key Symptoms of Fungal Acne
Tell-Tale Sign 🔍

The Forehead & Hairline Pattern

Clusters of identical small red or skin-colored bumps along the forehead and hairline that appear suddenly after heavy sweating, workouts, or coconut oil hair oiling.

Yeast Feeder 🪔

Hair Oil & Fatty Acid Triggers

Malassezia yeast feeds on fatty acids with carbon chain lengths C11–C24 (found in coconut oil, olive oil, polysorbates, and heavy creams). Avoid heavy oiling!

4
The Ketoconazole 2% Wash Routine

The most effective short-contact treatment for fungal acne is **Ketoconazole 2% shampoo or soap** (Nizoral, Scalpe, or Ketanov):

  1. Lather a coin-sized amount of Ketoconazole 2% wash across the affected forehead, chest, or back.
  2. Allow the foam to sit undisturbed for **3 to 5 minutes** so the antifungal agent can break down yeast cell membranes.
  3. Rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water. Repeat 3 times a week for 2 weeks.
5
Fungal-Safe Skincare Essentials

To prevent fungal recurrence, switch to oil-free, fungal-safe light gels containing Hyaluronic acid, Niacinamide, or Squalane (100% pure plant squalane is C30 and cannot feed yeast).

Expert FAQ: Fungal Acne
How do I know if my acne is fungal or bacterial?+
Fungal acne (Malassezia folliculitis) presents as monomorphic (uniform sized), intensely itchy small bumps along the forehead, hairline, chest, and back that flare in humid weather. Regular bacterial acne features varied sizes of non-itchy blackheads, whiteheads, and pus-filled pimples.
Does Salicylic acid work on fungal acne?+
Salicylic acid helps unclog pores but will NOT eliminate Malassezia yeast overgrowth. You must use an antifungal active like Ketoconazole 2% (Nizoral / Ketanov) or Zinc Pyrithione to eradicate fungal folliculitis.
How do I use Ketoconazole 2% shampoo as a face wash for fungal acne?+
Apply a coin-sized amount of Ketoconazole 2% shampoo (Nizoral/Scalpe) to damp affected skin, leave the lather on for 3 to 5 minutes, and rinse thoroughly 2 to 3 times a week.
