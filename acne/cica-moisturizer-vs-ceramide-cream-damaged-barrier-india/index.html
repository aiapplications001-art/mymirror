1. Clinical Overview & Background

Over-exfoliating with active acids or retinoids strips the skin's protective lipid barrier. This leads to transepidermal water loss (TEWL), stinging when applying plain water, and red breakouts.

2. Quick Verdict: Which Active Should You Pick?

Clinical Active Recommendation

Choose a Cica Moisturizer (Centella Asiatica / Madecassoside) if your main symptom is acute stinging, burning, or active red inflammation. Choose a Ceramide Cream (Ceramides 1, 3, 6-II in a 3:1:1 lipid ratio) if your main symptom is chronic tightness, flaking, or structural barrier damage from acid overuse.

3. Dermatological Action Mechanisms & Science

Madecassoside and Asiaticoside in Cica suppress inflammatory cytokines (TNF-alpha, IL-1beta) to calm burning. Ceramides rebuild the intercellular lipid matrix (50% of stratum corneum lipids), physically locking moisture into the skin barrier.

Centella Asiatica Anti-Inflammatory

Contains Madecassoside & Asiaticoside. Calms cytokine signals, relieving stinging and redness in minutes.

3:1:1 Lipid Ratio Barrier Reconstruction

Ceramides constitute 50% of skin lipids. Pairing Ceramides with cholesterol and fatty acids restores barrier integrity.

4. Decision Criteria for Indian Skin Types

For severe barrier damage, combine both: apply a light Cica gel first to soothe stinging, followed by a Ceramide cream to seal in lipid reconstruction over 14 to 21 days.
