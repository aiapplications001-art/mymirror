Emulsification Rule

Always emulsify cleansing oils with lukewarm water for 15 seconds before rinsing. Rinsing without emulsifying leaves hydrophobic traces that trap dead corneocytes in hair follicles.

Dry Skin Rule

Apply cleansing oil onto completely dry palms and dry facial skin. Water applied too early blocks lipophilic active dissolution of water-resistant sunscreen polymers.
