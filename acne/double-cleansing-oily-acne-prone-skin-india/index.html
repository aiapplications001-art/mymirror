The Science: Like Dissolves Like

Most modern sunscreens (especially physical/mineral SPFs) and makeup are formulated in silicone or oil bases designed to be **water-resistant**. Standard water-based face washes cannot fully break down these filters. Over time, sunscreen residues accumulate inside the follicle, binding with dead skin cells to create blackheads and whiteheads. Double cleansing uses a lipid-based first cleanser to dissolve these oil-soluble impurities, followed by a gentle second wash to clean the skin surface.

✅ The Emulsification Key: A true cleansing oil contains **emulsifiers**. When you splash water on your face after massaging the oil, it emulsifies (turns milky-white) and rinses off completely. This ensures zero pore-clogging film is left behind.

Double Cleansing Step-by-Step PM Protocol

This protocol ensures complete removal of residues without triggering irritation or barrier breakdown:

1
Apply First Cleanser to Dry Face Take 2–3 pumps of cleansing oil or a dime-sized balm. Massage onto completely **dry skin** for 45–60 seconds, focusing on nose, chin, and areas with heavy sunscreen or makeup.
2
Emulsify with Warm Water Wet your hands and continue massaging. The oil will turn into a **milky-white emulsion**. Massage for another 15 seconds to ensure all impurities are detached from the skin.
3
Rinse Fully Splash your face with lukewarm water until the milky film is completely gone.
4
Follow with Second Cleanser While skin is wet, apply your regular gentle, water-based face wash (e.g. Cetaphil). Lather for 30 seconds to clean the surface, then rinse dry. Follow immediately with moisturiser.

Expert FAQ: Double Cleansing
Will double cleansing oil break out my skin?+
Cleansing oils do not cause breakouts if they are formulated with emulsifiers. Emulsifiers allow the oil to bond with water, milky-emulsifying and rinsing off completely without leaving a greasy film. Clogging only occurs if you use plain, raw oils (like coconut or olive oil) or fail to follow up with a water-based second cleanser.
Do I need to double cleanse in the morning?+
No. Double cleansing is an evening-only step. Its sole purpose is to dissolve water-resistant mineral sunscreens, heavy makeup, and accumulated sebum from the day. A single, gentle water-based wash is sufficient in the morning.
Can I double cleanse if I have active cystic acne?+
Yes, but be very gentle. Do not scrub or rub vigorously. Use a lightweight cleansing oil or micellar water with minimal friction. Double cleansing helps keep surrounding skin healthy and prevents new blockages from forming.
