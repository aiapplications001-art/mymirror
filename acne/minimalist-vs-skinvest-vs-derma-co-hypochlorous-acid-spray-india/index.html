Frequently Asked Questions

Is 200 PPM better than 150 PPM for acne?

Not necessarily. Clinical studies show that 100–150 PPM is sufficient to kill 99.9% of C. acnes within 15–30 seconds. While 200 PPM offers slightly higher potency for stubborn body acne or athlete biofilms, 150 PPM is gentler for twice-daily facial application.

Can I transfer my HOCl spray to a transparent travel bottle?

No! Never decant hypochlorous acid into clear bottles. HOCl degrades rapidly when exposed to ultraviolet light, transforming into simple inactive saline. Always keep it in the manufacturer's opaque, UV-shielded packaging.

Why is Minimalist cheaper than Skinvest?

Minimalist uses in-house high-volume manufacturing and a standard HDPE dispenser, passing scale savings to consumers (₹3.99/ml). Skinvest utilizes a specialized micro-atomizing actuator and higher 200 PPM concentration, commanding a premium price (₹6.50/ml).
