3 Ways to Reapply Without Ruining Your Foundation

1

The "Stipple" Method

Dispense a pea-sized amount of a lightweight gel onto a clean, damp beauty sponge. Gently "stamp" it over your face. This deposits micro-layers of SPF without the friction that causes foundation to slide.

2

The SPF Mist

Close your eyes, tuck your lips, and perform 3 broad passes across the face. Best for quick reapplication during your commute or between meetings.

3

The Blot-and-Set Method

Always use a blotting paper first to remove excess sebum. Only then, apply an SPF-infused powder to mattify the skin and reinforce your coverage.

Clinical FAQ

Q: Does my SPF Foundation count as reapplication?

A: Usually, no. Most foundations are applied too thinly to reach their stated SPF. Think of them as a "bonus layer," not your primary defense.

Q: How do I prevent "pilling" when reapplying?

A: Wait 60 seconds after your morning moisturizer before SPF. For midday top-ups, always blot first—oil is the main cause of product pilling.
