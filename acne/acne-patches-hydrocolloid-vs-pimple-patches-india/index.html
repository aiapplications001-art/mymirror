HOW IT WORKS

The Science Behind the White Blob: What Is Actually Inside?

Most people celebrate when they peel off a pimple patch and see a thick white gunk inside, believing they vacuumed out pure pimple fat. Here is what actually happens:

It Is a Gel Reaction, Not Pure Extracted Oil

Hydrocolloid dressings were originally invented for hospital burn and ulcer care. The inner layer is packed with dry particles of sodium carboxymethylcellulose. When you stick the patch over an open blemish or popped pimple, these dry particles absorb escaping wound fluid, pus, and tiny amounts of sebum through capillary suction.

As the particles drink up moisture, they swell up and turn into a soft, cloudy, white gel blob. That white spot is physical proof that fluid was pulled away from your pore and trapped in the gel so your skin can heal in a moist, sterile cocoon.

The Real Benefit: Because the patch shields your blemish, you cannot pick, scratch, or squeeze it with your fingers. Stopping mechanical picking cuts down post-acne dark marks (PIH) by more than half on Indian skin.
Skin Safety Warning: Never Trap Active Acne Creams Under a Patch Never apply prescription adapalene, tretinoin, or leave-on benzoyl peroxide directly beneath a hydrocolloid sticker. A patch creates an airtight, occlusive seal that multiplies medicine absorption by 3 to 10 times. Trapping strong exfoliating acids or retinoids underneath an airtight sticker frequently causes chemical burns, blistering, and severe hyperpigmentation on South Asian skin tones. Keep the skin underneath completely bare and dry.

STEP-BY-STEP

How to Apply for Maximum Adhesion & Drainage

Follow these 4 simple steps to ensure your patch sticks securely all night without peeling off:

1

Wash & Pat Bone Dry

Cleanse with a mild soap-free wash. Pat completely dry with a clean towel. Patches slip right off damp or oily skin.

2

Leave Skin Bare

Do not apply toner, serum, or moisturizer over the blemish. Skincare oils ruin the adhesive seal instantly.

3

Press Firmly (20s)

Place the patch directly over the pimple. Press firmly with your warm fingertip for 20 seconds to activate the sticky edge.

4

Remove When White

Wear for 6 to 10 hours. Peel off gently once the center turns cloudy white. Cleanse the area with cool water.
