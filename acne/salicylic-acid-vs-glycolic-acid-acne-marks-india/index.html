How It Works

How They Work: Inside the Pore vs On the Surface

Understanding the simple chemistry difference between oil solubility and water solubility.

Salicylic Acid (BHA)

Inside the Pore: Dissolves Oil & Plugs

  • Dives Past Skin Grease: Water-based washes slide right off skin oil. Because salicylic acid loves oil (lipophilic), it sinks straight down into the pore where clogs start.
  • Clears the Breeding Ground: It breaks down the sticky sebum glue holding dead cells together, flushing out the pocket where acne bacteria grow.
  • Calms Redness Fast: Chemically related to aspirin, it soothes swollen, tender bumps overnight so they don't leave deep dark scars.
Glycolic Acid (AHA)

On the Surface: Lifts Away Dark Marks

  • Un-glues Stained Skin Cells: Melanin pigment gets trapped inside stubborn dead cells. Glycolic acid dissolves the microscopic glue connecting them, helping dark marks shed faster.
  • Smallest Molecule Speed: Having the tiniest molecular size among AHAs, it glides across the top stratum corneum layer to polish rough, uneven texture.
  • Stops at the Pore Opening: Because it is water-soluble, it cannot cut through oily pore plugs. It polishes the surface, but won't unclog deep cystic bumps.
Indian Skin Guidelines

The 4 Golden Rules for Indian Skin

Because Indian skin produces protective melanin rapidly, following these rules prevents accidental rebound darkening.

1. Salicylic Acid Loves Oil (The Pore Sweeper)

Oil-soluble BHA flows straight past surface sebum to dissolve the waxy plugs that trap bacteria. It also shares chemical roots with aspirin, reducing swelling and calming red, angry pimples before they leave a scar.

2. Glycolic Acid Fades Surface Melanin (The Polisher)

Water-soluble AHA has tiny molecules that gently loosen the glue holding stained dead skin cells together. As old dark flakes detach, fresh new cells replace them, accelerating the fading of stubborn brown marks.

3. Never Layer High Strengths at the Exact Same Minute

Slathering BHA serum and AHA cream on top of each other creates an acid burn on melanin-rich skin. This irritation triggers melanocytes to produce more pigment, resulting in darker marks than you started with.

4. Daily Sunscreen is Mandatory

Because Glycolic acid sweeps away the top protective dead skin layer, fresh skin cells underneath are easily sun-damaged. Without SPF 50 every morning, outdoor UV rays darken your marks right back up.

Decision Guide

Which Active Ingredient Does Your Skin Need Today?

Pair your current skin state with the right clinical active without trial-and-error irritation.

Active Breakouts

"I Have Active Pimples & Blackheads"

What you see: Red bumps, tender swelling, black dots on nose, or oily midday shine.
The root problem: Excess sebum trapped inside the pore canal, fostering bacterial proliferation.
Clinical target: Oil-soluble intra-follicular cleansing to clear out plugs.
Recommended Formulation: Salicylic Acid 2% (Saslic DS / Sebogel)
Leftover Marks

"Pimples Stopped, But Dark Spots Remain"

What you see: Flat brown, purple, or dark marks on cheeks after old pimples healed.
The root problem: Melanin pigment deposits trapped in stubborn surface skin layers.
Clinical target: Accelerated surface shedding to lift hyperpigmentation without peeling.
Recommended Formulation: Glycolic Acid 6% (Glyco-6 Cream)
Combination Routine

"I Have Both Breakouts AND Dark Marks"

What you see: New pimples forming alongside old brown marks from previous weeks.
The common mistake: Never apply both active acids in the same minute or routine.
Clinical target: Time-split regimen (Salicylic wash in AM; Glycolic cream 2 nights/wk).
Recommended Strategy: Time-Split Alternating Protocol

4-Step Weekly Schedule

The 4-Step Weekly Schedule (Without Peeling)

Step-by-step weekly guidelines to clear bumps and fade marks simultaneously without barrier damage.

Phase 01 • Mon / Wed / Fri

Morning BHA Wash

Lather with Saslic DS (2% Salicylic Acid) on damp skin. Leave the active foam on for 45 to 60 seconds before rinsing to clear trapped oil without drying out your face.

Phase 02 • Tue / Thu

Evening Glyco-6 Dab

After evening cleansing, tap a tiny dot of Glyco-6 Cream directly onto flat dark marks. Start just twice a week. Keep away from active open pimples or freshly popped bumps.

Phase 03 • Daily AM

Mandatory Sun Shield

Apply 2 finger lengths of mineral SPF 50 sunscreen every single morning. This prevents freshly exfoliated skin from absorbing sunlight and producing rebound dark melanin stains.

Phase 04 • Weekends

Barrier Recovery Rest

Zero active acids on Saturday and Sunday. Rehydrate stratum corneum lipids with non-comedogenic ceramide creams (such as Bioderma Atoderm) to maintain a soft, bouncy barrier.
