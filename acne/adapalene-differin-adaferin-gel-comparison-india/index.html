Adapalene vs. Differin vs. Adaferin:
Which One Should You Buy in India?

Galderma distributes both Differin and Adaferin at vastly different price tags across Indian pharmacies. Let us walk through the chemistry differences, real pharmacy prices, and how to start without stripping your skin barrier.

Clinical Summary for Quick Decisions

If your doctor prescribed Differin, you can ask for Adaferin 0.1% at the pharmacy counter. It contains the exact same active molecule manufactured by the same parent firm (Galderma), but costs roughly ₹230 instead of ₹1,400+ for imported boxes.

If dealing with inflamed, tender red bumps filled with white fluid, dermatologists frequently start with Adalene Nanogel because it pairs adapalene with an antibiotic (clindamycin). However, plain adapalene remains the long-term workhorse for comedones and blackheads.

Why Does One Retinoid Have Half a Dozen Brand Names?

Walking into an Indian pharmacy or scrolling through Apollo 24|7, Netmeds, and Tata 1mg reveals an overwhelming wall of retinoid tubes: Differin, Adaferin, Adalene Nanogel, Deriva-MS, and Adiff. While patient packaging looks completely distinct, every single one relies on the exact same core chemical: Adapalene.

Adapalene belongs to the third generation of topical retinoids. Unlike first-generation tretinoin, adapalene selectively targets retinoic acid receptor subtypes beta and gamma in the epidermis. In plain English, that receptor selectivity means it signals skin cells to normalize their shed cycle with substantially less flaking, burning, and light sensitivity than traditional tretinoin.

Because adapalene molecules remain chemically stable when exposed to light and oxygen, you can apply them at bedtime without the extreme fragility associated with older vitamin A derivatives.

The Four Formulation Categories in Indian Pharmacies

Imported Original

Differin 0.1% Gel

Developed originally by Galderma Laboratories in Europe and the US. It features a lightweight carbomer vehicle that spreads evenly without balling up. Because tubes sold in India are often parallel imports, prices on Amazon or specialty importers reach ₹1,200 to ₹1,600 for a single 15g tube.

Domestic Leader

Adaferin 0.1% Gel

Manufactured and marketed domestically by Galderma India. The underlying pharmaceutical formulation matches Differin note-for-note, using the identical micronized adapalene crystal structure. At roughly ₹230 per 15g tube, it represents the most sensible direct alternative.

Antibiotic Hybrid

Adalene Nanogel (Zydus)

A specialized Indian formulation combining 0.1% Adapalene with 1% Clindamycin phosphate. The nano-emulsion base enhances drug delivery into congested sebaceous glands. Dermatologists reserve this combination for active, pustular breakouts rather than maintenance care.

Economy Generics

Adiff & Adaphyllene

Indian generic alternatives produced by Glenmark, Ajanta, and domestic laboratories. These retail between ₹100 and ₹160. While clinically therapeutic, the base vehicle can feel slightly tackier on oily skin compared to Galderma's proprietary carbomer emulsion.

The Dermatologist Verdict on Brand Selection

There is zero pharmacological reason for someone residing in India to purchase imported US Differin over domestic Adaferin. Both formulas originate from Galderma, share the exact same active API specifications, and exhibit identical binding affinity to follicular retinoid receptors.

Target Skin Conditions: What Does Adapalene Treat?

Patients often wonder whether adapalene is strictly an acne spot treatment or a comprehensive surface modifier. Topical retinoids work by altering genetic transcription inside follicular keratinocytes. Because of that biological mechanism, their scope spans several related skin complaints:

Primary Target

Resolving Closed Comedones and Follicular Plugs

When sebum mixes with abnormally shedding keratinocytes, it creates an invisible blockage called a microcomedone. Adapalene speeds up follicular turnover, pushing these micro-plugs outward before they can become full-blown blackheads, whiteheads, or inflamed pimples.

Secondary Benefit

Fading Post-Inflammatory Hyperpigmentation (Dark Spots)

Adapalene does not inhibit tyrosinase directly like hydroquinone or kojic acid. However, by accelerating the shedding of melanin-loaded epidermal cells, it cuts down the time required for flat brown acne marks to fade on Fitzpatrick III to V skin tones.

Collagen Maintenance

Textural Smoothing and Premature Photoaging

While tretinoin remains the gold standard for clinical anti-aging, extended adapalene use encourages mild procollagen synthesis and thickens the living layers of the epidermis, smoothing out rough micro-texture and fine expression lines.

Understanding Combination Formulas

Standalone adapalene addresses pore turnover, but certain acne types demand synergistic ingredients:

Adapalene with Clindamycin (Adalene Nanogel, Deriva-CMS): Adding an antibiotic suppresses Cutibacterium acnes bacteria within inflamed pimples. However, dermatologists advise limiting continuous daily use to roughly 8 to 12 weeks. Prolonged antibiotic monotherapy risks creating resistant bacterial strains across your facial microbiome.

Adapalene with Benzoyl Peroxide (Epiduo): Benzoyl peroxide introduces oxygen into the follicular canal, destroying anaerobic acne bacteria through oxidation without developing bacterial resistance. This dual approach tackles both congested pores and swollen bacterial cysts simultaneously.

The Retinoid Protocol: How to Prevent Peeling and Irritation

Most patients who give up on adapalene do so within the first fourteen days because of stinging, redness, or peeling around the mouth and nostrils. This reaction, known as retinoid dermatitis, happens almost exclusively because of user error rather than an inherent allergy to the medication.

Rule 01

Never Apply to Wet or Damp Skin

Water breaks down the skin's surface barrier and dramatically accelerates the transdermal absorption of retinoids. After cleansing your face with a mild, non-foaming wash, wait a full 15 to 20 minutes until your skin feels bone-dry before touching the medication.

Rule 02

Respect the Single Pea-Sized Meter

Squeeze one pea-sized droplet onto your index finger. Dot that single droplet evenly across your forehead, both cheeks, and chin. Do not rub thick clumps onto individual pimples—adapalene is a preventative field treatment, not a targeted spot cream.

Rule 03

Deploy the Sandwich Technique for Sensitive Skin

If you have a reactive barrier or dry patches, apply a lightweight ceramide moisturizer first. Allow it to absorb for 10 minutes, layer your pea-sized adapalene over top, and finish with a second thin layer of moisturizer to lock in hydration while buffering active strength.

Rule 04

Morning Sunscreen is Non-Negotiable

Adapalene speeds cell renewal, exposing fresh epidermal cells that burn and tan much faster under Indian UV levels. Always apply a broad-spectrum, PA++++ sunscreen every morning, even when working indoors near windows.

Purging vs. Allergic Barrier Damage

Around weeks two through four, deep pre-existing microcomedones surface at an accelerated rate. True retinoid purging occurs strictly in areas where you routinely get breakouts, and the spots resolve faster than normal blemishes. Conversely, if you develop stinging from plain tap water, hot red patches across your cheeks, or breakouts in completely new zones, stop the retinoid for five days and focus entirely on barrier balms.

🇮🇳 डॉक्टर का पर्चा: एडापलीन जेल को हिंदी में समझें

भारतीय क्लीनिकों में अक्सर डॉक्टर एडापलीन या एडलीन नैनो लिखते हैं, लेकिन मरीज इसे लगाने के सही तरीके से अनजान रहते हैं। यहाँ जानिए डॉक्टर के निर्देशों का सरल हिंदी अनुवाद:

एडापलीन जेल (Adaferin / Differin) क्या है?

एडापलीन एक थर्ड-जनरेशन रेटिनॉइड (विटामिन ए का आधुनिक रूप) है। यह त्वचा की पुरानी कोशिकाओं को हटाकर रोमछिद्रों (pores) को साफ रखता है। इससे ब्लैकहेड्स, व्हाइटहेड्स और नए मुँहासों का बनना धीरे-धीरे बंद हो जाता है।

एडलीन नैनो जेल (Adalene Nanogel) कब उपयोगी है?

इसमें एडापलीन के साथ 1% क्लिंडामाइसिन (Clindamycin) एंटीबायोटिक मिला होता है। यह लाल, दर्दनाक और मवाद (pus) से भरे मुँहासों के बैक्टीरिया को खत्म करता है। इसे डॉक्टर की सलाह पर सिर्फ 2 से 3 महीने ही लगाना चाहिए।

लगाने का सही नियम (ज़रूरी सावधानियां)

1. सिर्फ रात में लगाएं: चेहरा धोने के कम से कम 15 मिनट बाद, जब त्वचा पूरी तरह सूख जाए, तभी लगाएं।

2. मटर के दाने के बराबर: पूरे चेहरे के लिए सिर्फ एक मटर के दाने (pea-size) जितनी दवा काफी है। ज्यादा दवा लगाने से मुंहासे जल्दी ठीक नहीं होते, बल्कि त्वचा जल सकती है।

3. सुबह सनस्क्रीन अनिवार्य है: दिन में धूप से त्वचा को बचाने के लिए SPF 30 या 50 सनस्क्रीन लगाना बेहद ज़रूरी है।

Medical Disclaimer: This clinical review is peer-reviewed for educational guidance. Adapalene, clindamycin, and benzoyl peroxide are Schedule H prescription drugs in India. Always consult a board-certified dermatologist before initiating active retinoid therapy.

The Pea-Sized Rule

Using more gel will not clear breakouts any faster. It only exhausts your natural lipid bilayer. Dispense one small pea-sized droplet to cover your entire forehead, cheeks, and chin.
