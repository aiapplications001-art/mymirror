# Pre-Writing Strategy & Page Packet Blueprint
## Topic: PanOxyl 4% Creamy vs 10% Foaming Acne Wash (USA)

### 1. Persona & Search Intent
- **Persona:** US-based consumers struggling with face or body acne, ranging from mild breakouts to severe, cystic flare-ups. They are researching over-the-counter (OTC) benzoyl peroxide (BPO) washes and are confused about which concentration (4% vs 10%) is appropriate for their specific acne type, skin sensitivity, and treatment area (face vs. body).
- **Search Intent:** Commercial Investigation / Informational. The user wants a detailed, objective comparison between the two leading PanOxyl strengths to make a confident purchasing decision. They need clinical clarity on efficacy, side effects (dryness, bleaching), and application techniques (contact therapy).

### 2. Competitor Analysis & Gaps
- **Current Landscape:** Many blogs simply list the product specs without explaining *how* benzoyl peroxide works or the concept of "short contact therapy."
- **Identified Gaps:** 
  - Lack of clear distinction between face (4%) vs. body (10%) protocols.
  - Insufficient explanation of US FDA OTC regulations regarding BPO.
  - Missing practical advice on preventing towel/clothing bleaching.
  - Need for dermatological insight into combining these washes with other actives (retinoids, salicylic acid).

### 3. Clinical Section Blueprint
- **Introduction:** Hook the reader, introduce benzoyl peroxide as the gold standard OTC acne ingredient, and state the core dilemma (4% vs 10%).
- **Understanding Benzoyl Peroxide:** Mechanism of action (keratolytic, bactericidal against *C. acnes*).
- **PanOxyl 4% Creamy Wash Deep Dive:**
  - Formula analysis (emollients, creamy texture).
  - Ideal candidates (sensitive skin, facial acne, beginners).
  - Pros & Cons.
- **PanOxyl 10% Foaming Wash Deep Dive:**
  - Formula analysis (maximum OTC strength, foaming agents).
  - Ideal candidates (body acne, chest/back breakouts, resistant/severe acne).
  - Pros & Cons.
- **The Head-to-Head Comparison:**
  - Efficacy vs. Irritation (The law of diminishing returns with BPO concentrations).
  - Texture and User Experience.
- **Dermatological Protocols (How to Use):**
  - Short Contact Therapy explained.
  - The "bleach warning" (towels, shirts, pillowcases).
- **Compatibility with Other Skincare:** What to avoid (layering with retinoids/AHAs/BHAs) to prevent barrier damage.
- **Conclusion:** Final verdict based on skin type and acne severity.
- **FAQ Section:** Address common queries (Can I use it every day? Purging? Pregnancy safety?).
