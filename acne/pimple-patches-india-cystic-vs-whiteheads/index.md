## Quick Answer

Pimple patches work differently based on their technology: **Hydrocolloid patches** (the most common type) are designed to "vacuum" out fluids from whiteheads that have already come to a head. **Micro-dart patches** (medicated with Salicylic Acid or Beta-Glucan) use tiny, self-dissolving needles to penetrate the skin and treat deep, painful **cystic acne** before it surfaces. For the best results in India, always apply patches to "clinical-dry" skin after double cleansing to prevent sebum from breaking the seal.

## Main Content: Hydrocolloid vs. Micro-dart

Choosing the right brand in India (Urban Yog, Gubb, or Dr. Belmeur) depends entirely on the *stage* of your pimple.

### 1. Hydrocolloid (The "Gunk" Drawer)
These are your budget-friendly staples. They work via osmosis, pulling pus and oil out of a "ripe" whitehead.
- **Best For:** Whiteheads and "popped" pimples.
- **Top Picks:** **Gubb Acne Patches** or **Urban Yog Hydrocolloid Patches**.

### 2. Micro-dart (The "Blind Pimple" Destroyer)
If you feel a painful bump under the skin that hasn't surfaced yet, a standard sticker won't help. Micro-dart patches have microscopic points that deliver Salicylic Acid directly into the pore.
- **Best For:** Blind pimples and early-stage cystic acne.
- **Top Picks:** **Dr. Belmeur Spot Patch** or **Acnes Spot Patch**.

### 3. Medicated Thin Patches
These are infused with Tea Tree oil or Cica, designed for daytime use under makeup or sunscreen.

## FAQ

**Why did my patch fall off in 2 hours?**
Indian humidity and oily skin are "patch-killers." Ensure you double cleanse and wait 5 minutes for your skin to be completely dry before applying. Avoid putting moisturizer *under* the patch.

**Can I wear a patch under my helmet?**
Yes! In fact, we recommend it. A patch prevents the "friction acne" (Acne Mechanica) caused by helmet straps rubbing against an active breakout.

**Can I use a patch on a blackhead?**
Generally, no. Blackheads are "open" comedones that need chemical exfoliation (like **Sebogel**). Patches are for "closed" inflammatory breakouts.
