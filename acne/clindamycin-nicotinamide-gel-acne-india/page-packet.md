# Pre-Writing Strategy & Page Packet

## 1. Persona & Search Intent
- **Target Persona:** Indian consumers (Fitzpatrick IV–VI) suffering from active pustular acne seeking pharmacy-grade topical gels (Clinka N, Clindac A, Clinmiskin).
- **Search Intent:** Informational & Commercial Investigation. Users want to know how Clindamycin 1% + Nicotinamide 4% clears inflammatory pimples, how to prevent antibiotic resistance, and CDSCO prescription guidelines.

## 2. Competitor Content Gaps
- Most competitor pages ignore bacterial resistance mechanisms after 4-6 weeks of antibiotic monotherapy.
- Lacks specific guidance for monsoon humidity and Fitzpatrick IV-VI post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH).

- **Section 2:** Preventing Antibiotic Resistance on Indian Skin.
