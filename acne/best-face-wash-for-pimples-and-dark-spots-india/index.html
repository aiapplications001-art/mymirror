The Dual Challenge: Clearing and Fading

Most oily-skinned Indian consumers face a double struggle: preventing **active breakouts** while trying to fade **post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH)** left behind by older pimples. A face wash formulated with active ingredients (like Salicylic Acid or Glycolic Acid) addresses both problems. It clears the pores to prevent new blockages while chemically exfoliating the surface to shed pigmented cells and fade dark marks faster.

The 60-Second Wash Rule

Most consumers splash active face washes on and rinse them off immediately. Active ingredients like Salicylic Acid require contact time to penetrate sebum plugs:

🧼 How to Wash with Active Cleansers

  1. Dispense a nickel-sized amount of wash and lather between wet palms.
  2. Massage onto damp face using gentle circular motions, focusing on congested areas (nose, forehead, chin).
  3. Let it sit for 60 seconds on the skin before rinsing. This allows the BPO or BHA to interact with the follicles.
  4. Rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water. Pat dry — never rub.

Expert FAQ: Cleansers for Pimples & Spots
How does a face wash help fade dark spots?+
Face washes containing chemical exfoliants like Glycolic Acid (AHA) or Salicylic Acid (BHA) gently dissolve the outer layer of dead skin cells. This accelerates cell turnover, shedding melanin-pigmented cells faster to fade dark marks (PIH) over 4–8 weeks.
Should I use a salicylic acid face wash twice a day?+
If you have highly oily skin, twice-daily use of a gentle salicylic acid wash is tolerable. However, if you are also using nightly retinoids (like tretinoin), twice-daily active washing will disrupt your skin barrier. We recommend using active washes in the morning and a simple, bland hydrating wash at night.
Which is better for active pustules: Acnelak or Saslic DS?+
For active, red pustules, Acnelak is highly effective because it contains zinc active components that combat C. acnes bacteria. For comedones (blackheads/whiteheads) and general oiliness, Saslic DS (2% Salicylic Acid) is the superior chemical pore unclogger.
