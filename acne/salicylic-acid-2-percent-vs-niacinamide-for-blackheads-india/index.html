2. Dual Biological Chemistry: Lipophilic Exfoliation & Sebum Regulation

Blackheads and enlarged open pores occur due to a combination of excess sebum production and abnormal keratinocyte desquamation inside pilosebaceous follicles:

Salicylic Acid 2% Mechanics
Salicylic Acid is a lipid-soluble beta hydroxy acid (BHA). Its aromatic ring structure allows it to dissolve directly into sebum lipids, penetrating deep into clogged pores. Once inside, BHA breaks down intracellular desmosomes (protein glues holding dead skin cells together), clearing out blackheads and preventing micro-comedones (read our Salicylic Acid Barrier Guide).

Niacinamide (Vitamin B3) Mechanics
Niacinamide acts as a precursor to NAD(P)H coenzymes, reducing sebaceous gland lipid production. It prevents pore wall distension by improving skin elasticity and stimulating natural ceramide synthesis, preventing BHA dryness.
