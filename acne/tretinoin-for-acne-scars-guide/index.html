How Tretinoin Works for Acne Scars

Tretinoin (all-trans retinoic acid) is the gold standard topical retinoid for skin resurfacing. According to NIH clinical studies, Tretinoin binds directly to retinoic acid receptors in dermal cells, stimulating the synthesis of new collagen type I and III. This process gradually fills in superficial pitted scars and accelerates stratum corneum renewal to fade post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH).

However, Tretinoin cannot completely resolve deep, fibrotic icepick or anchored boxcar scars on its own, as these require structural remodeling beneath the dermis. Combining Tretinoin with clinical treatments like subcision or microneedling provides the best outcomes.

2
The Sandwich Method: Minimizing Retinoid Irritation

Retinoid-induced dermatitis (redness, flaking, and burning) is a common side effect, especially for beginners on Indian skin. Dermatologists recommend the **Sandwich Method** to buffer the active ingredient and limit trans-epidermal water loss (TEWL):

  1. Apply a thin layer of a simple, fragrance-free ceramide moisturizer to clean, dry skin.
  2. Wait 15–20 minutes to allow the moisture barrier to settle.
  3. Apply a pea-sized amount of Tretinoin (0.025% is recommended for beginners) evenly across the face.
  4. Wait another 10 minutes, and seal the active with a second layer of moisturizer.

The 12-Week Tretinoin Ease-In Calendar

Rushing Tretinoin application is the primary cause of retinoid burns. Follow this clinical scheduling matrix to safely build tolerance over 12 weeks:

Phase Frequency Application Protocol Expected Purpose
Weeks 1 - 2 Twice a week (every 4th night) Buffered Sandwich Method with Tretinoin 0.025%. Acclimatizes cell receptors, minimizes early peeling and redness.
Weeks 3 - 6 Once every 3 nights Sandwich method. Ensure skin is 100% dry after cleansing. Manages the initial purging phase; pushes trapped microcomedones to the surface.
Weeks 7 - 12 Alternate nights (3 times a week) Apply Tretinoin directly to dry skin, follow with barrier repair cream. Cellular stabilization begins; early texture smoothing and fading of PIH/PIE.
4
Purging Management & Safety Rules

Skin purging refers to the rapid expulsion of deep-seated clogs as cellular turnover accelerates. It typically peaks around week 3 to 4 and subsides by week 8. Avoid picking or squeezing purging lesions, as the skin barrier is fragile and picking during Tretinoin therapy will cause permanent structural pitting.

Always use a broad-spectrum SPF 50 sunscreen during the day, as Tretinoin makes the skin highly photosensitive, increasing the risk of sunburn and PIH.

💡 Clinical Tip

Always start Tretinoin with the lowest strength (0.025% gel or cream). Never apply on damp skin, as water increases absorption depth, leading to severe chemical irritation.

Key Retinoid Rules

  • Use pea-sized amount only
  • Never skip SPF 50 daily
  • Apply only at night
  • Do not mix with BHA/AHA
