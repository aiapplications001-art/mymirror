⚡ Quick Clinical Summary (Direct Answer)

What is Perobar? Perobar is a syndet cleansing bar by Ajanta Pharma containing micronized Benzoyl Peroxide (2.5% or 5%). It destroys acne-causing bacteria through oxidation rather than antibiotics, making it impossible for bacteria to build resistance.

Strength Pick

Use Perobar 2.5% for facial acne or sensitive skin. Use Perobar 5% for back acne (bacne), chest pimples, and buttocks bumps.

Contact Time

Build a lather in hands, apply to wet skin, and leave for 2 to 3 minutes before washing off completely (Short Contact Therapy).

Shower Frequency

Start 2 to 3 times weekly for the first 2 weeks. Increase to once daily if your skin tolerates it without tightness.

Bleach Warning

Benzoyl peroxide bleaches colored fabrics. Always dry off with white towels and wear light clothing after showering.

The "Oxygen Blast" Analogy: Why Bacteria Cannot Resist Perobar

To understand why dermatologists in India prescribe Perobar for stubborn acne, you only need to understand one simple rule about how acne bacteria breathe:

🔬 The Everyday Analogy

Acne Bacteria Hate Fresh Air

The Bacteria Hides in a Dark Cave: The bacteria responsible for red, throbbing pimples is called Cutibacterium acnes. It is an anaerobic organism, which means it can only survive in oxygen-deprived places. When excess oil and dead skin glue your pores shut, it creates a cozy, airtight cave where bacteria multiply rapidly.

Perobar is an Instant Oxygen Blast: When Benzoyl Peroxide touches your wet skin, it breaks down into benzoic acid and releases microscopic oxygen radicals directly into the pore. It does not try to poison the bacteria like an antibiotic tablet. Instead, it blasts pure oxygen into the cave, wiping out 99% of bacteria within 48 hours.

Why Resistance Never Happens: If you use antibiotic creams (like Clindamycin) for several months, bacteria learn to mutate and resist the drug. But bacteria can never become immune to oxygen, just as humans can never become immune to breathing. You can use Perobar for years and it will stay just as effective as day one.

Lather Technique: Build a rich foam in your hands first. Never drag the dry bar roughly over irritated skin.
🛡️
Why Syndet Matters Over Regular Soap
Traditional bath soaps have an alkaline pH of 9 to 10, which damages your natural acid mantle and triggers more oil. Perobar is a synthetic detergent (syndet) bar with a skin-friendly pH of 5.5, preserving barrier lipids.
🚿
Short Contact Therapy Advantage
Leaving benzoyl peroxide gels on overnight can cause intense peeling and redness on brown skin. Washing with Perobar for 2 minutes delivers full antibacterial action with almost zero peeling.

The 5-Step Shower Wash Routine (Short Contact Therapy)

Do not treat Perobar like an ordinary bathing bar that you wash off instantly. To kill bacteria effectively without peeling, use the **Short Contact Therapy** method:

1

Wash Body Normally First

Take your regular shower first. Shampoo and condition your hair, and wash away hair product residues that run down your back, as conditioner residue is a common trigger for back acne.

Step 1: Prep Skin
2

Build Foam in Hands (Never Rub Bar on Skin)

Wet your hands and rub the Perobar between your palms until a dense, creamy white lather appears. Applying the smooth lather prevents physical friction over inflamed pimples.

Step 2: Rich Foam
3

Apply to Acne Zones and Wait 2 to 3 Minutes

Spread the lather across your back, chest, or face. Turn off the shower stream or step away from the water. Let the lather sit on your skin for 2 to 3 minutes so the oxygen penetrates deep into follicles.

Step 3: Contact Window
4

Rinse Thoroughly with Lukewarm Water

Rinse your skin with plenty of water until all slippery soap residue is gone. Pay special attention to the neck folds, hairline, and shoulder blades.

Step 4: Complete Rinse
5

Pat Dry with a White Towel and Hydrate

Pat your skin dry with a clean white cotton towel. Apply a lightweight, oil-free moisturiser on damp skin to replenish hydration.

Step 5: White Towel Care

The Towel & Pillow Bleach Trap: How to Save Your Fabrics

Benzoyl Peroxide does not bleach human skin or hair in normal contact washes, but it **will bleach colored fabrics permanently**. If you are not careful, your dark towels, bedsheets, and T-shirts will turn blotchy pink or orange.

⚠️ Fabric Bleaching Rules:

  • Dedicate White Towels: Use only white bath towels and white hand towels. Benzoyl peroxide cannot bleach what is already white.
  • Rinse Hands Twice: After washing with Perobar, rinse your hands thoroughly before touching door handles, curtains, or colored clothing.
  • Dry Off Completely: Never pull on a dark gym t-shirt or colored kurta while your back or chest is still damp from the shower.
