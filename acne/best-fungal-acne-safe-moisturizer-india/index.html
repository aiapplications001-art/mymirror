The Science: What Feeds Fungal Acne?

Fungal acne (*Malassezia folliculitis*) is not true acne vulgaris. It is an infection of the hair follicle caused by an overgrowth of **Malassezia yeast**, which thrives in hot, humid Indian climates. This yeast feeds on lipids (fatty acids) with carbon chain lengths between **11 and 24**. Standard moisturisers containing plant oils, fatty acids (stearic/palmitic acid), and esters (glyceryl stearate, polysorbates) act as direct food for the yeast, causing forehead bumps to multiply rapidly.

✅ The Yeast-Free Standard: A fungal acne-safe moisturiser must contain **zero yeast food**. It relies on water-binding humectants (hyaluronic acid, glycerin, aloe) and synthetic silicone occlusives (dimethicone) that the yeast cannot metabolize.

Ingredients to Avoid (Yeast Food)

If you suspect fungal acne, scan your skincare labels and immediately discard products containing these Malassezia-feeding elements:

  • Organic Oils: Coconut oil, olive oil, rosehip oil, argan oil, almond oil (only Squalane, Mineral Oil, and Caprylic/Capric Triglycerides are safe).
  • Fatty Acids: Stearic Acid, Palmitic Acid, Lauric Acid, Myristic Acid, Oleic Acid.
  • Esters (ending in -ate): Isopropyl Palmitate, Glyceryl Stearate, Polysorbate-20/60/80, PEG-8 Stearate, Ethylhexyl Palmitate.

Expert FAQ: Fungal Acne Safe Moisturisers
What ingredients feed fungal acne?+
Malassezia yeast feeds on fatty acids with carbon chain lengths between 11 and 24. This includes nearly all organic oils (like rosehip, argan, or olive oil), fatty acids (like stearic acid, palmitic acid, lauric acid), and esters (ingredients ending in -ate, such as isopropyl palmitate, glyceryl stearate, or polysorbates).
Is Aloe Vera gel fungal acne safe?+
Pure Aloe Vera gel is 100% fungal acne safe. However, many commercial Aloe Vera products contain added emulsifiers, oils, or polysorbates to improve texture and shelf-life, which can feed the yeast. Always check the full ingredient list.
How do I treat fungal acne bumps on my forehead?+
To clear fungal acne: (1) Stop all moisturisers and cleansers that contain yeast food. (2) Use **Ketoconazole 2% shampoo** (like Nizoral or Scalpe Plus) as a face wash. Apply to forehead, leave for 5 minutes, and rinse off 2–3 times a week. (3) Hydrate exclusively with a 100% fungal-acne-safe gel like Sebamed.

⚡ Safe Lipids

  • Squalane oil (100% safe)
  • Mineral Oil / Petrolatum
  • Caprylic/Capric Triglycerides

❌ Avoid These

  • All organic seed/fruit oils
  • Stearic/Palmitic fatty acids
  • Polysorbates & Esters (-ate)
