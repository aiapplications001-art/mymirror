You spend three weeks meticulously clearing your skin, only for the "Period Purge" to wipe out all your progress in 48 hours. If this feels familiar, you are treating your skin with a static routine for a dynamic biological cycle.

In 2026, the clinical shift is toward Cycle-Syncing: adjusting your actives based on the Estrogen-Progesterone balance of your 28-day clock. In this guide, we provide the week-by-week defense strategy for the Indian urban climate.

Week 1: Menstrual Phase

The "Repair & Recover" Protocol

The Biological Shift: Estrogen and Progesterone are at their lowest. Your skin barrier is most fragile, leading to increased sensitivity and transepidermal water loss (TEWL).

The Protocol: Pause high-strength retinoids. Focus on **Centella Asiatica** and **Hyaluronic Acid**. In the Indian humidity, use a gel-based ceramide like Excela or Emolene.

✨
Week 2: Follicular Phase

The "Glow & Boost" Window

The Biological Shift: Estrogen rises, increasing collagen production and skin thickness. Your skin is at its most resilient and luminous.

The Protocol: This is the time for **High-Potency Actives**. Use your Vitamin C (10-15%) and stronger AHAs. Skin absorption is optimal; maximize this window for brightening and texture repair.

🛡️
Week 4: Luteal Phase

The "Predictive Defense" Protocol

The Biological Shift: Progesterone peaks, stimulating sebum glands. This "thickens" the oil in your pores, creating the perfect environment for period pimples.

The Protocol: **Predictive BHA.** Don't wait for the pimple. Start a 2% Salicylic Acid wash exactly 7 days before your period to "flush" the follicle. Pair with **Azelaic Acid (Aziderm)** to keep inflammation low.

Clinical FAQ

Q: Should I stop my Retinol during my period?

A: If your skin feels tight or itchy during Week 1, YES. The Estrogen drop makes your barrier "leaky," which increases Retinol-induced irritation. Skip it for 3-4 days and focus on Ceramides.

Q: When exactly should I start the 'Defense' protocol?

A: For most women, the oil spike happens around Day 21 of a 28-day cycle. Starting your BHA on Day 18-20 ensures the pore is clear *before* the oil becomes trapped.
