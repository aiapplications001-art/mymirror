Application Timing

Always apply Aziderm to completely dry skin 15 minutes post-cleansing. Applying to damp skin dramatically increases ionic percutaneous flux, triggering transient prickling and erythematous flushing.

Pregnancy Safety

Azelaic acid is US FDA Category B with minimal systemic absorption (<4%), making it the safest clinical depigmenting agent during pregnancy and lactation.
