The Clinical Mechanism: BHA vs. BPO

To choose the right wash, you must understand how these two molecules interact with the 13-layer-thick skin on your back.

Salicylic Acid (BHA)

The Drain Opener

Salicylic Acid is lipophilic—it loves oil. It can dive through the sebum "plug" and dissolve the glue holding dead skin cells together.

  • Dissolves blackheads & whiteheads
  • Ideal for "Strawberry legs" (KP)
  • Best for non-inflamed bumps
Benzoyl Peroxide (BPO)

The Oxygen Bomb

BPO works via oxidation. It releases oxygen into the pore, killing anaerobic C. acnes bacteria that cannot survive in oxygen-rich environments.

  • Bactericidal (Kills bacteria)
  • Superior anti-inflammatory action
  • Zero-resistance factor (No bacterial immunity)

Diagnosis: Clogged Pores vs. Inflamed Cysts

In 2026, the MyMirror clinical protocol suggests a "Visual Check" to determine your ingredient:

Type A: The Texture (Comedones)

Small, skin-colored bumps that feel rough but don't hurt. These are clogged pores.

Treatment: Salicylic Acid.

Type B: The Pain (Inflammation)

Red, angry bumps that hurt when your shirt rubs against them. These are infected lesions.

Treatment: Benzoyl Peroxide.

The "White Towel Rule": A Lifestyle Necessity

If you choose Benzoyl Peroxide, you must prepare for its most famous side effect: Fabric Bleaching. BPO will turn your dark blue towels, cotton saris, and linens into a patchy mess of orange and white.

1. The White Towel Only Policy: Invest in dedicated white cotton towels. Residue on your hands or back is enough to ruin colored fabrics.
2. The Shared Bathroom Warning: Rinse the shower area thoroughly. BPO residue on a bath mat can ruin a family member's clothes.
3. The Nightwear Sacrifice: Wear only white or old cotton t-shirts to sleep if you use BPO in your evening shower.

Short Contact Therapy (SCT): The 3-Minute Rule

The biggest mistake users make is rinsing their medicated wash off too quickly. The thick skin on your back needs time for the actives to penetrate the biofilm.

Step 1

Wet the skin and apply the wash. Lather and massage for 60 seconds.

Step 2: The Wait

Leave the lather on your skin for 3 to 5 minutes while you wash your hair or shave.

Step 3

Rinse thoroughly. This maximizes efficacy while minimizing dryness.

Clinical FAQ

1. Can I use both? +

Yes, but not at the same time. We recommend the "Alternating Day" strategy: SA on Mon/Wed/Fri for texture, and BPO on Tue/Thu/Sat for bacteria.

2. Why is my skin itching after BPO? +

BPO can be extremely drying, especially in Hard Water areas. Always follow up with a fragrance-free, non-comedogenic moisturizer.

3. Does medicated body wash cause purging? +

Yes. As Salicylic Acid clears out deep-seated gunk, you may see a temporary increase in whiteheads for 1-2 weeks. Clear skin usually begins by Week 4.
