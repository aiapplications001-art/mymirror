The Fundamental Difference: Internal vs External

Many Indian women and men struggle with persistent acne for years because they treat **hormonal acne** as if it were simple **bacterial surface acne**. Hormonal acne is driven internally by androgen hormone surges and insulin resistance (PCOS), while bacterial acne is caused by surface pore blockages and *Cutibacterium acnes* bacterial colonization.

PCOS Connection 🩸

PCOS & Insulin Resistance in India

PCOS affects over 20% of Indian women. Elevated insulin levels stimulate the ovaries to produce excess androgen male hormones (testosterone & DHT), causing sebaceous glands in the lower face to swell and produce sticky sebum.

Benzoyl peroxide (Persol / Galderma Benzac AC) is the gold standard topical for bacterial acne because it releases oxygen to destroy C. acnes without causing bacterial resistance like topical antibiotics.

5
Why Topical Creams Fail on Hormonal Cysts
🛑 The Anatomical Limitation: Deep hormonal cysts form deep inside the dermis. Spot-applying Benzoyl peroxide or Salicylic acid on top of a blind cyst will dry out the surface skin, causing flaking and hyperpigmentation, but will NOT shrink the deep hormonal lesion under the skin. Systemic hormonal regulation (under medical supervision) is necessary.

Expert FAQ: Hormonal vs Bacterial Acne
How do I tell if my acne is hormonal or bacterial?+
Hormonal acne typically presents as deep, painful, blind cysts localized along the jawline, chin, and lower face that flare cyclically before menstruation or during stress. Bacterial acne presents as surface whiteheads, blackheads, and superficial red pustules scattered across the forehead, nose, and cheeks.
Why don't topical acne creams work on hormonal jawline acne?+
Hormonal acne originates deep within the dermis due to androgen-stimulated sebum overproduction and systemic hormone fluctuations (PCOS, insulin resistance). Surface topical creams cannot penetrate deep enough to address internal endocrine triggers, requiring systemic treatments (Spironolactone, oral contraceptives, or Inositol).
What supplement helps with PCOS hormonal acne in India?+
Myo-Inositol + D-Chiro Inositol (in a 40:1 ratio) is clinically proven to improve insulin sensitivity, reduce circulating free testosterone levels, and regulate menstrual cycles to treat PCOS-related hormonal acne.
