When to See a Dermatologist for Face Acne

When to See a Dermatologist for Face Acne for Indian urban adults (16-40), with clear guidance, references, and one primary action path.

Quick Answer

You should see a dermatologist if your acne meets any of these three criteria: 1. Persistent Resistance: No improvement after 6 weeks of OTC care. 2. Physical Pain: Deep, "blind" bumps (cysts) that don't come to a head. 3. Permanent Changes: Acne that leaves behind "pits" (atrophic scars) or significant dark marks (PIH). In India, a standard consultation costs between ₹500 and ₹1,500, and early intervention can prevent expensive laser treatments later.

Context: Why DIY Fails for Severe Acne

The biggest mistake we see in the Indian market is users attempting "Home Extractions" with needles or fingers.

  • Deep Infection: Cystic acne is too deep for surface pressure. Squeezing pushes the bacteria into the bloodstream.
  • The Scarring Loop: In Indian skin tones, trauma to the dermis leads to immediate hyperpigmentation that can last for years.
  • Steroid Abuse: Many "over-the-counter" creams in local pharmacies contain hidden steroids (like Panderm or Lobate) which provide instant relief but cause "Steroid-Induced Acne" that is incredibly hard to treat.

    • Main Content: What the Doctor Can Do

    A dermatologist has access to medical-grade tools and oral medications that are not available in a Nykaa or Amazon cart.

    1. Medical Extractions & Clean-ups

    Performed in a sterile environment, clinical extraction clears "comedones" (blackheads/whiteheads) without damaging the surrounding tissue. This is often included in a "Medical Facial."

    2. Chemical Peels (Salicylic/Glycolic)

    High-concentration acids (20%+) are applied to the skin to reset the texture and "kill" active acne.

  • Cost in India: ₹2,000 – ₹5,000 per session.

    • 3. Oral Medications (Isotretinoin)

    For severe, scarring acne, a doctor may prescribe Isotretinoin. This is the closest thing to a "cure" for acne as it permanently shrinks sebaceous glands. Warning: This must only be taken under strict medical supervision.

    4. Scar Revision (MNRF & CO2)

    If you already have pitted scars, topical creams will not work. You need clinical collagen stimulation.

  • MNRF (Microneedling RF): ₹5,000 – ₹12,000 per session.

    FAQ

    Does health insurance cover acne treatment in India?

    Most basic health insurance plans do not cover acne as it is considered "cosmetic." However, if the acne is a symptom of a medical condition (like PCOS), some diagnostic costs may be covered.

    How much does a dermatologist visit cost in Mumbai/Bangalore?

    In metro cities, expect to pay ₹1,000 – ₹2,500 for a senior specialist. Online consultations are often cheaper, starting at ₹500.

    Will a dermatologist just give me expensive brands?

    A good dermatologist will usually prescribe "Pharmacopeia" brands (like Benzac or Adaferin) which are significantly cheaper and more stable than luxury skincare brands.
