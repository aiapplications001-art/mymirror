# Pre-Writing Strategy & Page Packet

## 1. Persona & Search Intent
- **Target Persona:** Indian skincare users seeking emergency 24-48 hour spot fixes for painful pimples that do not leave dark brown PIH marks behind.
- **Search Intent:** Commercial Investigation. Users want product recommendations (Benzac AC, Sebogel, Zitcare) and usage protocols.

## 2. Competitor Content Gaps
- Competitors fail to distinguish between papule spot treatments (BPO) vs post-acne dark spot treatments (Azelaic Acid, Alpha Arbutin).
- Fails to address Fitzpatrick IV-VI melanin over-reactivity.

## 3. Clinical Blueprint
- **Section 1:** Emergency Spot Treatment Matrix (BPO 2.5% vs BHA 2% + 6% Niacinamide).
- **Section 2:** Preventing Post-Inflammatory Hyperpigmentation (PIH).
- **Section 3:** Pimple Patch Hydrocolloid Application Protocol.
- **Section 4:** 8 Detailed Clinical FAQs.
