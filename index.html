MyMirror maps visible skin signals across six facial zones and turns them into a personalised Care Map. Results consider skin age, oil balance, breakouts, evenness, sensitivity, smoothness, and fine lines.

You look into your camera while MyMirror completes a real-time analysis. In about 60 seconds, you receive a region-by-region view and a personalised routine starting point.

No. MyMirror helps you understand visible skin patterns and routine priorities; it does not diagnose conditions or replace a qualified dermatologist.

Yes. You choose whether to share selected wins, send a full Care Map to a dermatologist, or keep everything private.
