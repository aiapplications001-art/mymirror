Start with zones

Your face does not have to be cared for as one surface.

Combination skin commonly means more shine through the forehead, nose or chin, while cheeks can feel normal or dry. Notice those areas separately before you add a product or change a routine.

Cleanse gently

Choose a baseline that leaves your whole face comfortable rather than squeaky clean.

Keep moisture flexible

Comfort matters more than a label: a texture can feel different by zone and season.

Protect every day

Use sun protection that feels workable enough for your routine to stay consistent.

A seasonal decision

Small adjustments beat a full routine reset.

If your T-zone feels shinier

Keep your baseline steady and avoid responding with harsh cleansing across your entire face.

If your cheeks feel less comfortable

Give that area extra attention without assuming every area needs the same thing.

When a guide is not enough

Persistent, painful, rapidly changing or worrying concerns deserve a dermatologist—not a cosmetic self-check or product experiment.

A calmer next step

Notice the zones. Choose the smallest useful change.
