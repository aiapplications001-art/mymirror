HOW DEPTH WORKS

Thin Face Skin vs Thick Body Skin: Why Depth Matters

Understanding why one bottle works wonders on your neck but burns your cheeks comes down to simple skin thickness.

● Thin Facial Skin (6% Glycolic Acid)

The outer dead skin barrier on your face is only 10 to 15 microscopic cell layers deep. Glyco-6 safely dissolves the surface glue between these loose cells. This allows fresh, evenly pigmented skin cells to show through without triggering redness or inflammatory peeling.

● Thick Neck & Body Skin (12% Glycolic Acid)

On body folds, elbows, and areas affected by insulin resistance, dead keratin cells stack up into a dense, compacted layer up to 40 cell layers thick. The lower pH and higher potency of Glyco-12 unglue these stubborn bonds, smoothing out the rough velvety texture.

STEP-BY-STEP SYSTEM

The 4-Step 8-Week Action Protocol

Follow these clinical rules to see noticeable smoothing without peeling, redness, or barrier damage.

1

Zero Friction Rule

Throw away your loofah. On Indian skin, scrubbing triggers melanocytes to produce more pigment as defense. Cleanse your neck and body using only your bare hands and a gentle foaming cleanser.

2

100% Dry Skin Rule

Never apply glycolic cream on damp skin. Water pulls acids in too quickly, leading to burning. Pat dry and wait 10 full minutes after your shower before touching the cream.

3

Short-Contact Start

For the first two weeks, apply a pea-sized amount, let it sit for 20 to 30 minutes, then rinse with cool water. Once your skin builds tolerance, you can leave it on overnight twice a week.

4

Ceramide & SPF Shield

Always follow evening acid treatments with a fragrance-free ceramide cream. Every morning, apply broad-spectrum SPF 50 sunscreen on your neck and face to protect fresh skin from sun darkening.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, medically verified answers to common questions about Glyco-6, Glyco-12, and body pigmentation.

Can I use Glyco-12 directly on my face for dark spots?
No. Facial skin has a much thinner stratum corneum than the rest of the body. Starting with 12% Glycolic Acid on your face often causes stinging, chemical peeling, barrier breakdown, and rebound dark marks. Always stick to 6% Glycolic Acid (Glyco-6) for facial spots, and begin with short-contact therapy.
Why did my dark neck turn blacker after using a loofah or scrubber?
Aggressive rubbing with loofahs, brushes, or rough towels causes chronic micro-injury to the skin. Indian skin contains reactive melanocytes that respond to physical friction by depositing extra melanin to shield the skin. This turns the area darker and thicker, a condition called Frictional Melanosis. Switching to chemical exfoliation without rubbing stops this cycle.
How does Glycolic Acid treat Acanthosis Nigricans?
Acanthosis Nigricans causes dead skin cells to multiply and pack tightly together, creating a dark, velvety texture. Glycolic Acid dissolves the microscopic glue holding these compacted dead cells together, helping them shed so that softer, lighter skin emerges. If insulin resistance is the underlying trigger, managing diet and blood sugar alongside cream use yields lasting results.
Can I leave Glyco-6 or Glyco-12 on overnight right away?
Beginners should start with Short-Contact Therapy. Apply a pea-sized amount to clean, dry skin, let it sit for 20 to 30 minutes, then rinse it off with cool water. Repeat this twice a week for two weeks. If your skin stays calm without itching or burning, you can graduate to leaving it on overnight.
Why must I wait until my skin is completely dry before applying glycolic cream?
Water acts as a penetration enhancer for Alpha Hydroxy Acids. If you apply Glyco-6 or Glyco-12 on damp or sweaty skin, the acid sinks in too rapidly and unevenly, triggering intense burning and irritation. Always pat your skin dry and wait 10 full minutes after bathing before applying.
Does Glycolic Acid help with dark underarms and strawberry legs?
Yes. For dark underarms caused by razor friction or deodorant buildup, Glyco-6 or diluted Glyco-12 helps dissolve dead pigmented cells. For strawberry legs (rough follicular bumps), Glyco-12 unplugs keratin buildup around hair follicles, smoothing rough texture on shins and thighs.
What is the difference between Glyco-6 and Demelan cream?
Glyco-6 contains only 6% Glycolic Acid to boost cell turnover. Demelan is a triple-action formulation combining 10% Glycolic Acid with two pigment-blocking actives: 5% Alpha Arbutin and 2% Kojic Acid Dipalmitate. Demelan works well for stubborn melasma or persistent body patches under medical supervision.
Do I need sunscreen if I only apply Glycolic Acid at night?
Yes, daytime sunscreen is mandatory. Glycolic acid removes the top protective layer of dead cells, leaving newly exposed skin more sensitive to ultraviolet rays for up to a week. Skipping morning SPF can cause immediate sun tanning and undo your progress.
